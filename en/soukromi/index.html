Privacy and traffic measurement

I share my travels and projects on the Cestovatel69.cz website. This page describes how I use Google Analytics here and how you can control the measurement.

Why and when do I measure traffic?

Google Analytics 4 helps me find out which pages people visit and which videos they click on. The measurement starts only after the selection "Enable measurement". If you reject the measurement or do not select any option, the Google analytics tag will not be loaded. The opt-out does not limit the content of the website.

The legal basis for this optional analytical processing is your consent. You can revoke it at any time in the privacy settings; this does not affect the processing carried out before the appeal.

What information is processed

After consent, the displayed page, its name, the referring website without address parameters and basic technical data processed by the Analytics service, for example the type of device or browser and the approximate area of the visit, are sent. Custom events also record clicks to play a video and go to YouTube. Clicking play alone does not confirm that the viewer actually watched the video.

I do not transfer name, e-mail, content of forms or the content of general parameters and fragments of the current address to the measurement settings. Exceptions are pre-verified link tags from YouTube's own videos: YouTube source, link type, video label, and its public name. Thanks to them, I know from which video the visitor came to a specific article or other page. This information is only processed after consent to the measurement. Automatic extended metering is disabled. Measurement is not included in the sample applications in the directories /demo/.

Cookies and remembering choices

The choice of consent or refusal is stored in the local storage of this browser for a maximum of 180 days. If you delete the website data or use another browser, the option will appear again.

Analytics uses cookies after permission _ga a _ga_BM11EHQG00 to distinguish between visits and sessions. Their set maximum validity period is 180 days. After withdrawing the consent, the website disables further measurements and deletes these cookies. Revocation of consent alone does not delete previously processed data.

In Analytics, a retention period of 2 months is set for detailed event data and 14 months for user data, with a renewal period upon new activity. These deadlines do not apply to standard summary reports, which may be kept longer.

Jak změnit rozhodnutí

Open the footer of the page "Privacy Settings". You can allow or refuse the measurement at any time. This option applies to this browser on the Cestovatel69.cz website.

Google and advertising features

Analytics provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The processing may include the transfer of data within Google's infrastructure as well as outside the European Economic Area according to the terms of the provider. They describe the details Google Privacy Policy, use of data from partner websites a data processing conditions.

In this configuration, advertising personalization, Google Signals and consents for advertising purposes are turned off. Consent to measurement is not consent to personalized advertising. Any inclusion of AdSense ads will require a separate corresponding setup.

Other services on the website

Some pages contain YouTube videos, external fonts or GetYourGuide affiliate offers. Their connection is separate from Analytics; said option controls Google Analytics measurement. In the article about Hoslovický mlýn, the YouTube player only loads after clicking on the preview. When you open an external service, its own terms and privacy policies apply.
