Notes on the trip

Stockholm is the "Venice of the North" — built on 14 islands in the Mälar archipelago. Gamla Stan (old town), Vasa Museum (elevated flagship), modern districts. Uppsala is an hour north by train — a university town with the oldest university in Scandinavia (1477).
