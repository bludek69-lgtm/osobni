Notes on the trip

I travel Austria several times a year — bus routes to Tyrol, Salzburg, Vienna. Every corner has its own character — Vienna is metropolitan elegance, while Söll and Pinzgau (Hollersbach) are quiet mountain idylls. Skiing in winter, hiking in summer.
