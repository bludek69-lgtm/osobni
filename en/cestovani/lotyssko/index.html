Notes on the trip

Riga has the largest concentration of art nouveau architecture in Europe — Albertova Street is the golden stud. The UNESCO Old Town with its Cathedral, St. Peter's Church and the Dom Cathedral is compact and beautiful. In addition, a calm Baltic atmosphere and good coffee.
