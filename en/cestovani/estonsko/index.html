Notes on the trip

Tallinn is one of those cities where history cannot be separated from the present. The UNESCO old town with its walls, Olviste church and Toompea (upper town) is like a movie set — and yet people live there. Estonia is also a digital pioneer (e-Residency, e-government), so around the corner of the former Hanseatic city you will find a cafe full of laptops.
