Notes on the trip

New on the blog: Bohemian paradise – rocks, castles and stories with video and map →

The Czech Republic is my home and at the same time a country that I often indulge in for recreation. V Krkonoší I go to colors in autumn, on Sticky in the summer to the water. Český Krumlov is the pearl of southern Bohemia, which is pointed out by the bus clientele. Prague as a cultural center and South Bohemia with the history of Písek, the nuclear plant in Temelín.
