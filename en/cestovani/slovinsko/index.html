Notes on the trip

Lipica is not a tourist destination in the usual sense — it is the oldest still active stud farm in Europe. Lipizzans are born dark and turn white with age. The area is freely accessible, horses graze around avenues of linden trees, and the surrounding landscape is beautiful in itself.
