Traveling from the driver's perspective

Driver's work trips

My job as a driver has taken me to many places in Europe over the years. It was not only about driving, but also about getting to know cities, countries and people. Behind the wheel of the bus, I got to stay and study tours, regular trips to Croatia and Italy, as well as sightseeing tours around Europe for foreign clients.

This part of the website captures business travel through the eyes of a driver — the buses, routes, stops, cities and places I've had the chance to get to know through my work.

📌 Note: This section is not an advertisement for any carrier. It is a personal travel archive. Sensitive work information (client names, contract details, license plates) has been removed or not disclosed from the photographs. You will not find exact route schedules and internal information here.
