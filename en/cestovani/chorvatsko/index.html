Notes on the trip

I have seen Croatia more times than any other country — but always mainly from the bus on the way with clients. It is the Adriatic Sea with thousands of islands, rocks, lavender and olive trees. Dubrovnik is the most distinctive, but every small town in Dalmatia has an equally strong atmosphere.
