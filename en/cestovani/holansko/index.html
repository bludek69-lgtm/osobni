Notes on the trip

The Netherlands in April is tulip season — field u Keukenhof shining from horizon to horizon. Amsterdam has its canals and the Rijksmuseum (Vermeer, Rembrandt), but the real experience is driving the country outside the capital — windmills, cheese towns, bikes everywhere.
