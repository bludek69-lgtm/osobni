Who is this page for: who likes Britain without stereotypes. Three different worlds that I have been in both professionally and personally — wild Scotland, global London and the industrial center.

What you won't find here: tourist itinerary (Buckingham, London Eye...). Instead, there are custom images and a few sentences for each location.

Three different worlds

Scotland is a landscape that gets under your skin — the Highlands, Loch Ness, castles on rocky outcrops. The weather changes every twenty minutes, but you appreciate every glimpse between the clouds all the more.

London no introduction needed. Westminster, Tower, Tower Bridge, Thames — all in one long day on foot. British Rail will get you there easily, just mind the gap.

Birmingham and Central England — industrial heritage, canals (more than in Venice, they say), less tourists, more authentic.

"You don't have to remember Scotland, just remember what you felt."
