Van conversion

Current stage: searching for a van and planning

I am looking for a van for mostly solo travel, with trips around Italy and fishing trips in the Czech Republic in mind. First I need to find the right vehicle and work out what will really be useful inside.

What I am considering ↓

Where I hope to go

Travelling through Italy

Places I love returning to and new stops along the way. I hope the van will let me travel at my own pace.

Fishing in Czechia

Trips to the water with room for fishing gear. How to store and dry it is still an open question.
