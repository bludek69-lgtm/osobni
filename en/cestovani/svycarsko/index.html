Who is this page for: who wants to see Switzerland from several seasons — from spring to autumn 2018-2019. Mostly work trips by bus, but also personal stops.

How to read it: sections are chronological. At the top is 2018, at the bottom is 2019, and at the end is Geneva.

Notes on the trip

Switzerland is a country where every Cantonese owns a landscape. The Bernese Alps with peaks of 4000+ m, Lac Léman and Geneva with a French flair, Lucerne with the wooden Kapellbrücke bridge. Traveling in Switzerland is expensive, but the trains there run like clockwork — and the views are part of the ticket.
