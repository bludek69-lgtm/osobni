Who is this page for: who likes Germany without the cliche "Berlin = beer, Munich = Oktoberfest". Six cities and places where I have been with the bus and in person.

What you will find here: Berlin, Dresden, Wolfsburg (cars), Baden-Baden (spa), Dachau (monument) and the private Raketenstation Hombroich.

Notes on the trip

Germany is a country that I travel several times a year as a driver, but I rarely stop. This page is about places where I stopped on purpose — in Berlin for a few days, in Dresden in the winter before Christmas, in Dachau on a misty April morning, and in Hombroich among the statues and concrete bunkers, where today art works stand instead of rockets.

Dachau is a separate chapter. A visit to the memorial cannot be described by photo or text — only the silence between the former watchtowers and panels with stories. It's one of those things you remember forever.
