🏰 Orschwiller — Haut-Kœnigsbourg (Alsace)

A village at the foot of the Vosges slopes, the dominant feature is the Haut-Kœnigsbourg castle (757 m above sea level). From the castle there is a view of the entire Rhineland and on clear days as far as the Black Forest and the Alps. (10 photos · July 2019)

🏔 Chamonix-Mont-Blanc

A town in the Hautes-Alpes, gateway to Mont-Blanc. Aiguille du Midi cable car, views of the Mer de Glace (glacier), a classic alpine resort with a history of the first Winter Olympics (1924). (6 photos · July 2019)

Notes on the trip

Alsace is a region that behaves half France and half Germany — German-sounding village names, half-timbered houses, vineyards on the slopes of the Vosges and a castle Haut-Kœnigsbourg high above the landscape.

Chamonix is a small town below Mont-Blanc (4810 m) — the starting point for mountain tourism, the cable car to the Aiguille du Midi (3842 m) and one of the most beautiful views of the Alpine giants in Europe.
