Notes on the trip

The south of Italy is a completely different world than Tuscany or the north. Here, everything is noisier, more colorful, more authentic. Life takes place on the street, Neapolitan pizza has a long tradition, and Mount Vesuvius stands in the background as a silent reminder that nature always has the last word.

Pompeii are a separate chapter — walking the streets of an ancient city, buried by an eruption in 79 AD, is an experience that cannot be described. Amalfi coast a Positano they are visually probably the most beautiful piece of Italy that I know.

Pompeii: what to check before visiting

Mine main video from Pompeii ↗ and photos show the archaeological city. When planning, give him his own space next to Naples and the coast. The official archaeological park publishes the current ticket types, opening hours, last entry and rules for visitors; conditions may change according to season.

Before departure: check the official ticket for the part of the site you want to see, the entrance point and the current access to the individual buildings. The park also publishes information about a more accessible "Pompeii for All" route. I do not describe the prices or times here, so that the page does not remain out of date.
