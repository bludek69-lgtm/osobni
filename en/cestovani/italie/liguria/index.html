Lerici & Golfo dei Poeti

A seaside town in Liguria on the Gulf of La Spezia, where I spent some time: a castle above the harbour, colourful houses and sunsets over the Golfo dei Poeti.

A few words

Lerici is in Liguria (province of La Spezia), on the coast of the Gulf of La Spezia, about 8 km from the city of La Spezia. Tuscany is close by, but Lerici belongs to a different region. The bay is known as the Golfo dei Poeti, the Gulf of Poets.

Walking from San Terenzo to Lerici

My photograph shows Lerici at sunset. For a closer look at the coastline, the town's official tourism site describes the approximately 2 km Walk of Poetry linking San Terenzo and Lerici. It starts near Piazza Brusacà, passes a place associated with Percy and Mary Shelley, and follows the shore by Venere Azzurra towards Lerici Castle. The route tells the stories of writers who spent time around the gulf.

My tip: allow time for the views instead of treating the walk as a race. My own photo records the town's evening atmosphere, not every stop on the walk. Check the official route description and current conditions before visiting.
