Notes on the trip

Lerici is located in Liguria (province of La Spezia), on the coast of the Gulf of La Spezia, about 8 km from the city of La Spezia — it is only a short distance from Tuscany, but it belongs to a different region. The gulf is called Golfo dei Poeti, Gulf of Poets.

Lerici on foot: from San Terenza to the port

In my photo I capture Lerici at sunset. If you want to walk the coast, the city's official tourist website describes approx 2 km long Walk of Poetry between San Terence and Lerici. It starts at Piazza Brusacà, passes the site associated with Percy and Mary Shelley and runs along the Venere Azzurra towards Lerica Castle. Interpretation along the route commemorates the poets and writers who stayed by the bay.

My tip: take your time for the views and don't treat the walk as a race between two points. My photo shows the evening atmosphere of the city; check the other stops in the official description of the route and its current passability before visiting.
