Lombardy

One morning might begin among Milan’s trams and the next aboard a boat below the slopes of Lake Como. Lombardy offers very different kinds of trip.

I would choose one as the main focus and add the other as a carefully planned excursion.

Examples of places: Milan · Lake Como.

Why choose this region?

Milan, old Bergamo, the southern plain and Alpine valleys offer very different scales of travel.

I would give the lakes their own time for waterfront walks and boat journeys rather than fit them between distant cities.

From the hustle and bustle of Milan, you can continue to the lakes and mountain regions in the north. Bergamo offers the historic mining town of Città Alta.

What to see

The Duomo of Milan, Bergamo Alta and the towns by the lakes show the different features of the region.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: Spring or autumn would be my choice for towns and lower ground. Plan mountains separately around snow and trail opening: lakeside walks and high-altitude hikes have different seasons.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Check Trenord and Trenitalia for Milan and other towns. At the lakes, combine rail travel with Navigazione Laghi boats, checking seasonal timetables and the type of service.
  • What to check: Check restricted traffic zones and parking before driving into historic centres. For lakeside accommodation, look at the climb from the station as well as the distance.

What to taste

Risotto alla milanese is associated with saffron; in Lombardy you can also find polenta and various cheeses. Stuffed tortelli di zucca pasta appears around Mantua.

Franciacorta is an area known for sparkling wines produced using the traditional method. There are other, different wine regions around the lakes and in Valtellina.
