Lombardy

Lombardy connects Milan with Alpine valleys and lakes such as Como and Garda. Milan Cathedral, pictured on Piazza del Duomo, is one of the city’s defining landmarks.

Milan

Piazza del Duomo connects the cathedral and Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Consider the Sforza Castle or districts beyond the busiest centre.

Bergamo Alta

The upper town rises above modern Bergamo and is surrounded by walls. Its streets lead to Piazza Vecchia; check access and parking before arriving.

Lakes Como and Garda

Alpine lakes shape the northern landscape and Lombardy includes Garda’s western shore. Consider boats between towns and check seasonal timetables.

Landscape and travel

Milan and Bergamo Alta contrast with the Alpine lakes and valleys. Lake Como and the western shore of Garda reward a slower itinerary; boat timetables vary by season.

Local cuisine and wine

Milanese risotto is flavoured with saffron. In Valtellina, try buckwheat pizzoccheri; lake areas also offer fish dishes.

Franciacorta is known for sparkling wine, while Valtellina is associated with mountain vineyards.
