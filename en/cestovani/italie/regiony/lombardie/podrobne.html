Lombardy: what to see and how to travel

One morning might begin among Milan’s trams and the next aboard a boat below the slopes of Lake Como. Lombardy offers very different kinds of trip.

I would choose one as the main focus and add the other as a carefully planned excursion.

Why choose this region?

Milan, old Bergamo, the southern plain and Alpine valleys offer very different scales of travel.

I would give the lakes their own time for waterfront walks and boat journeys rather than fit them between distant cities.

Places worth knowing

Milan

Piazza del Duomo connects the cathedral with the Galleria Vittoria Emanuele II. Other stops may include Sforza Castle or neighborhoods outside the busiest center.

Bergamo Alta

The Upper Town lies above the newer part of Bergamo and is surrounded by walls.

The alleys lead to the Piazza Vecchia; check access and parking options in advance upon arrival.

Lakes Como and Garda

The north of the region includes the lakes below the Alps and the west bank of the Garda.

When traveling between coastal cities, consider shipping and allow for seasonal changes in timetables.

Two detours beyond the main stops

Sabbioneta

This planned Renaissance town reveals its layout as you walk between squares and walls.

I would naturally combine it with Mantua rather than drive back across the region.

Varenna

The village on Como’s eastern shore combines waterfront walks with boat excursions. Check actual sailing times before designing a circuit between harbours.

Landscape and road

Lombardy passes from the plain around Milan to the Alpine valleys and Lakes Como, Maggiore and Garda.

Bergamo and Brescia offer historic cities between the metropolis and the mountain landscape.

Milan and Bergamo can be explored by train; for lakes, choose a specific shore and compare boat and rail connections.

For mountain trips, follow seasonal access, weather and distance from the station.

What to taste

Risotto alla milanese is associated with saffron; in Lombardy you can also find polenta and various cheeses. Stuffed tortelli di zucca pasta appears around Mantua.

Franciacorta is an area known for sparkling wines produced using the traditional method. There are other, different wine regions around the lakes and in Valtellina.

How I would choose a meal

Milanese risotto can be recognized by the saffron.

But Lombardy also has mountain cuisine: buckwheat pizzoccheri are served in Valtellina, while there is no shortage of fish dishes around the lakes.

I would choose Milanese risotto or Valtellina dishes according to where I am.

For mountain wines, ask about the actual growing area and grape rather than simply looking for Lombardy.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: Spring or autumn would be my choice for towns and lower ground. Plan mountains separately around snow and trail opening: lakeside walks and high-altitude hikes have different seasons.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Check Trenord and Trenitalia for Milan and other towns. At the lakes, combine rail travel with Navigazione Laghi boats, checking seasonal timetables and the type of service.
  • What to check: Check restricted traffic zones and parking before driving into historic centres. For lakeside accommodation, look at the climb from the station as well as the distance.
