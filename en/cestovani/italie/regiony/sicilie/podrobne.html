Sicily

Sicily is the largest Mediterranean island, with a distinctive history and cuisine. The photograph shows coastal Cefalù; Palermo and Mount Etna are other notable places.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Palermo (in Czech) →

Palermo

Markets, historic streets and monuments from different periods shape Palermo. Choose between the cathedral and Norman Palace according to your interests and time.

Cefalù

The seaside town is known for its cathedral and La Rocca above the roofs. Old lanes lead towards the sea and the small harbour.

Etna and archaeological sites

Etna is an active volcano, so access changes with activity and weather. Sicily also has Greek and Roman sites, including around Agrigento and Syracuse.

Landscape and travel

Palermo’s markets, Cefalù’s historic centre and Mount Etna offer very different experiences. Etna is active: always check access and conditions before visiting.

Local cuisine and wine

Arancini are breaded, fried rice specialities with varied fillings and shapes. Sicilian sweets include cannoli and granita.

Nero d’Avola and Etna wines represent different parts of the island.
