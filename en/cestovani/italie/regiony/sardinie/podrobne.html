Sardinia

Sardinia is a Mediterranean island with a long coastline and a mountainous interior. The collage shows places in Cagliari, the regional capital in the south of the island.

Places worth exploring

Cagliari

The Castello quarter rises above Sardinia’s southern capital and looks towards the gulf. A walk can be combined with the Poetto waterfront.

Alghero

Alghero has an old centre and sea-facing walls. Nearby Capo Caccia and the Grotta di Nettuno are subject to weather and access conditions.

The interior and nuraghi

Stone nuraghe towers recall Sardinia’s ancient past. Allow for longer drives inland and check opening arrangements for the site you choose.

Landscape and travel

Cagliari and Alghero offer different coastal cities, while inland Sardinia preserves ancient nuraghe towers. Distances can be longer than they look on a map.

Local cuisine and wine

Culurgiones are filled pasta associated with Ogliastra; fillings may include potatoes, cheese and mint. Try pecorino or pane carasau as well.

Vermentino and Cannonau are among the island’s better-known wine names.
