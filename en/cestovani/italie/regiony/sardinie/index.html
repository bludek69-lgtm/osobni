Sardinia

Beyond Sardinia’s well-known beaches lie stone nuraghi, mountain roads and towns with their own traditions.

The colour of the sea tells only part of the story. I recommend choosing one area of the island and balancing beach days with trips inland.

Examples of places: Cagliari · Alghero.

Why choose this region?

Cagliari, Alghero, Gallura and Ogliastra are not neighbouring stops. Each needs a different plan.

I would save one area for another trip rather than spend every day driving a long way to a new beach.

The island alternates between beaches, rocky coasts and inland villages. When planning, it is worth considering a ferry or flight and longer transfers.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: For coast and inland outings, I would choose late spring or September. A May trip should not depend on warm seawater and swimming every day.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Choose an airport or ferry port to suit your chosen area, not just the cheapest fare. Check public transport for towns; consider a car for several remote stops in one day.
  • What to check: Check reservations, visitor limits and access rules for the chosen beach or protected area. If bringing a car by ferry, allow for check-in and the drive from port.

What to taste

Among the local dishes, you can try stuffed culurgiones pasta, pane carasau thin bread or sheep cheeses. The menu varies between the coast and the interior.

Cannonau is the island's famous red variety and Vermentino is used for white wines. The specific character depends on the region and the manufacturer.
