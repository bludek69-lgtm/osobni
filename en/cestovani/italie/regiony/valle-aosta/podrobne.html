Valle d’Aosta

The Aosta Valley is framed by Alpine massifs including Mont Blanc and Monte Rosa. Mountain valleys, stone villages and medieval castles shape the landscape; the photograph shows Fénis Castle.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Aosta →

Aosta

Roman remains, including the theatre and city gates, connect Aosta with its ancient past. The compact centre is a practical starting point for the valley.

Fénis Castle

The castle is recognised by its walls and towers. Check access, guided visits and opening times before travelling.

Gran Paradiso and the high Alps

Alpine valleys offer viewpoints and walks of different difficulty. Check the park’s current guidance and plan around weather, altitude and the season.

Landscape and travel

Aosta’s Roman remains provide a city stop amid Alpine valleys. Courmayeur, Cogne and the mountains need separate planning; check weather and seasonal access.

Local cuisine and wine

Fontina is a cheese closely linked to the Aosta Valley and used in warming mountain dishes. Polenta and other local cheeses also appear on menus.

Mountain vineyards produce regional wines at a range of altitudes; styles differ by producer.
