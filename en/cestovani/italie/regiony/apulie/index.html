Why choose this region?

Gargano, central Puglia and Salento offer different balances of towns, countryside and coast.

I recommend choosing two neighbouring areas instead of crossing the whole region every day.

Other stops include Bari and baroque Lecce. In the summer season, it is reasonable to check the conditions for visiting the most famous places.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: I would choose May or September for town visits. In July and August, save longer walks for morning or evening, and prioritise shade and water over another sight.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Bari and Lecce fit into a rail itinerary. For Valle d’Itria and remote beaches, check actual connections and replacement buses; a line on the map is not enough.
  • What to check: Have an alternative coastal outing ready for changing wind and sea conditions. Check afternoon opening at smaller businesses; there is no single closing rule across Puglia.

What to taste

Try the orecchiette, small pasta served with, for example, cime di rapa, or focaccia barese with tomatoes.

Fish and seafood appear on the coast, legumes, vegetables and olive oil inland.

Primitivo and Negroamaro are among the famous local blue varieties.

The resulting wines vary by region and producer; it is worth asking about the origin and style directly during the tasting.
