Calabria

Calabria forms the southern tip of mainland Italy between the Tyrrhenian and Ionian seas. The picture shows the historic center of Tropey above the coast.

Reggio Calabria lies on the Strait of Messina, which separates the mainland from Sicily. The coastal sections are complemented by a mountainous interior.
