Calabria

Calabria forms the southern tip of mainland Italy between the Tyrrhenian and Ionian seas. The photograph shows the historic centre of Tropea above the coast.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Scilla →

Tropea

Tropea’s historic streets stand above the Tyrrhenian Sea. Viewpoints look towards the coast and the sanctuary on its rocky outcrop.

Scilla

Scilla overlooks the Strait of Messina. Walk through the coastal Chianalea district and look up towards the castle above the water.

Sila and Aspromonte

The interior rises into the Sila and Aspromonte mountain areas. Choose a route for the season and check current access and weather.

Landscape and travel

Calabria stretches between the Tyrrhenian and Ionian seas, with mountains in between. Tropea, Scilla and the interior each invite a separate stop.

Local cuisine and wine

’Nduja is a spicy spreadable sausage associated with Spilinga. Try it sparingly on bread or in a sauce; red onions from Tropea are another local ingredient.

Cirò is a Calabrian wine area; grape and style depend on the individual wine.
