Umbria

Inland Umbria is a region of hills, olive groves and historic towns. In the photo, Assisi is associated with St. Francis; the regional center is Perugia.

Other locations include Lake Trasimeno and the city of Orvieto. In the hilly hinterland, it pays to expect slower movements.
