Umbria

Landlocked Umbria is a region of hills, olive groves and historic towns. The photograph shows Assisi, associated with Saint Francis; Perugia is the regional capital.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Perugia →

Perugia

The regional capital stands on a hill, with historic streets and viewpoints. Allow time for the ascent and a walk through the centre.

Assisi

Assisi is associated with Saint Francis and its basilica. Explore the stone streets on foot and check opening arrangements for the places you want to enter.

Orvieto and Lake Trasimeno

Orvieto rises on volcanic rock and is known for its cathedral. Lake Trasimeno offers a contrasting waterside stop; these are separate trips.

Landscape and travel

Perugia, Assisi and the countryside around Montefalco make a varied inland itinerary. Smaller towns and hill roads reward a slower pace.

Local cuisine and wine

Look for olive oil, pulses and truffles as well as Sagrantino wine from the Montefalco area. Check tasting arrangements and transport before visiting wineries.

Montefalco Sagrantino is connected with the Sagrantino grape and the Montefalco area.
