Veneto

Veneto stretches from the Dolomites past Verona to the Adriatic. Venice, pictured here, grew across islands in the lagoon, where canals and pedestrian lanes shape everyday life.

Beyond the lagoon, Verona and Lake Garda are worth exploring. Check local driving restrictions before entering cities.

Places to explore: Venezia · Verona.

Places to explore

Venice and Verona are complemented by smaller cities such as Padua and Vicenza.

Landscape and travel

The region includes part of the Dolomites, Lake Garda and the lagoon coast.

Local flavours

Look for local risotto, polenta or Venetian cicchetti snacks.
