Veneto

Veneto stretches from the Dolomites through Verona to the Adriatic Sea. The Venice in the picture was created on the islands of the lagoon, where canals and pedestrian streets are part of everyday life.

In addition to the lagoon, Verona and Lake Garda are worth attention. In cities, it is a good idea to check the local entry rules in advance.
