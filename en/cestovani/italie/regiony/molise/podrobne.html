Molise

The small region of Molise includes inland hills and a short Adriatic coast. The photograph shows the old town of Termoli above the sea; Campobasso is the regional capital.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Campobasso (in Czech) →

Termoli

The old town sits on a coastal promontory, with narrow streets, a cathedral and a fortress. Seasonal boats may connect the port with the Tremiti Islands.

Campobasso

The regional capital rises on a hillside below Monforte Castle. Its old streets and viewpoints offer a contrast to the Adriatic coast.

Mountains and villages

From the sea the region becomes a hilly interior with small villages and mountain roads. Allow extra travel time and check the weather.

Landscape and travel

The old town of Termoli overlooks the Adriatic, while Campobasso and the mountain villages reveal the inland region. Allow extra time on rural roads.

Local cuisine and wine

Cavatelli are small shaped pasta served with a variety of sauces. Local cooking also draws on regional cheeses and seasonal produce.

Tintilia is a grape associated with Molise; styles differ among producers.
