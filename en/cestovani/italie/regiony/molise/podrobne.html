Molise: what to see and how to travel

From Termoli’s harbour, the journey leads inland to hilltop towns and archaeological sites away from the main roads.

I would choose Molise for a trip with fewer stops and more time at each. Changing accommodation every evening would defeat the point.

Why choose this region?

The coast is only one part of Molise; inland journeys lead to Campobasso, Isernia and mountain villages.

I would pair a town with one nearby destination and allow transfer time. A small region does not guarantee quick connections.

Places worth knowing

Thermos

The old town on the coastal promontory consists of narrow streets, a cathedral and a fortress above the sea.

Termoli is also the port from which, depending on the season, connections to the Tremiti Islands depart.

Campobasso

The inland capital is located on the hillside below Monforte Castle. Historic streets and viewpoints provide a different stop than the Adriatic coast.

Mountains and villages

Molise passes from the sea to the hilly interior.

Smaller villages and mountain roads are suitable for a slower journey by car, during which it is good to expect longer journeys.

Two detours beyond the main stops

Sepino – Altilia

Roman remains make the town’s layout and everyday life easier to imagine. Check access and activities with the site manager before assuming all areas can be visited.

Agnone

The town is associated with bell-making and other crafts. Arrange any workshop or museum visit before arriving rather than relying on an unplanned call.

Landscape and road

The small region between the Apennines and the Adriatic includes a mountainous interior, villages and a short coast near Termoli.

Unlike just looking at the map, moving in the mountains can take more time.

I consider the coastal Termoli and the inland towns of Campobasso and Isernia as separate stops.

When planning public transport, check connections in advance; in smaller municipalities the intervals may not be frequent.

What to taste

Local pastas include cavatelli; in different regions they are served with sauces according to the season. Cheese, olive oil and legume dishes are also typical.

Tintilia is a variety associated with Molise. When buying, orientate yourself according to the designation of origin and the specific winemaker.

How I would choose a meal

Cavatelli are small shaped pasta also widespread in Molise.

They can be served with various sauces or simply with breadcrumbs; the photo recipe is one of the possible modifications.

Cavatelli come with different preparations; I would choose a seasonal sauce.

When tasting cheese, ask about its origin and maturation instead of choosing only by portion size.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: Spring or early autumn would be my choice for inland walks. In winter, expect different conditions in mountain villages and on the coast.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Consider Termoli first as a rail destination. For smaller villages, check current bus services and operating days. I would avoid changing bases repeatedly without confirmed connections.
  • What to check: Allow spare time and bring food for longer drives. Check small restaurants and craft businesses directly rather than assuming they remain open throughout the afternoon.
