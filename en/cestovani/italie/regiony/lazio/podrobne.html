Lazio

Lazio surrounds Rome and reaches the Tyrrhenian Sea. In the capital, ancient monuments meet Baroque squares and fountains; the photograph shows the Trevi Fountain.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Tivoli →

Rome

The Colosseum and Roman Forum can be combined with the squares and fountains of the historic centre. Divide the city into several walks.

Tivoli

Villa d’Este is known for its gardens and fountains; Hadrian’s Villa is another archaeological site in the wider area. Check entry and transport between them.

Lake Bracciano

The volcanic lake and nearby towns offer a quieter stop outside Rome. Check local transport and access to the waterfront for your travel dates.

Landscape and travel

Rome’s historic centre and ancient sites are best divided into several walks. Tivoli and Lake Bracciano provide different excursions beyond the capital.

Local cuisine and wine

Roman classics include carbonara, amatriciana and cacio e pepe. The last combines pecorino romano cheese and black pepper.

Frascati is a wine area in the hills southeast of Rome.
