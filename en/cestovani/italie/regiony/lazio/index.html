Lazio

Lazio surrounds Rome and extends to the Tyrrhenian Sea. In the capital, ancient monuments meet baroque squares and fountains; the picture shows the Trevi Fountain.

Outside of Rome, you can visit, for example, Tivoli or the coast south of the capital. Traffic restrictions in Rome need to be verified according to the specific route.
