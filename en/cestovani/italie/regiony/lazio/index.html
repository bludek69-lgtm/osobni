Lazio

A short walk through Rome can take you from ancient walls to a busy square and a café.

Lazio continues beyond the capital to lakes, tuff cliffs and the coast.

I would give Rome its own time and treat trips outside the city as another chapter.

Why choose this region?

The contrast between Rome and the smaller places around it is part of Lazio’s appeal.

Tivoli, the Viterbo area and the coast lie in different directions; I would not combine them into one rushed day.

Outside of Rome, you can visit, for example, Tivoli or the coast south of the capital. Traffic restrictions in Rome need to be verified according to the specific route.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: For long walks through Rome, I would choose spring or autumn. Reserve major sights for specific times, but leave gaps rather than filling every hour.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Start with trains for Rome and other larger towns. For Civita di Bagnoregio and smaller coastal destinations, check the final bus connection and return, particularly at weekends.
  • What to check: Plan parking before driving towards a historic centre. Check ZTL restrictions, accommodation access and official ticket conditions. Remember that Vatican City is a separate state.

What to taste

Roman cuisine is associated with pasta cacio e pepe, amatriciana and carbonara.

It often uses pecorino romano; the offer in markets and restaurants changes according to the season.

Frascati is a famous wine region around Rome. With food, you can compare local white wines by variety and producer.
