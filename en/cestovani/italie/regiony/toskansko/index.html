Tuscany

Tuscany offers Renaissance cities as well as an agricultural landscape among the hills. Pictured are cypresses in the Val d'Orcia; the area also includes Florence, Siena and Pisa.

Between cities and the countryside, the journey can be divided into several stops. In Florence, Siena and other historic centers, watch for restricted entry zones.

Examples of places: Firenze · Siena · Pisa · Val d'Orcia.
