Tuscany

Tuscany combines Renaissance cities with cultivated hills. The photograph shows cypresses in Val d’Orcia; Florence, Siena and Pisa are also in the region.

Places worth exploring

Siena

Piazza del Campo forms the heart of medieval Siena, with the town hall and tower. Continue on foot to the cathedral and neighbouring historic streets.

Detailed guide →

Florence and Pisa

Florence brings together Renaissance art around its cathedral and the Arno. Pisa’s Piazza dei Miracoli contains the cathedral, baptistery and leaning tower.

Val d’Orcia

South of Siena, fields, cypress trees and historic towns shape the valley. Combine viewpoints with Pienza and stop only safely, respecting private land.

Landscape and travel

Spend time in Siena and Florence, then explore Pienza, San Quirico d’Orcia and the hills of Val d’Orcia. Respect restricted traffic zones and stop only where parking is safe and permitted.

Local cuisine and wine

Ribollita is a hearty soup with bread, vegetables and beans; near Pienza look for pecorino. Chianti wines often use Sangiovese, while Brunello comes from Montalcino.

Chianti and Chianti Classico are distinct wine designations; Brunello di Montalcino is made around Montalcino.
