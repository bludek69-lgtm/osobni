Friuli Venezia Giulia

Friuli Venezia Giulia borders Austria and Slovenia. Trieste, shown in the photograph, is an Adriatic port, while the north of the region rises into the Alps.

Places worth exploring

Trieste

The Adriatic port combines Piazza Unità d’Italia, waterfront walks and cafés. The Karst plateau rises behind the city, with different scenery inland.

Miramare

Miramare Castle stands above the sea northwest of Trieste, surrounded by a park. Check opening arrangements before visiting the interiors.

Aquileia and Grado

Aquileia preserves Roman remains and an early Christian basilica with mosaics. Nearby Grado faces the Adriatic lagoon and offers a coastal walk.

Landscape and travel

Trieste sits between the Adriatic and the Karst plateau. Val Rosandra offers rocky scenery inland; the mountains further north require their own itinerary and weather checks.

Local cuisine and wine

Frico is a cheese dish made in crisp or soft versions, sometimes with potatoes. The borderland cuisine combines Italian, Alpine and Slovenian influences.

Friulano, Ribolla Gialla and Pinot Grigio are among the grapes encountered in regional wines.
