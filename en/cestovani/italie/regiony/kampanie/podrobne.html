Campania

Campania brings together the Bay of Naples, Vesuvius and the Amalfi Coast. The photograph shows Positano between mountains and sea; Pompeii is one of the region’s major archaeological sites.

Naples

Walk the historic centre, see Piazza del Plebiscito and follow the waterfront for different views of the city. Allow for busy traffic.

Pompeii and Vesuvius

Pompeii preserves the remains of a Roman city buried by the eruption of Vesuvius. Allow time for the extensive site and check volcano access separately.

Amalfi Coast

Amalfi, Positano and other towns sit on a steep coastline. Roads are narrow and can be busy; compare seasonal bus and boat options.

Landscape and travel

Naples, Pompeii and the Amalfi Coast each need time. Check access to Vesuvius and compare seasonal bus and boat services along the narrow coast.

Local cuisine and wine

Try Neapolitan pizza, including margherita and marinara, and local buffalo mozzarella. Recipes and menus differ from one place to another.

Among regional white wines are Fiano di Avellino and Greco di Tufo.
