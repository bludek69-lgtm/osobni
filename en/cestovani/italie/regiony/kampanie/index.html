ITALY · REGIONS

Campania

Vesuvius remains part of the view across the Bay of Naples whether you are having coffee or waiting for a boat.

In Campania, volcanic landscapes are woven into everyday city life.

I recommend combining Naples with a few excursions that work together geographically and by public transport.

Examples of places: Naples · Pompeii · Amalfi.

Why choose this region?

Naples, archaeological sites, islands and the Amalfi Coast each need their own plan. In the city I would walk and use public transport.

A free day between outings leaves room for weather changes and rest.

The landscape around Vesuvius recalls the ancient history of Pompeii. On the Amalfi Coast, expect narrow roads and check local traffic rules.

What to see

Naples, Pompeii and the Amalfi Coast can be connected to the islands of the Gulf of Naples.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: Spring or autumn would be my choice for Naples and archaeological sites. In summer, visit exposed areas early and leave part of the day for shade or a museum.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Plan Pompeii, Herculaneum and other stops around actual stations and operators. Compare boats for Amalfi and the islands, remembering that seasons and sea conditions affect services.
  • What to check: Check Vesuvius admission, transport and the walking section separately. For volcanic areas, follow current park, INGV and civil-protection information rather than assuming access shown in an older film remains available.

What to taste

Neapolitan pizza is associated with the city of Naples, the region is also home to mozzarella di bufala campana.

You can find fish and lemon specialties near the coast, and vegetable and pasta dishes inland.

Local wines include white Fiano di Avellino and Greco di Tufo; other wine regions also follow the volcanic soils. When tasting, follow the origin indicated on the label.
