Marche

Marche connects the Adriatic coast with the Apennines. The photograph shows Urbino and its Ducal Palace and historic centre; the regional capital Ancona lies on the coast.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Ancona →

Urbino

The Renaissance city is associated with the Ducal Palace and surrounding hills. Explore the historic centre on foot and expect steep streets.

Ancona and Conero

Ancona is an Adriatic port. South of the city, the Conero coast combines cliffs and bays near Sirolo and Numana.

Ascoli Piceno

Stone squares and passages characterise this inland town. Combine a walk around Piazza del Popolo with a taste of local stuffed olives.

Landscape and travel

Urbino’s Renaissance centre, the port of Ancona and the Conero coast make contrasting stops. Ascoli Piceno is known for its stone squares inland.

Local cuisine and wine

Olive all’ascolana are stuffed and fried olives associated with Ascoli Piceno. Along the coast, look for fish dishes such as brodetto.

Verdicchio is a grape associated with the region’s white wines.
