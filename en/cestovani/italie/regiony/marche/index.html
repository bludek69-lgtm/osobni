ITALY · REGIONS

Marche

Green slopes rise above the Adriatic at Conero, while inland towns stand on hilltops among the fields.

I would explore Marche in shorter stages between the sea and the Apennines. Each area deserves more time than it takes to collect another photograph.

Examples of places: Ancona · Urbino.

Why choose this region?

Urbino, Ascoli Piceno and Ancona reveal different urban sides of Marche. The countryside between them deserves attention too.

For a first visit, I would choose the north or south and add a coastal day.

On the coast lies Ancona and the Conero region; inland, roads lead to hills and historic towns.

What to see

Urbino resembles the Renaissance; Ancona is the port center of the region.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: For towns and hills, I would choose May, June or September. Plan swimming and walks in the Sibillini separately around actual conditions.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Check trains first for coastal destinations. For hill towns, find the station’s location and onward bus. Several inland stops may be easier to link by car.
  • What to check: Check mountain trail access with the park. Confirm the opening of individual monuments too: an accessible town centre does not mean every building is open.

What to taste

Olive all’ascolana are stuffed and fried olives associated with Ascoli Piceno; vincisgrassi is a baked layered pasta.

On the coast you will find various local forms of brodetto fish soup.

Verdicchio is the famous white variety of the region; look for wines from the Castelli di Jesi or Matelica regions, for example.
