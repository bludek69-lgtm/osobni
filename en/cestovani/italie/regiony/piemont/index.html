Piedmont

Piedmont lies beneath the western Alps, with Turin as its capital. The wine hills of the Langhe lie to the south; the photograph shows Turin’s Piazza Castello.

Beyond Turin, Alba and the vineyards around Barolo are worth exploring. Mountains and food can share one itinerary.

Places to explore: Torino · Langhe.

Places to explore

Turin combines squares and museums; Alba is a starting point for the Langhe hills.
