Piedmont

Piedmont lies beneath the western Alps, with Turin as its capital. The wine hills of the Langhe lie to the south; the photograph shows Turin’s Piazza Castello.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Alba →

Turin

Piazza Castello and Piazza San Carlo are good starting points. Arcades, museums and the River Po reveal different sides of the city.

Langhe and Alba

South of Turin lie vineyard hills and towns around Alba. Plan a route between villages and viewpoints, leaving time for the local food.

Alps and lakes

The region rises into Alpine valleys to the north and west. Mountain routes depend on the season; check roads and access before departure.

Landscape and travel

Turin’s squares and arcades make a good city walk. South of Turin, the vineyard hills of Langhe and towns around Alba invite a slower journey; the Alps lie to the north and west.

Local cuisine and wine

Tajarin are thin egg noodles. The Langhe are also associated with hazelnuts, truffles and wines; truffle availability depends on season and origin.

Barolo and Barbaresco are wines made from Nebbiolo in distinct areas of the Langhe.
