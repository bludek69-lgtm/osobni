Emilia-Romagna

Emilia-Romagna stretches from the Po plain and Apennines to the Adriatic. Bologna, shown here, is known for its porticoes and medieval towers; Modena and Ravenna are other stops.

Places worth exploring

Bologna

Explore Piazza Maggiore, the historic streets and Bologna’s long arcades. The city is also a convenient rail base for nearby towns.

Ravenna

Ravenna is celebrated for early Christian and Byzantine mosaics. Check opening times and ticket arrangements for the individual monuments.

Modena and surroundings

Modena’s cathedral and historic centre can be combined with the region’s food traditions. Nearby producers may offer visits; arrange them in advance.

Landscape and travel

Bologna, Parma and Modena are linked by rail and each deserves its own stop. The Apennines and the Adriatic coast offer contrasting landscapes.

Local cuisine and wine

Taste tagliatelle al ragù or tortellini in Bologna. Parmigiano Reggiano and traditional balsamic vinegar have defined areas of origin.

Lambrusco refers to a family of grapes and wines, with different styles and degrees of sweetness.
