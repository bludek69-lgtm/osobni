ITALY · REGIONS

Trentino-Alto Adige

Below the rocky Dolomites, the valleys contain vineyards, orchards and towns shaped by several language traditions.

Trentino and South Tyrol share a mountain setting but have distinct identities.

I recommend choosing one valley as a base and planning excursions from there.

Examples of places: Trento · Bolzano · Dolomites.

Why choose this region?

Trento, Bolzano, the Garda area and the high valleys offer different holidays. Temperature, trails and transport change between town and mountain.

I would not change bases daily simply for another photographed lake.

When planning mountain routes, keep an eye on the weather and the seasonal operation of the cable cars.

In addition to the mountains, the region offers the historic centers of both capitals.

What to see

Trento and Bolzano have historic centers; mountain valleys offer a number of smaller stops.

Practical travel tips

  • When to go: I would choose spring or autumn for valley towns. For high mountain walks, follow snow conditions and hut or trail openings; a June booking alone does not guarantee summer conditions.
  • Cars, trains and connections: Check Trentino Trasporti and südtirolmobil before relying on a car. For cable cars, verify the last descent and connecting bus to avoid an unplanned long walk down.
  • What to check: Keep checking mountain weather and have a shorter alternative. Verify road, parking and lake access for the actual day: seasonal transport or booking arrangements may apply.

What to taste

Canederli, or dumplings made of pastry, speck and cheese, show the connection between mountain and Central European cuisine.

Polenta and apple desserts also appear on the menu.

Local white wines include, for example, Pinot Grigio or Gewürztraminer; among other reds, Lagrein is grown here.

Trentino is also famous for its Trento DOC sparkling wines.
