Trentino-Alto Adige

In the north, Italian and German-speaking cultures collide; the administrative centers are Trento and Bolzano. The mountain landscape consists of, among others, the Dolomites, while the photo shows Bolzano in the valley.

When planning mountain routes, keep an eye on the weather and the seasonal operation of the cable cars. In addition to the mountains, the region offers the historic centers of both capitals.

Examples of places: Trento · Bolzano · Dolomites.
