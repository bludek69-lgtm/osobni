Trentino-Alto Adige

Italian- and German-speaking cultures meet in this northern region, whose administrative centres are Trento and Bolzano. The mountains include the Dolomites; the photograph shows Bolzano in its valley.

Trento and Bolzano

Trento and Bolzano each have historic centres and make useful bases for the valleys. Their architecture and traditions reflect the region’s varied cultural history.

Dolomites

The Dolomites offer mountain passes, valleys and walking routes. Choose a route suitable for your experience and check weather, lifts and path access.

Northern Lake Garda

Riva del Garda and Torbole lie at the northern end of the lake, beneath steep mountains. Walk by the water or plan an outing according to weather conditions.

Landscape and travel

The Dolomites, Alpine valleys and towns such as Trento and Bolzano offer many possible routes. Choose walks for the season and check weather, lift and path conditions.

Local cuisine and wine

Canederli, or Knödel, are bread dumplings served in broth or with other ingredients. Variations include cheese, spinach or cured meat.

Regional wines include Teroldego and wines from the Gewürztraminer grape.
