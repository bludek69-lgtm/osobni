Basilicata

Basilicata is a largely mountainous region of southern Italy. The photograph shows Matera and the Sassi districts, where homes and spaces are partly carved into rock.

Places worth exploring

Detailed guide: Matera (in Czech) →

Matera

The Sassi are historic districts of stone houses and cave dwellings. Explore the viewpoints and lanes on foot, allowing for steps and uneven surfaces.

Dolomiti Lucane

Rocky peaks rise above the villages of Castelmezzano and Pietrapertosa. Mountain roads and walking routes take time; check conditions before travelling.

Maratea

Maratea provides a Tyrrhenian coastal stop with a historic centre and views from the slopes above the sea. The coastline has coves and steep roads.

Landscape and travel

Matera’s stone districts are a striking contrast to the mountains and villages inland. The Vulture area in the north offers another perspective on the region.

Local cuisine and wine

Look for Matera bread, pulses and the crisp dried peppers called peperoni cruschi. Aglianico del Vulture is a red wine from the north of the region.

Aglianico is the grape; Vulture identifies the area linked to the wine.
