Basilicata: what to see and how to travel

Basilicata is a predominantly mountainous region of southern Italy. The photo shows Matera with the Sassi districts, whose houses and spaces are partially carved into the rock.

On this page: Mother · Lucan Dolomites · Maratea

Places worth knowing

Detailed guide: Matera →

Mother

The old Sassi neighborhoods consist of houses and churches carved into the rocky slopes above a deep ravine. The best way to get to know them is on foot; stairs and uneven surfaces are good to take into account when planning.

Lucan Dolomites

Rocky peaks inland surround the villages of Castelmezzano and Pietrapertosa. Choose between viewpoints and walking routes according to the weather and physical condition.

Maratea

The city and its surroundings lie along the short Tyrrhenian coast of Basilicata. Views of the bays are complemented by a statue of Christ above the city and mountain roads leading inland.

Landscape and road

Basilicata connects the mountainous interior with short stretches of the coast of the Tyrrhenian and Ionian seas. Matera sits above the Gravina gorge, while the Lucane Dolomites create a rocky backdrop around the mountain villages.

Explore Matera on foot and reserve another day for the mountain towns of Castelmezzano and Pietrapertosa. Between the coast and the interior, expect bends and longer driving times than the distance alone would suggest.

Local cuisine and wine

Typical of the region are dried peperoni cruschi peppers, often served with local dishes, and pastries or cheeses from the mountain regions. The composition and appearance of meals varies by municipality.

Aglianico del Vulture is a wine associated with the surroundings of the extinct volcano Vulture in the north of the region. When choosing, focus on specific origin and vintage.

What to taste on the way

Peperoni cruschi are dried peppers that acquire a brittle consistency after a short preparation. They are used as part of pasta dishes and as a side dish; individual recipes differ between municipalities.
