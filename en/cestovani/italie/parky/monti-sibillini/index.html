ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Monti Sibillini

Mountain landscape of the central Apennines between Umbria and Marche. Viewpoints, plateaus and mountain towns can be combined into an inland route.

What to see in the park

Mountains and plateaus

The surroundings of Castelluccio are known for their highland landscape, the appearance of which changes throughout the year. When choosing a view, watch access and parking and do not enter cultivated fields.

Tips for trips and stops

Castelluccio and the plateau

You can admire the landscape near Castelluccio from the permitted paths and viewpoints. The flowering season varies from year to year; do not enter the fields.

Visso and Cupi

The area of Visso and Cupi is part of the stage network of the park. For a shorter visit, choose a safe section and verify access in advance.

Fiastra

The area around Fiastra can be another base for exploring the northern part of the park. Check traffic and visitor restrictions for a specific location.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
