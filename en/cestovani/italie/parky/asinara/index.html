ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Asinara

The island off the northwest coast of Sardinia has a history as both a quarantine station and a prison. Today it is a protected area with special visiting rules.

What to see in the park

An island with an unusual past

Asinara served as a quarantine station as well as a prison and is now a protected island. Plan your arrival and movement around the island in advance according to the rules of administration and boat connections.

Tips for trips and stops

Sentiero della Memoria

The memory trail introduces the island’s human history. Check the starting point and whether a guide is required.

Sentiero del Granito

The granite trail is one of the ways to get to know the island's landscape. Weather and transport to the island affect the entire visit schedule.

Historic sites of the island

Former quarantine and prison sites bring Asinara's past closer. Access to individual objects may vary.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
