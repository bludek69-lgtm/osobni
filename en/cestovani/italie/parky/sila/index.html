ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Sila

The plateau in the interior of Calabria is made up of forests, pastures and lakes. Elevation means different weather to the nearby coast.

What to see in the park

Highland forests of Calabria

Sila offers a high wooded landscape, which differs in nature and temperature from the coast. A stop at one of the lakes can be combined with a shorter walk depending on local conditions.

Tips for trips and stops

A visit to the forests of Sily

The forest landscape can be explored from various visitor points. Find out the current entry options and trail selection.

Villaggio Mancuso

The Villaggio Mancuso area is one of the starting points in the network of the park's routes. For a specific stage, follow the signs and transport back.

Lake landscape

The surroundings of the lakes show a different face of the Calabrian hinterland. For the stop, select permitted accesses and parking.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and possible reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take away the garbage and keep animals at a distance.
