ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Val Grande

An extensive mountain area in the Piedmont part of the Alps. Abandoned pastures, forests and steep valleys remind of earlier farming and the gradual return of wild nature.

What to see in the park

The landscape of old farmsteads

Abandoned mountain pastures and stone buildings remind us that today's wilderness has been farmed for generations. Many roads lead through remote terrain, so choose the route according to your experience and time.

Tips for trips and stops

Cicogna

The mountain village of Cicogna is one of the starting points for exploring the park. Even arriving on a mountain road requires time and caution.

The story of work in the mountains

Look for the route Il respiro della storia or La civiltà della fatica. They introduce life and work in the mountains; check the specific course on the map.

To the remote hinterland

Choose longer transitions into deserted valleys only with experience in mountain terrain. Signal, water and return options may not be self-evident.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
