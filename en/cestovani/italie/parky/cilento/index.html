ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Cilento, Vallo di Diano e Alburni

The vast park in the south of Campania connects the coast with the mountainous interior, villages and agricultural countryside. Leave enough time for the journey between individual parts.

What to see in the park

From the coast to the interior

The Cilento is vast and the connection between the coastal communities and the mountainous interior will take time. You can build a route around one valley or part of the coast instead of trying to drive through the entire park in one day.

Tips for trips and stops

Cilento coast

Choose one part of the coast and explore it with time to spare. Bays and access roads have varying accessibility.

Vallo di Diano

The inland valley offers a different character of the journey than the coast. Reserve a separate day to visit the villages and surrounding trails.

Alburni

The Alburni Limestone Range is an option for more experienced visitors. Find out about the status of routes and access to cave areas.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
