ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Gran Sasso e Monti della Laga

The high Apennine massifs, plateaus and mountain villages make up the diverse region of central Italy. Conditions on mountain trails change quickly.

What to see in the park

Campo Imperatore and mountain villages

The Campo Imperatore plateau shows the open form of the Apennines and is the starting point for various excursions. Mountain roads, weather and snow can significantly change availability throughout the year.

Tips for trips and stops

Campo Imperatore

The plateau is one of the park's most distinctive landscapes. Combine a lookout or a stop with considerate parking and a mountain weather check.

Mountain villages

When traveling in Abruzzo, you can combine the countryside with a visit to the surrounding mountain villages. Plan your moves with a reserve and check closures.

Routes of the Gran Sasso massif

Longer ascents in the massif require mountain equipment and experience. Choose a specific route according to the conditions and information of the park administration.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
