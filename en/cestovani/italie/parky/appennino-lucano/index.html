ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Appennino Lucano

The mountains of the southern Apennines, forests and smaller villages give a glimpse into the hinterland of Basilicata. Distances on mountain roads can be time-consuming.

What to see in the park

Interior of Basilicata

The mountain valleys and villages of Basilicata show the calmer side of southern Italy. When driving, compare time instead of just distance: inland roads can be slow and winding.

Tips for trips and stops

Forest area Abetina

Silver fir forests are among the possibilities for exploring the mountain environment. Check the starting point and the status of the route with the park administration.

Itinerary of the monks

The Itinerario dei Monaci connects exploring the territory with its cultural context. Find out in advance the direction and difficulty of the chosen part.

Val d'Agri landscape

Stops in the valley and surrounding villages will give you a glimpse into the hinterland of Basilicata. Plan your journeys on mountain roads with a margin.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and possible reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
