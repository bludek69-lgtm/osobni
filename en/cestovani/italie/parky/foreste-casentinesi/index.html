ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Foreste Casentinesi

An extensive forest area between Tuscany and Emilia-Romagna. Its peculiarities include old forests and places associated with monastic tradition.

What to see in the park

Forests and monastic landscape

Vast forests form, together with places like Camaldoli and La Verna, a landscape where nature has long been intertwined with human activity. Choose your starting point depending on whether you want a short walk or a mountain hike.

Tips for trips and stops

Camaldoli

The surroundings of Camaldoli combine a forest environment with a monastic tradition. Choose the route depending on whether you want a short stop or a longer forest walk.

La Verna

The La Verna sanctuary allows you to combine a cultural stop with exploring the surrounding mountain landscape. Before you travel, check the opening and access.

Campigna

From Campigny, you can plan a visit to the forest part of the park. For longer stage journeys, find out the map, elevation gain and return transport options.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and possible reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take away the garbage and keep animals at a distance.
