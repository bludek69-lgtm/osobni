ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Abruzzo, Lazio e Molise

The mountain forests and meadows between the three regions provide a habitat for rare animals including the Marsican brown bear. Observe the animals from a distance and never feed them.

What to see in the park

Forest valleys and rare animals

The park protects the Apennine forests and the habitat of the Marsican brown bear. Observe quietly from a distance; sightings are not guaranteed and animals must never be fed.

Tips for trips and stops

Val Fondillo

The valley near Opi is the starting point for several routes. A shorter stay in the valley and a longer journey to the mountain saddles have different demands.

Barrea and the lake

Barrea is a convenient stop to explore the mountain landscape by the lake. A longer route to Lago Vivo requires separate planning.

Val di Rose

Val di Rose is one of the famous mountain destinations where special nature protection rules may apply. Check access with park management in advance.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and possible reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
