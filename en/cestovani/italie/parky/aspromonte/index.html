ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Aspromonte

The southern end of the Apennines offers mountain ridges and deep valleys near the sea. Inland travel may be slower than the map suggests.

What to see in the park

Between the mountains and the sea

The deep valleys and ridges of Aspromonte reveal the geological diversity of southern Calabria. Choose one starting valley and leave time for slower mountain moves.

Tips for trips and stops

Gambarie

Gambarie is the starting point for mountain trips. Plan ascents towards Montalto according to the weather and your own condition.

Pietra Cappa

A distinctive rock formation belongs to the places that can give a clear goal to the path. Check the arrival and marked access in advance.

Ancient Greek paths

The Antico Sentiero dei Greci commemorates the cultural history of the area. For longer sections, return traffic and the current state of markings are important.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
