ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Gran Paradiso

High mountain environment between Valle d'Aosta and Piedmont. The park is associated with ibex conservation; mountain villages are the starting point for trips of various lengths.

What to see in the park

The Cogne and Valsavarenche valleys

From the mountain villages, you can observe the differences between the pastures at lower altitudes and the alpine environment. Cogne and Valsavarenche are among the well-known valleys from which excursions are planned. Do not approach or disturb the ibex or other animals.

Tips for trips and stops

Cogne and Lillaz Falls

From Cogne, head to the Lillaz settlement and waterfalls. Choose the length of the walk according to the weather; in winter and after rain, the surface may change.

Lake Ceresole

At Ceresole Reale, the park offers a circuit around the lake. Check in advance which part of the route is accessible in the given season.

Pont and Nivolet

From Pont in Valsavarenche, a mountain trail leads towards Nivolet. This is a more demanding high-altitude trip; verify elevation, weather and return conditions.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
