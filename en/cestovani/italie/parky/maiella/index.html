ITALY · NATIONAL PARK

Maiella

The limestone massif of Abruzzo hides mountain trails, deep valleys and rock hermitages. When choosing a route, follow the altitude and the weather forecast.

What to see in the park

Rocks, valleys and hermitages

In the Maiella massif, the natural landscape meets traces of hermit life. Valley paths and rock hermitages have varying accessibility; choose a suitable approach in advance.

Tips for trips and stops

Lama Bianca

At Sant’Eufemia a Maiella, the administration offers visitor routes in the Lama Bianca forest area. Find out which option and access are open at the time of your visit.

Bocca di Valle

The Bocca di Valle area is one of the starting points for exploring the valley and the rocks. Check the length and profile of the chosen route.

Hermitages in the rocks

Maiella is associated with hermitage sites. Plan your visit to each hermitage according to the access conditions, any guide and the weather.

How to plan a visit

Choose the starting point according to which part of the park you want to visit. Compare the route by length, elevation gain, surface and return time. In the mountains and on the islands, the weather can change quickly.

Before leaving, check with the park administration for the current state of the trails, access conditions and any reservations. Respect the marked protection boundaries, take your litter home and observe wildlife from a distance.
