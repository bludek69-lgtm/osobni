A few words

Northern Italy spans several regions. Milan and Bergamo are in Lombardy; Lake Garda also reaches Veneto and Trentino in Trentino-Alto Adige. Around the lake are Riva del Garda, Sirmione, Garda, Bardolino and Malcesine. These are my photographs from towns, valleys and lakes.
