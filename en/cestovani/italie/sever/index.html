Notes on the trip

Northern Italy includes multiple regions. Milan and Bergamo lie in Lombardy; Lago di Garda also extends into Veneto and Trentino in the Trentino-Alto Adige region. Around the lake you will find Riva del Garda, Sirmione, Garda, Bardolino and Malcesine. Here are my photos of cities, valleys and lakes.
