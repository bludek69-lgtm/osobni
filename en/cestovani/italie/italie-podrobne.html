Documents, health and travel assistance

What to check before departure

Citizens of the Czech Republic traveling to Italy must prepare a valid identity card or passport. Each child must have their own travel document. Conditions may vary by nationality and route; also check the requirements of the countries you are passing through.

Save the contact to the Czech embassy and the assistance service of the insurance company separately from the documents. If you lose your document, contact the local police and the Czech embassy, ​​who will advise you on the next procedure.

Health and insurance

The European Health Insurance Card helps to obtain medically necessary care in the public system under conditions applicable to local insured persons. It may not cover deductibles, private care or transportation home. Before your trip, consider travel insurance and check its exclusions and mountain activities.

In an emergency, call 112. State the place, what happened and how many people need help. If you are traveling with a dog or cat, check with the veterinarian the identification, the animal's passport and the validity of the rabies vaccination as well as the rules of the transit countries.

By car and van to Italy

Plan your route based on your starting point

From southern Bohemia, a passage through Austria towards Linz and Salzburg is often offered; from Moravia via Vienna or Graz. From western Bohemia, a route through Germany and Austria may make sense. The specific direction depends on the destination, closures and traffic; not every alpine route has the same fees.

Compare distance, travel time and stops. In summer, expect queues at mountain crossings and at the approaches to the sea. For a van, check the height, weight, vehicle category and restrictions on the selected route in advance.

Vignettes, tolls and toll gates

In Austria, check whether you need a motorway vignette and whether a separate toll is paid for a specific mountain section. In Italy, on many motorways, tolls are based on the section driven; some use a barrierless system. The operator of the given section determines the conditions, deadline and method of payment.

If the payment at the gate does not go through, use the service call and follow their instructions. Keep any proof of unpaid toll and resolve the situation with the correct operator. Do not reverse at the toll gate.

Cities, parking and overnight stays

Before entering the historic center, check the local ZTL: border, validity period and any permit. The colors of the parking lines are a useful guide, but the road sign and local rules decide. Ask the accommodation in advance about arrival and parking.

For the van, distinguish between parking and camping. Staying overnight, pulling up chairs or awnings may be subject to local regulations; it is safer to look for a legal parking or campsite and check the conditions in advance. My rebuild plan is open for now.

Trains, ferries and other transport

How to travel by train

It pays to compare long-distance and high-speed connections between major cities; regional trains are suitable for smaller stops. At the station, track the train number, time and platform: partenze means departures, arrivi arrivals, binario platform and ritardo delay.

Conditions vary by carrier and ticket type. The paper ticket may need to be marked before boarding; a digital ticket may have different requirements. Read the instructions for the specific ticket, the rules change. Keep a reserve for the transfer and check the traffic and any strikes before the trip.

Ferry and plane

When traveling by ferry, compare ports and embarkation times. With a car or van, state the actual length, height and possible load when booking; check conditions for gas, animals or later changes directly with the carrier. Upon arrival, find out in advance the connection from the airport and the conditions of possible car rental.

Mountains, nature and national parks

View individual national parks →

Italy offers alpine valleys, the Apennines, a volcanic landscape and a protected coast. The question of whether you want an easy walk, an all-day hike or just a viewpoint accessible by car will help you choose.

Where beyond the countryside

In Valle d'Aosta you can discover the mountain landscape of Gran Paradiso. In Liguria footpaths connect the five coastal villages of Cinque Terre. Gargano in Apulia connects forests and seas; Sicily adds the volcanic landscape of Etna.

Before your hike, check local warnings, weather, access conditions and closures. Choose the route according to your experience, equipment and time until dark. In protected areas, respect the signs, do not disturb the animals and take your rubbish away.

Sea, beaches and islands

The Adriatic offers long stretches of sandy beaches as well as rocky places; the Ligurian and Tyrrhenian coast alternates between bays, cliffs and sand. On the islands, conditions vary even between neighboring beaches. Consider access, shade, traffic and water depth when choosing.

You will find public beaches as well as operated areas with services and equipment rental. Price and availability vary by location and season. Follow the instructions of the lifeguards and the local meaning of the flags; in strong winds or waves, do not enter the water just because the beach is open.

For protected beaches, check reservations, access and local prohibitions in advance. Leave the sand, stones and nature in place. For inspiration, continue to Sardinia, Liguria or Apulia.

Holidays, opening hours and markets

In addition to national holidays, individual cities can celebrate their patron saint. Opening hours therefore vary by municipality, day and season. Smaller shops may have a lunch break; do not automatically count on normal traffic on Sundays or during holidays.

At the weekly market you will find fruit, vegetables, local products and clothes; the indoor market may have a different regime. Check the time and place of the event on the city's website. When buying, ask about the origin and price, for food, consider transporting it in heat.

Traffic restrictions and shop operations may change on the feast of the patron saint. If you are driving to the center, check the parking and closing times on the city's website in advance.

Italian food in practice

Understanding restaurants and the bill

Ristorante, trattoria and osteria may refer to different types of businesses, but you can't tell the quality or the price by the name alone. View the menu before ordering. Coperto is an item associated with the setting, servizio may indicate service; check conditions and prices on site.

A menu typically lists antipasti as starters, primi such as pasta or risotto, secondi as main courses, and contorni as side dishes. You do not have to order every course. Tell the staff about allergies before ordering.

At the market and at the table

Parmigiano Reggiano, pecorino and mozzarella differ in raw material and production. For olive oil, follow the label and origin; you can ask about the composition of the ice cream. When choosing fish, ask whether the price is per piece or by weight. The ingredients and specialties vary according to the region and the time of year.

History, art and local stories

Read a guide to the history of Italy →

Italy was not always a unified state. Ancient monuments, medieval cities, trading republics and later unifications help to understand why individual regions have different traditions and appearance.

Start with the place you want to visit

In Rome the story of the ancient city intertwines with later squares and churches. Siena recalls the time of independent city-states. In Venice the heritage of maritime trade as well as the tradition of carnival masks can be traced.

In Apulia, Alberobello is known for its stone trulli houses. I want to gradually add similar stories to specific places and connect them with the series Roads with a story.

Italian that comes in handy on the go

At the station: Dov'è il binario? – Where is the platform? Il treno è in ritardo? – Is the train late?

In the restaurant: Il conto, per favore. – Bill, please. Sono allergico / allergica a… – I am allergic to…

In case of problems: Ho bisogno di aiuto. – I need help. Ho perso i documenti. – I lost my documents.
