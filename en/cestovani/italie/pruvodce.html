1. Mafia — basic orientation

The documents were verified on 27 October 2026. The year to which they refer is given for the statistics.

The Mafia is not one organization. These are various criminal structures that use intimidation, extortion, corruption and money laundering. Their influence can affect even seemingly ordinary business. INTERPOL — organized crime in Italy.

Which names are worth knowing

  • Cosa Nostra: historically associated with Sicily.
  • ’Ndrangheta: comes from Calabria; its networks also operate beyond the borders of Italy.
  • Camorra: associated with Campania and Naples, made up of various clans.
  • Sacra Corona Unita and other Apulian groups: not a single organization operates in Puglia. DIA also distinguishes groups from Foggia and Bari; Sacra Corona Unita is mainly associated with Salento.

They are areas of origin and historical influence, not the boundaries of their activities or a label for local residents. DIA — 2024 report, English (PDF).

How their influence manifests itself

In addition to the drug trade, it involves extortion, legalization of proceeds and penetration into the economy. The investigation also documented corruption of officials, influencing elections and public contracts. An example is the international crackdown on the ’Ndrangheta network in 2023; the description of a specific case cannot be automatically applied to every business in the region. Europol — the case from Calabria.

What does the state do?

The Direzione Investigativa Antimafia (DIA) coordinates the investigation of mafia structures. Interventions against their property are also an important part of the fight. Message for the year 2024, introduced in May 2025, lists 53 completed investigative operations and 309 measures restricting personal freedom. This is not a statistic for 2026. Home Office — DIA 2024 results.

Prevention also refers to the protection of public investments from infiltration. In July 2026, the Ministry of the Interior published information on the activities of the relevant structure for the period until June 30, 2026. Ministry of the Interior — prevention in 2026.

What does this mean for the visitor?

From the price of the menu, the appearance of the restaurant, or the greeting of the waiter, you would not recognize ties to the mafia. A specific company or person cannot be labeled in this way without supporting documents. At the same time, reports on organized crime are not an assessment of the safety of every street or tourist route.

2. Customs, rules and fines

Verified as of 10/27/2026. Amounts refer to specific locations and violations; seasonal rules need to be verified for the year of visit.

At the table and when greeting

  • Customs are not laws: cappuccino is typical for breakfast, but you can also order it later. Cheese is not usually given to fish pasta, but it depends on the recipe. Spaghetti is usually eaten with a fork; in an informal setting, you can mop up the sauce with bread (fare la scarpetta).
  • Coperto is not a tip or the same as servizio. Check the extra charges and service price in the menu. In Lazio, including Rome, the coperto surcharge is prohibited; being on the menu does not legalize it. Regional Act, Article 77.
  • Tipping is voluntary, with no mandatory percentage. Buongiorno, buonasera or salve are safe polite greetings; ciao is informal, not prohibited in the store.

Clothes

  • In the basilica of St. Petra, everyone must have their shoulders covered. Men should wear long trousers, women trousers or skirts below the knees; shorts and miniskirts are not allowed. For other monuments, check your own visiting regulations. Rules of the Basilica.
  • Dress up outside the beach. Bans on swimsuits or bare torsos are local, not uniform for the whole of Italy. The specific municipal decree and its territorial and temporal validity are decisive.

Beaches and islands

  • Sardinia: removal of sand, pebbles and shells is prohibited (€500-€3,000). Entry to the dunes and access by vehicle to the beaches: €100-€1,000. Region rules.
  • La Pelosa: the official portal for the 2026 season listed reservations from May 15 to October 15 and entry with a QR code. This period is now over. Check the dates, price, rules for children and mandatory equipment for the next season again at official booking website; the terms of 2026 do not automatically apply to 2027. The portal also warns of fake booking pages.
  • Capri: do not assume a general obligation to reserve every beach. Check the rules of the selected beach before your trip current municipal ordinances, especially seasonal traffic restrictions.

Car and transport

  • ZTL: restrictions depend on zone, time and vehicle category. Neither navigation nor hotel reservation is a permit to enter. Confirm with the accommodation in advance the permitted route and the method of registering the license plate number; does not automatically apply to other zones or reserved lanes. Example: Florence. The amount of the penalty depends on the specific violation.
  • Florence — Scudo Verde: it is a broader emission restriction than the historical ZTL. As of July 1, 2025, it includes Euro 0 gasoline vehicles and Euro 0–1 diesels, according to the city's tourism portal. From 1 December 2026 an extension to Euro 1 petrol registered before 1997 and Euro 2 diesel registered before 2001 is announced, with exceptions and conditions specified by the city. For an older car, check the emission category and the validity of the exemption before the trip. City of Florence — ZTL and Scudo Verde.
  • Tolls: on many highways you pay at the gate, but there are also sections without barriers. On Pedemontana Lombardo, unpaid tolls are paid within 15 calendar days. Payment and terms of the operator.

Monuments and public space

  • Rome: do not bathe in the historic fountains or otherwise use them inappropriately. Respect the bans on sitting and eating at monuments, including the Spanish Steps. Sanctions are governed by the specific violation and applicable regulation. City rules.
  • Venice: for prohibited sitting, lying or eating on the ground, bridges, stairs and other listed places, the city reports 100–200 €. For swimming in the canals 350 €, for moving in a swimsuit or with a bare torso 250 €. Some violations also include an immediate vacate order. These are not uniform fines for the whole of Italy. Municipal overview of prohibitions and sanctions.
  • In other cities and museums, follow their visiting rules and signs. Don't transfer fines from Venice to Florence or Verona; damage to a monument may have other legal consequences.

3. Myths — what not to consider as a rule

  • Ordering "un caffè" usually means espresso. The Czech "piccolo" is not a common Italian name for the drink.
  • Cappuccino is typical for breakfast, not forbidden after 11 o'clock. It's a habit, not a health rule about milk digestion.
  • Tipping is voluntary. You determine the height yourself; there is no prescribed maximum or obligation to add a certain percentage.
  • Italian cuisine varies by region and by establishment. Ragù is typically paired with tagliatelle in Bologna; it does not follow that you will not find other combinations anywhere.
  • Bread with olive oil exists in Italy, for example as bruschetta or when tasting the oil. The customs of one restaurant do not apply to the whole country.
  • You can drink espresso while standing or sitting. Prices al banco and al tavolo may vary, but there is no uniform multiple; the price list decides.

5. Types of coffee — a complete overview

"Caffè" in Italy = espresso. Period. If you want anything else, you have to specify it.

Coffee (Espresso)

Strong, concentrated, 25-30 ml. The basis of everything. It will be drunk in 30 seconds at the bar.

Caffè doppio

Double espresso. 50-60 ml. For a stronger morning start.

Caffè ristretto

"Drained" espresso — even less water, even stronger. 15-20 ml.

Caffè lungo

"Long" espresso — more water, weaker. 50 ml. Closer to filtered coffee.

Coffee macchiato

Espresso "tainted" with milk. A drop of milk on top. You can anytime.

Latte macchiato

On the contrary — a glass of warm milk, "tainted" with espresso. Layered.

Cappuccino

Espresso + whipped milk + foam (approx. 1:1:1). Typically for breakfast, but no prohibition at other times.

Caffè americano

Espresso topped with hot water. Weaker, similar to filter coffee.

Caffè corretto

Espresso "improved" with a shot of grappa or sambuka. After lunch / dinner.

Moroccan

From Piedmont. Espresso + cocoa + foam + more cocoa on top. Lighter in taste.

Caffè shakerato

Summer version. Espresso shaken with ice in a shaker, icy, foamy.

Caffè freddo

Cold espresso, often sweetened. In a thermos in the bar, it is poured into a glass.

Caffè decaffeinated

Coffee with reduced caffeine content, colloquially "deca". It doesn't have to be completely decaffeinated.

Coffee ginseng

A drink based on coffee and ginseng, often from a pre-sweetened mixture. The composition varies by product.

How to order — a practical walkthrough

  1. Enter the bar. "Buongiorno!" service.
  2. Decide: table or bar? Bar and table service prices may vary. Check the price list before ordering.
  3. Order: "Un caffè, per favore" (= espresso). Or "Un cappuccino e un cornetto, per favore" (= cappuccino and croissant).
  4. Salary: at a bar, you often first pay at the register, get a receipt (scontrino), and go to the barista with it.
  5. Drink up. Italians can do it in 30-60 seconds. "Grazia, arriveder!" and you go

6. Safety — pickpockets, scams, how to protect yourself

Italy is generally a safe country. The main risk for tourists is petty thieves and fraudsters, not violent crime. Here is a list of what you will actually encounter.

Places with the highest risk of pickpockets

  • Rome: Termini (main station), metro lines A and B, Colosseum, Roman Forum, Trevi, Piazza Navona, Spanish Steps, Vatican line, buses 40 a 64 (Termini → Vatican) are famous.
  • Florence: Ponte Vecchio, Santa Maria Novella train station, San Lorenzo market, Duomo.
  • Venice: San Marco in seasons, vaporetto lines 1 and 2, narrow streets with no view.
  • Naples: Garibaldi metro station, the area around Spaccanapoli, coastal promenades. Watch out for motorbikes — the well-known "thief Scippo" (he snatches the purse while driving).
  • Milan: Duomo + surroundings, metro line 1 (especially to San Babila).

The most common scams — how they work

Fake "helpers" at ticket machines

Termini, Milan Centrale, wherever. Someone willingly offers you "help" with the ticket machine, drags you into buying an unnecessary surcharge, copies the card, or keeps you busy while a colleague cleans your pockets.

Defense: seek help from the marked cash desk or verified staff. Do not entrust your card or PIN to strangers.

Rose / friendship bracelet

Someone near the tourist attractions will "give" you a rose or put a bracelet on your hand. Then he wants €10-20 "for a gift". When you refuse, he makes a scene.

Defense: don't touch When they offer you something, hands in your pockets, "well, grazie" and you move on.

Petition / map

A woman or child comes to you with a petition sheet or a folded map ("Help me!"). The map covers the view of your bag, a colleague steals from behind. Don't get distracted by a group of people and watch your luggage.

Defense: sharp "no!", leave, don't open your purse.

Crowd at the subway door

When the subway doors are closing, several people "coincidentally" push you to the exit. They pick your pockets in the chaos.

Defense: a bag in front of the body, hands on zippers. If you feel the pressure, loudly say "ATTENTO!" — the locals will understand, the scammers will withdraw.

Restaurant — undelivered bill, fake items

At tourist attractions: after the meal they will bring you a "menu fisso" bill without detail. Or they add "wine" that you didn't order. Or they calculate prices from an unupdated sheet.

Defense: you always are request an account ("il conto, per favore") and check the lines. Check the service according to the menu; in Lazio, coperto may not be charged, other items must match the order.

Taxi from the airport — "invalid taxi meter"

"Taxi" in front of Fiumicino or Capodichino airport says "taxi meter not working, price €80 to the center". The real taxi fare Fiumicino → Rome is fixed €55 at the city of Rome taxi for the trip inside the Aurelian Walls. Before driving, check the conditions in the current airport overview of tariffs.

Defense: just official white taxi with the city number and logo (TAXI ROMA / TAXI MILANO etc.). They stand in line in the designated stall. Say "tariffa fissa, per favore" before boarding — fixed tariff.

Fake cops

Someone shows you a "badge" and asks to check your passport or wallet because of "counterfeit money in the area". They'll give you everything back, but a few bills are missing.

Defense: Ask for proof of identity. If you suspect fraud, call 112 and verify the situation; do not hand over cash to an unverified person.

Practical behavior — checklist

  • ✅ Bag in front of the body, head strap, front zipper.
  • ✅ Wallet NEVER in your back pocket. Inner pocket, waist pocket under the shirt, money belt.
  • ✅ Don't put your phone on the table in a restaurant by the street. There is a threat of rapid alienation.
  • ✅ Two wallets: one "operational" (little money, possible victim), the other main (hidden).
  • ✅ Copies of documents in email / cloud. A copy does not replace a valid travel document; keep documents safely stored and available for inspection.
  • ✅ Insurance abroad — solve with the bank / insurance company.
  • ✅ 112 — a single European emergency line for a real emergency.
  • ❌ Never let strangers into a hotel room "for maintenance". Check with reception.
  • ❌ ATM only inside the bank or in publicly monitored places. Never in an abandoned alley — skimmer risk.

When it happens

  1. 112 — immediate emergency line.
  2. Loud announcement (denuncia) to the nearest police station. Deposit confirmation for the insurance company; the conditions of fulfillment are determined by your policy. The station is referred to as questura (state) or caserma dei carabinieri (Gendarmes).

The route, fees and rules depend on the starting point, vehicle and date. Before departure, check the current information for the entire route.

Depending on the destination, you can compare the route via Brenner or via Villach and Tarvisio. Navigation, closures and weather decide the specific route; include all countries traveled in the calculation.

Czech and Austrian stamp

Before entering tolled sections, check the conditions for your vehicle and license plate. At ASFINAG, when purchased online by a consumer, there are restrictions on the start of validity for the two-month and one-year digital stamp; one-day and ten-day can pay immediately. Check the terms of purchase.

Separate Austrian toll

Some Austrian sections have special tolls, for example the A13 Brenner or the tunnels on the A10. Check what you need on each part of the route, including the approach sections.

Italian highways and tolls without barriers

On parts of the highways, a ticket is collected at the entrance and paid at the exit; elsewhere, a different system may work, including passage without barriers. Check the method of payment and any deadline according to the specific operator.

Cities, ZTL and parking

ZTL are restricted entry zones. Times, exceptions and brand registration are determined by the specific city. A hotel reservation does not in itself constitute permission; check the procedure with the accommodation and the city.

More ideas for a trip to Italy

Detailed guide: documents, transport, nature and culture →

Cities, coasts and countryside can be combined into one trip. The following tips will help with the selection of stops and practical preparation.

Trips around the area

From Siena you can continue through the Val d'Orcia countryside or visit Pisa. From Lerici there is a walk to San Terenza and other trips along the bay. In Venice, leave time for the islands of the lagoon; water connections follow the timetable.

Coast and islands

Liguria offers a rugged coastline and villages connected by train. In Sardinia and Sicily expect longer distances; check access, parking and local rules before choosing a beach. Water transport is convenient for the lagoon islands in Venice.

Trains and ferries

For the train, compare the carrier, the length of the journey and the conditions of the specific ticket. According to the carrier, the Trenitalia digital regional ticket is automatically activated upon scheduled departure; other tickets may have different rules. For ships, check the port, season, car transport and any weather changes in advance.

National parks and mountains

Cinque Terre can be explored by train or on foot, but trails may be closed or subject to fees. In the mountains, monitor the weather, route length, elevation gain and season; check the condition of the roads with the administration of the respective park.

Markets, holidays and beaches

Markets are held on different days and opening hours may vary on holidays; check the date on the municipality's website. At the beach, distinguish between freely accessible sections and paid facilities. In Sardinia, leave the sand, pebbles and shells in place and do not enter the dunes.
