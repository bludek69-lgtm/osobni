National Parks of Italy

Mountains, forests, volcanoes and islands: overview of individual parks and connections with regions. Check with the relevant park administration for trails, entrances, weather and safety rules before traveling.

Gran Paradiso

High mountain environment between Valle d'Aosta and Piedmont. The park is associated with ibex conservation; mountain villages are the starting point for trips of various lengths.
