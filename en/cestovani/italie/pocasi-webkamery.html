Sources and notes

Weather: data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), free and without an API key. Values are fetched directly in your browser and refreshed about every ten minutes. This is a forecast model, not an official warning — before you travel, also check Aeronautica Militare (meteoam.it).

Webcams: the streams are run by third parties (SkylineWebcams, I Love You Venice, Webcam Italia, Il Mattino, Espansione TV, RAMSAT, Vatican News, Paesaggi Digitali, Roma Five Suites) and shown with the official YouTube player. The player loads only after you click, so YouTube learns nothing about you until you press play. If a camera is not broadcasting at the moment, use the “Source” link.

The webcams are not mine and I have no control over them; availability of the streams may change.
