Notes on the trip

Venice cannot be described in one way. Piazza San Marco, Palazzo Ducale, Rialto Bridge — that's the tourist layer. Below it is a city of loitering gondolas, narrow streets where you get lost every day, and quiet neighborhoods (Cannaregio, Castello) where the Venetians really live. Burano with colorful houses and Murano with the glass workshops is worth a separate day.
