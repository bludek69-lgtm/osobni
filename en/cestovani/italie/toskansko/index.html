Florence, Val d'Orcia & Lerici

Travel memories: Florence and Siena, Val d’Orcia, thermal springs and Lerici in neighbouring Liguria, where I lived. A familiar part of Italy, not just a tourist destination.

A few words

Tuscany means more to me than cypress-covered hills: Florence, medieval Siena, Val d’Orcia and the thermal springs each have their own atmosphere. This page also includes Lerici, where I lived — in Liguria, on the Gulf of La Spezia, near Tuscany.
