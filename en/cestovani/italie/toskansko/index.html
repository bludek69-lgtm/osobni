🇮🇹 Italy · Tuscany

Florence, Val d'Orcia & Siena

Travel memories: Florence and Siena, Val d’Orcia, thermal springs and natural pools. A familiar part of Italy, not just a tourist destination.

A few words

Tuscany means more to me than cypress-covered hills: Florence, medieval Siena, Val d’Orcia and the thermal springs each have their own atmosphere. Lerici, where I spent some time, is in neighbouring Liguria — see the Liguria page.

Siena beyond the photograph

My Siena gallery is paired with a full walking video ↗. Piazza del Campo is a natural point of orientation. The official Visit Tuscany guide describes the Palazzo Pubblico and its civic museum, the Torre del Mangia and the Fonte Gaia around the square. There is more to see here than one panoramic frame.

How I would explore my archive: Siena's streets first, then the landscape of Val d’Orcia, with Florence as a separate stop. The photos come from past trips, so this is not a fixed one-day itinerary. Check current museum access and reservations with the operators.
