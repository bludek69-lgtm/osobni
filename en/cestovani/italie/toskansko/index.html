Notes on the trip

For me, Tuscany is more than hills with cypresses: Florence, medieval Siena, the landscape of Val d'Orcia and the thermal springs each have a different atmosphere. The town of Lerici, where I stayed for some time, is located in neighboring Liguria — you can find it at Liguria page.

Siena in context

Mine completes Siena's gallery main video from the city walk ↗. Its natural reference point is Piazza del Campo. According to the official Visit Tuscany, the square houses the Palazzo Pubblico with the city museum and the Torre del Mangia tower; on the opposite side is the Fonte Gaia. This gives a visit to the square more than one photogenic shot.

How I would browse my own archive: first Siena and its streets, then the landscape of Val d'Orcia and finally Florence on its own. The photos are from older trips, so I'm not making them into a fixed one-day itinerary. For museums and viewpoints, check the current access on the official website.
