Notes on the trip

Rome is a city where you can't see everything in a week — let alone a day. The Vatican and the Basilica of St. Peter, Colosseum, Pantheon, Roman Forum, Trevi Fountain. Add to that the streets of Trastevere, Roman cuisine (cacio e pepe, carbonara, supplì) and after a few days you will understand why it is "eternal".
