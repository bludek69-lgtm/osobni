Matera

Explore the area

Explore viewpoints over Sasso Caveoso and Sasso Barisano and walk up to the cathedral. The Sassi and the surrounding rock churches are recognised by UNESCO. Streets and steps can be steep; check access before exploring Murgia Materana across the ravine.

Before you go

Check opening times, access, transport and weather for your travel dates.
