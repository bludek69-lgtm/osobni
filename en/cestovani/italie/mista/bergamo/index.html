Italy / Lombardy / Bergamo Alta

ITALY · LOMBARDY

Bergamo Alta

Upper Bergamo lies above the newer part of the city and its historic streets are protected by the city walls.

On this page: What to see · The story of the place · Excursions and transport

What to see

Piazza Vecchia and Palazzo della Ragione form the heart of the upper town. Walk through the surrounding streets and, depending on the time, find a view of the lower town.

The story of the place

The historic fortifications recall the period of Venetian administration and are part of the wider UNESCO heritage of Venetian defensive buildings.

Excursions and transport

The connection to the lower part is ensured, among other things, by a ropeway. When traveling by car, check the current entry and parking restrictions.

Before visiting, check the operation, entry conditions and availability according to the season.

Flavors of the region

Milanese risotto with saffron and mountain cheeses show different sides of Lombard cuisine.
