Rome

Rome combines ancient monuments with lively squares. The Colosseum, the Roman Forum and the Palatine form an archaeological park; check the entrances before visiting.

What will you see here?

Rome combines the monuments of antiquity with later squares, churches and city life. The Colosseum and the Roman Forum are located in the archaeological part of the city.

How to schedule your visit

For a longer visit, it makes sense to divide the center into several walks. Check entrances and traffic restrictions directly with the administrators of monuments and the city.
