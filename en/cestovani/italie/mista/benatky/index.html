Venice

The city on the lagoon offers St Mark's Square, canals and less busy neighbourhoods. Before you travel, check the entry and transport rules.

What will you see here?

Venice is made up of islands connected by bridges and canals. The most famous places include St. Mark's Square, the Rialto Bridge and the Grand Canal.

How to schedule your visit

You can walk around the city or use water transport. Before visiting, check access rules, prices and current conditions with the city and carrier.
