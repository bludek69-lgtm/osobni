ITALY · ABRUZZO

L'Aquila

The mountain town of L'Aquila is the starting point for exploring the interior of Abruzzo. The historic center, the surrounding mountains and the proximity of Gran Sassa allow you to combine a city walk with a trip to nature.

On this page: What to see · The story of the place · Excursions and transport

What to see

Start at Piazza del Duomo and continue through the historic streets. Fontana delle 99 Cannelle recalls the traditions of the city; schedule your tour according to the really accessible places.

The story of the place

The city was severely affected by the 2009 earthquake and the restoration of buildings continues. The shape of the streets and the accessibility of monuments can therefore change.

Excursions and transport

You can continue into the mountain landscape towards Gran Sasso and Rocca Calascio. Altitude and weather differences are important when planning.

Before visiting, check the operation, entry conditions and availability according to the season.

Flavors of the region

Lamb skewers arrosticini and pasta alla chitarra show the fusion of pastoral tradition and home cooking.
