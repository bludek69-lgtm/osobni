ITALY · APULIA

Alberobello

In the interior of Apulia lies a city known for its stone trulli houses. Their light-colored walls and conical roofs create the characteristic appearance of the local neighborhoods.

On this page: Trulli houses · Quarters and a walk · Surroundings and transport

What are trulli

A trullo is a traditional building made of local limestone. Its shape is typical of a stone roof laid out in a cone; the buildings were used for living and as a background for farming.

The ensemble of trulli in Alberobello is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The protected area includes the districts of Monti and Aia Piccola as well as several individual sites.

Quarters and a walk

Rione Monti offers a dense network of lanes between the trulli. The second historic area is Aia Piccola; when visiting keep in mind that some of the houses are still inhabited.

Among the notable individual buildings is the Trullo Sovrano with a floor. Check access and opening hours directly with the administrator before your visit.

Surroundings and transport

Alberobello is one of the stops in Valle d'Itria. The route can be connected to the towns of Locorotondo or Martina Franca; compare distances and current public connections in advance.

When arriving by car, check parking and any local entry restrictions. Comfortable shoes are good for the stone streets.

Flavors of Puglia

In the region, you can taste orecchiette, local olive oil or focaccia. Primitivo di Manduria wine is associated with another part of Puglia; follow the label for a specific bottle.
