Italy / Sicily / Palermo

ITALY · SICILY

Palermo

Palermo is the capital of Sicily and a place where historical monuments, markets and everyday city life meet.

On this page: What to see · The story of the place · Excursions and transport

What to see

The cathedral in the photo can be included in a walk through the center. Depending on the time, you can continue to Quattro Canti square and the area of ​​the Norman palace.

The story of the place

The city's architecture reflects several historical periods and cultural influences. Individual monuments have their own visiting regime.

Excursions and transport

From Palermo you can continue to Monreale or along the coast towards Cefalù. For trips around the island, expect longer distances.

Before visiting, check the operation, entry conditions and availability according to the season.

Flavors of the region

Arancini, cannoli and dishes with pistachios are among the well-known Sicilian specialties.
