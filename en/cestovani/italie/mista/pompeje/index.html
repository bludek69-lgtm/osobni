Pompeii

The archaeological site near Vesuvius offers a view of a city preserved after the eruption. Check times, tickets and accessible buildings on the park’s official website.

What you can see

Pompeii was a Roman city beneath Vesuvius. The archaeological park preserves ancient streets and remains of homes, shops and public buildings.

Planning your visit

The site is extensive and paths can be uneven. Comfortable shoes and water are useful; check admission and opening hours with the park.
