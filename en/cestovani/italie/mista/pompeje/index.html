Pompeii

The archaeological area under Vesuvius offers a view of the city preserved after the eruption. Check times, tickets and accessible houses on the park's official website.

What will you see here?

Pompeii was a Roman city below Vesuvius. The archaeological area allows you to walk through ancient streets and the remains of houses, shops and public buildings.

How to schedule your visit

The area is extensive and the tour leads over an uneven surface. Comfortable shoes and water are good for the trip; check entrance and opening hours with the park administration.
