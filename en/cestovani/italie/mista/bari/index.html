Bari

Explore the area

Bari Vecchia connects the Basilica of Saint Nicholas, the cathedral and the waterfront. The basilica holds relics of Saint Nicholas; respect services and visiting rules. Alberobello and the Itria Valley make a separate trip: check current train and bus timetables.
