Naples

Explore the area

Walk through the historic centre to the Church of Gesù Nuovo, then explore Piazza del Plebiscito and the waterfront. Naples preserves layers of history from antiquity onward. Pompeii, Vesuvius and trips across the bay require separate planning and current access checks.

Before you go

Check opening times, access, transport and weather for your travel dates.
