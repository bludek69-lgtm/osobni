Siena

Piazza del Campo sits at the heart of the historic centre. A walk can continue towards the cathedral and old streets.

What you can see

Piazza del Campo has a fan shape and is bordered by Palazzo Pubblico and Torre del Mangia. Historic streets climb towards the Cathedral of Santa Maria Assunta.

Planning your visit

Divide a walk between the square, cathedral and surrounding lanes. The historic centre has traffic restrictions; check parking and access for your destination.
