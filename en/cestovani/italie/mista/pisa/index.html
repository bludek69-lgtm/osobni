Pisa

Piazza dei Miracoli combines the leaning tower, the cathedral and the baptistery. Check entrances and availability with the grounds manager.

What will you see here?

Piazza dei Miracoli is home to the cathedral, the baptistery and the bell tower known as the Leaning Tower. Distinctive white buildings are part of the historical area.

How to schedule your visit

To visit the tower, check the entry conditions and availability in advance. The walk can be supplemented by the streets towards the Arno River.
