Lerici

Lerici is located in Liguria by the Gulf of Golfo dei Poeti. I stayed for some time in Lerici; the coastal promenade leads to San Terenza.

What will you see here?

Lerici is located on the eastern side of the Gulf of Golfo dei Poeti. A castle stands above the coast and a promenade connects the coastal part with nearby San Terence.

How to schedule your visit

Views of the bay can be enjoyed from the harbor and promenade. For boat connections and parking, check seasonal traffic and local rules in advance.
