Aquileia

Aquileia, in the lowlands near the Adriatic, preserves the remains of a Roman city and an important basilica complex.

On this page: What to see · The story of the place · Excursions and transport

What to see

The dominant feature is the Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta with its extensive mosaic floor. Archaeological remains are also located outside the basilica itself.

The story of the place

Roman Aquileia was one of the important cities of the region. UNESCO protects the archaeological site together with the patriarchal basilica.

Excursions and transport

The visit can be combined with the lagoon city of Grado or with Trieste. Check the access to individual parts of the site in advance.

Before visiting, check the operation, entry conditions and availability according to the season.

Flavors of the region

Frico is a dish based on cheese and, in various modifications, potatoes. A well-known product of the area is San Daniele ham.
