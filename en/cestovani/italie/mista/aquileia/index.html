Aquileia

Explore the area

The Basilica of Santa Maria Assunta contains an extensive mosaic floor; Roman archaeological remains extend beyond the church. UNESCO recognises the archaeological area and basilica. Grado and Trieste are possible onward stops; check access to individual sites.
