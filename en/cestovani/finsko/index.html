Notes on the trip

Helsinki is a capital city that is in no rush. Neoclassical architecture on Senate Square, Art Nouveau blocks along the harbor and a modern library Oodi — everything within walking distance. Ferries to Tallinn and Stockholm dock at the port, making Helsinki a logical junction for traveling around Northern Europe.

Outside the city, the Finnish landscape is as one imagines it to be: lakes, forests, red wooden cottages and immense silence.

"Finland is a country where silence has its own voice."
