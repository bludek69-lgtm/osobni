Notes on the trip

St. Petersburg is a city of contrasts — baroque palaces next to Soviet monuments, the Neva under the sky, which almost never stops shining in August. Walks along the Nevsky Prospekt, the Hermitage, the Peter and Paul Fortress, the Church of the Savior on Blood — everything so big that it will remain in the memory more as a feeling than as a precise itinerary.

Peterhof is a separate chapter. Czar's summer residence outside the city with a park full of cascades and fountains. When you get close to the big cascade and at high noon all the jets go off at once, you will understand why the place is called the "Russian Versailles".

"St. Petersburg isn't about what you get to see — it's about what amazes you as soon as you step off the bus."
