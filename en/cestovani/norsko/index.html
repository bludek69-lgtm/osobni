Notes from the trip

Photographs from a coach journey in July 2018, grouped by the places visited: Lillehammer, the Lofoten Islands, Vesterålen, Helgeland, northern Norway and Oslo. The route also passed through Abisko in Swedish Lapland.

What makes Norway special to me

In the Lofoten Islands, mountains rise straight out of the sea. The views at Reine and Hamnøy, with red fishing cabins beneath the ridges, are only the beginning. Vesterålen and Helgeland feel more open and quieter; the coastal road, Kystriksveien, is one of the most beautiful in Europe.

In the far north, the sun does not set in summer. Even at two in the morning it is light, and the landscape takes on remarkable colours. I remember that whenever winter darkness falls at four in the afternoon back in Czechia.

“You cannot capture the Lofoten Islands in a photograph — reality always wins.”
