What makes Norway Norway

Lofoten — an archipelago where the mountains jut right into the sea. Classic postcard scenery from Reine a Hamnøy (red fishing huts rorbuer against ridges) are just the beginning. In Vesterål and Helgeland, the landscape is more open and quiet — the coastal road Kystriksveien it is one of the most beautiful in Europe.

The sun does not set in the north of Norway in the summer. A polar day means that at two o'clock in the morning it is still clear — the landscape takes on strange colors that cannot be described. I remember that every time I watch it get dark at four in the afternoon in the Czech Republic in the winter.

"Lofoten can't be photographed — you try to catch them, but reality always wins."
