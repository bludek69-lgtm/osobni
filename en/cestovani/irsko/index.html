Notes on the trip

Ireland did not get the mood from the photos — the landscape there is exactly as one imagines it. Endless pastures divided by low stone walls, sheep almost everywhere, abbey ruins in the fields and the sea that always appears where you least expect it.

The journey begins with a ferry — the best way to get into the Irish mood. Slowly, coffee in hand, across the Irish Sea. Then when you disembark on land, you have the feeling that time has slowed down a bit.

"Even the rain has its own tune in Ireland."
