Amalfi: from port to cathedral and the story of the compass

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 minutes of reading
Video · 6:40 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

From the boat, Amalfi looks like a city centered between the sea and the hillside. After arriving, the main direction becomes the road to the square and the cathedral. The video follows this transformation and at the end adds a seemingly small monument whose story leads to the history of navigation.

From the port to the square

The beginning of the film consists of the cruise and the own photographs, then comes the approach to the coast. It is useful to perceive them together: the general view will show the location of the city, the pedestrian part will reveal its real passages and distances. The stairs to the cathedral then give the visit a completely different scale than the distant silhouette from the sea.

At the square, the road naturally slows down. In addition to the main building, the surroundings, the street entrances and the way people move between the coast and the center are also worth paying attention to. These transitions tend to be more memorable from a short stop than a single detail of the facade.

Flavio Gioia and the story of the compass

By the sea stands a monument to Flavius Gioii, a figure traditionally associated with the invention of the compass. However, the official Visit Amalfi points out that this attribution was the result of a misinterpretation of an older manuscript. Local tradition must thus be distinguished from documented authorship of the invention.

However, this does not mean that Amalfi's relationship with navigation is disappearing. The same source mentions the important role here in the use of the magnetic needle at sea. The monument is therefore a good reason to stop at the question of "who invented it" and at how the port city remembers its maritime past.

For your own trip, check the current boat connection and return time, as well as the conditions of the visit at the cathedral. If you don't want to rush the program, choose a smaller part of the coast and leave time to stay in the city. Both sailing and walking need their own space.

It offers another view of the same coastal environment Positano. When comparing the two videos, you can notice how the relationship of the houses to the slope changes, the approach from the sea and the way from the port to the most prominent monument.
