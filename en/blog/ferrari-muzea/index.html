Ferrari in Modena and Maranello: two views of one story

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 minutes of reading

Workshop, engines, road cars, racing history and finally Fiorano. The 20-minute video from July 2023 connects two different Ferrari museums: Modena and Maranello. The article helps to distinguish their roles and to choose the part in the film that you want to return to.

Modena and Maranello are not one museum

The official tourist portal VisitModena presents the Museo Enzo Ferrari in Modena in relation to the founder of the brand and his life. The Maranell Museum emphasizes automotive and sports history. So when planning, it's not just about two buildings of the same tour in one place: you need to distinguish between two cities, two entrances and time to move.

In the video, the Modena part begins with a historical workshop and a museum pavilion. The exposed engines then turn the attention from the exterior shape of the car to the technology inside. It is interesting to compare what the body reveals about the car and what the mechanical part displayed separately shows.

Car names as orientation in the record

The video description includes chapters dedicated to, among others, the 408 4RM, F40, FXX, Purosangue and later the F50, Enzu or LaFerrari. These names serve here as a guide to the specific recorded footage. They do not mean that all the mentioned cars are waiting for the visitor in today's exhibition: museums vary their exhibitions and the selection of exhibits.

Maranello opens at 7:48 on the record. Along with the cars come trophies, formulas and a simulator, so the focus also shifts to the racing environment. The final part at Fiorano then connects the museum story with the place of development and testing.

Find your own chapter

  • 1:22 - Experimental 408 4RM
  • 5:02 - historic workshop and engines
  • 7:48 – Museo Ferrari Maranello
  • 19:32 - Fiorano test track

Before visiting, check the current exhibitions, tickets and any additional programs. A visit to the museum does not automatically mean entry into production or the test track. For each service, it is necessary to find out its specific conditions.

If you want to enjoy the movie without knowing the models, focus on the rotation of the workshop, freely displayed parts and finished cars. It is this continuity that shows that behind the resulting shape lies the technical work and the people whose story the museums commemorate.
