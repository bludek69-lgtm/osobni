Greece: mountain villages, bridges and the road to Chios

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 6:07 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Stone bridges in the mountains, villages with distinctive architecture, a city surrounded by fortifications and ornate houses on Chios. The Greek part of Hidden Gems connects five sites that show the country from different sides: Zagori, Nymfaio, Dimitsana, Monemvasia and Pyrgi.

The selection leads across the mainland to the island. For your own travel, it is therefore more useful to divide it into areas than to try to connect all the stops in a short trip. A common theme is the settlement's relationship to the landscape and the way people used stone, water and the natural terrain.

Zagori and Nymfaio: Greece on a mountain scale

Zagori represents a group of mountain settlements and a landscape of stone bridges. The bridge here is not just an object for photography. It reminds of the need to cross a river or ravine and connect places that naturally separate the terrain from each other. When watching, the area around the building itself is also worth paying attention to.

Official Visit Greece describes the local stone architecture and the connection of the villages with the mountain landscape. The same first part of the selection includes Nymfaio, another option to look at Greece through a mountain residence. For both places, it is a good idea to leave time for walking and changes in the weather in the program, rather than counting only the arrival by car.

Dimitsana: water as part of the story

Dimitsana in Arcadia shifts the focus from the bridges to the mansion and its grounds. The stone building belongs to the mountain landscape, but the story of the area is also related to the use of water. Tourist Board of Greece reminiscent of mills and other operations based on water power.

The map to the video therefore also contains an expansion point, the Museum of Water Power near Dimitsana. If you are interested in technical aspects, you can extend your stay in this direction. It is not necessary to perceive each village in the same way: somewhere the main theme will be a walk, elsewhere the history of work.

Monemvasia and Pyrgi: entering another environment

Monemvasia is a walled city on a prominent rock formation connected to the mainland by a narrow approach. His character helps to understand the very arrival. Beyond the walls continues a different world of streets and houses than that suggested by the distant silhouette of the rock. He explains the division into upper and lower town official overview of Monemvasia.

Pyrgi on Chios offers a final contrast: attention is focused on decorated houses. From a large landscape scale, the narrative reaches the detail of the building's surface. For the island part of the trip, it is also necessary to plan the connection with Chios, not only the transfer between the points drawn on the map.

How to choose your own stops

Start with what you expect from the trip. For a mountain residence and landscape choose the northern part of the selection, for the Peloponnese Dimitsana or Monemvasia, for an island stay Pyrgi. Each area can be a separate journey and does not require visiting all five locations.

Map to video contains both main stops and additional destinations. Before visiting, check the transport, opening hours of the selected objects and the form of access. The video can help with the choice of direction; it is convenient to adapt the specific route and timetable to the actual date and your own pace.
