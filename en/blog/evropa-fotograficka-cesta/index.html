A photographic journey through Europe: cities, mountains and coasts

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 35 min · YouTube will load after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Historic cities, the Alps, the Nordic coast and a bus trip. Photographic journey through Europe combines images from Luďká cesta into a longer narrative about places and movements between them. You can follow it as a whole or return to specific countries through chapters.

The selection starts in the Czech Republic and continues through Central and Western Europe to the north, to the Baltics and then south again. It is not a record of a single continuous expedition or a proposal of a current itinerary. The individual photos resemble different paths, and the video connects them into a common image.

From familiar home to other cities

The introduction is reminiscent of traveling by bus, then comes the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Poland and Germany. Among the examples mentioned in the video are Český Krumlov and Berlin. Photographs of cities can be read in different ways: some people look for a well-known landmark, others notice the scale of a square, street or normal traffic.

The comparison can be more interesting than the list of visited countries. As the different urban spaces connect to each other, differences stand out that would easily disappear when viewing the album separately. Start in the Czech Republic from 0:18 ↗

The Alps and the way to the west

Austria and Switzerland bring a more pronounced mountain theme. Across urban areas, distances and the way of orientation change: valleys, ridges and the relationship of buildings to the landscape are important. A photo can show the big picture, but also recall a specific place where it stopped along the way.

France, the Netherlands, Scotland, England and Ireland follow. The video mentions the Scottish countryside and the Irish coast, so the urban and natural motifs continuously alternate. Switzerland from 11:12 ↗ · Scotland from 16:25 ↗

Ferry, the North and the Baltics

A separate chapter on the ferry journey reminds us that the transfer can be part of the experience. Norway brings, among other things, fjords, followed by Sweden, Finland, Estonia and Latvia. A long photographic narrative allows us to perceive the gradual changes in the environment.

With such a memory trip, there is no need to immediately change each place to an item of the future program. Sometimes it is enough to choose a motif that attracts you: the coast, urban architecture or the relationship between transport and the landscape. Only then can it become a topic for your own trip. Norway from 21:16 ↗

Back to the South and an Italian ending

After the Russian chapter come Hungary, Slovenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Italy and the Vatican. Among the named stops is Mostar. The conclusion thus connects other types of cities and landscapes and returns the narrative towards the Mediterranean.

Each country has its own time slot in the video. So you don't have to start again from the first minute if you want to return to a certain part. Bosnia and Herzegovina from 29:30 ↗ · Italy from 31:45 ↗

A photographic story and personal travel

Map overview helps with orientation in the countries that the video represents. It is an overview of the photographic narrative, not a navigation with the exact locations of all the images.

For further viewing, you can go to travel galleries and choose a specific area. If a motive leads you to your own journey, plan it according to current conditions and available transport. But the value of these photographs is also simpler: they remind us that the journey is not only about the destination, but also about the connection between places and what remains in the memory after returning.
