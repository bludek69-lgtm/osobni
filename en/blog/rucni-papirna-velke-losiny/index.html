Velké Losiny: how rags became paper

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 6:14 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

A sheet of paper seems like an obvious thing until you start to see how much work goes into its creation. In Velké Losiny, the story of paper meets several centuries of craftsmanship. The video Journeys with a story follows the journey from textile raw material to the finished sheet, as well as the fate of a paper mill that was able to sustain itself.

The narrative is based on the Losinsk paper mill and the Paper Museum. Photos of other European paper mills also show some of the production procedures; therefore, not every production frame in the video captures Velké Losiny. Looking at the whole process is more useful than looking for a single moment in which the paper "comes into existence".

From grain mill to paper mill

The history of the company begins at the end of the 16th century. According to the administrator, Jan the Younger from Žerotín had a paper mill built on the site of the former grain mill. The start of production falls between 1591 and 1596, and the oldest known slip dates from 1596. The slip is a preserved trace of production that gives the given date a specific meaning.

The following centuries brought changes of owners, changes in technology and difficult times. An overview of these contexts is published Velké Losiny Hand Paper Mill itself. Historical part of the video from 1:08 ↗

Rags, water and paper

The title of the video recalls the time when old textiles were the source of fibers. The path to paper therefore began with the raw material and its preparation. The remains of the material are not yet a sheet: the fibers need to be processed and dispersed in water so that a new layer can be formed from them.

Here it is worthwhile to put aside the idea of today's common office roll of paper. It is interesting to observe the transformation of the material: something that has already served as a textile is given a different purpose. In doing so, the water serves as an environment in which the fibers can break down and rejoin. Rags and water from 2:13 ↗

From paper stock to single sheet

The next chapter describes drawing the sheet. In manual production, a long web is not created in the same way as on an industrial paper machine. Attention is focused on the individual sheet and on the follow-up work, during which the water must be gradually removed from the material.

To monitor the process, it is good to ask simple questions: what holds the fibers together in a given step, where does the water go, and when can the leaf be handled? Such a view combines the individual devices into a comprehensible procedure. From paper to sheet from 3:24 ↗

A craft that continues

The conclusion is devoted to difficult times, a comparison with the paper mill in Doubrava and the present Losin. The meaning of the preserved manufactory is not only in the age of the buildings. It is also a continuation of skills that one can understand only by looking at the work of the hands and the context of the individual steps.

Sightseeing circuit according to of the operator's current overview it combines the museum with a look at the production and lasts approximately one hour. Check the specific date and availability in advance, as this is an organized guided visit.

Map to video will direct you to Velké Losiny. During your visit, try to follow the entire chain of work: from raw materials to wet paper to a sheet on which you could write a letter. It is this way that gives a new meaning to a seemingly ordinary material.
