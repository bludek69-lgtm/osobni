With the band to Germany: a bus driver’s memories from 1998

Luděk · Cestovatel6922. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 8:11 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗

In May 1998 I drove the Turnovanka brass band by coach on a tour to Lower Saxony, near Hanover. The camera travelled with us. Twenty-eight years later I digitised the tape, gently restored it and edited it into a short documentary.

Father’s Day and the Onkel Nickel guesthouse

We stayed at the timber-framed Onkel Nickel guesthouse. On Thursday 21 May 1998 Germany celebrated Father’s Day (Vatertag), which falls on Ascension Day. Doctors from a Hanover hospital welcomed us at the station in bowler hats and top hats, and the band took out their instruments straight away. In the evening I sat with the owner of the guesthouse while young soldiers at the next table kept buying us caraway schnapps. Father’s Day from 0:12 ↗

The marksmen of Meißendorf

Our hosts were the Schützenverein Meißendorf shooting club. The band warmed up in a clubhouse full of trophies, we met the shooting king with his royal chain, and local marksmen showed us how to load and fire a small salute cannon. The club celebrated its centenary that year; an honorary target with a stag and the year 1998 was presented in the clubhouse. Shooting king from 1:52 ↗

Concerts and the way home

In the large marquee of the 100th Schützenfest a local marching band played first, then Turnovanka. On Saturday we drove to Hanover to visit a dialysis unit and in the afternoon to a concert under the trees. On Sunday there was still Meyers Biergarten, a stop at the Heidemuseum – and the journey home. Turnovanka plays from 4:35 ↗

About the recording

The video comes from my own tape (VHS, later DVD). The picture is gently restored without any generated additions; the narration is a synthetic voice based on my memories. Places are verified from signs in the shots; what only I remember is told as a memory.
