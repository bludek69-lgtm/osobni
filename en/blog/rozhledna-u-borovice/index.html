Near Borovice: stone lookout tower above Roprachtice

Stone tower, wooden walkway and open landscape around Roprachtice. A short view from a drone shows the lookout tower U Borovice as part of the Krkonoše countryside. On your own trip, however, it is useful to know the story of the building and the difference between an aerial view and a visitor's view.

A tower of stone and wood

The official information center of Vysokého nad Jizerou states that the construction started in April 2008 and the ceremonial opening on November 17, 2009. There is a covered wooden walkway above the stone part. There are seventy steps leading to the viewpoint, so the visit takes a rather specific form: from the vicinity of the tower, first up the stairs and only then out into the countryside.

In the video you can notice the contrast of the materials. The solid lower part holds the structure to the ground, while the walkway gives the whole tower a different rhythm. At the same time, the shot from a greater distance helps to understand its position among the fields and scattered buildings. The detail of the building and the overall view complement each other well here.

What can't be seen from a drone

The aerial footage is not a substitute for the view from the walkway itself. The camera can look from a different height and a different direction; visibility and weather are also decisive for a normal visit. The purpose of the video is to zoom in on a place in the landscape, not to promise that a person will see the exact same composition from the observation tower.

Therefore, take some time to visit the area under the tower during your trip. Looking across the field can explain orientation better than a quick ascent and return. If you enjoy comparing different viewpoints, continue with the story Kozákova. Both towers have a different appearance and relationship to the surroundings.

Current visit

With an older video, it is easy to take over older travel information as well. In this case, there is a significant change in operation: the info center now lists the municipality of Roprachtice as the operator and explicitly points out that the typewriter museum is no longer located at the lookout tower. Therefore, plan your trip for the lookout and the surrounding landscape, not for the earlier exposition.

  • Current opening and entrance verification on the info center page or at the municipality. The tour of the promenade depends on the operation of the observation tower.
  • Count the stairs; the aerial view does not indicate the accessibility of the interior.
  • Use the map to find the place, check access and parking according to local signs.

You can add for another Krkonoše stop Semily from Jizera. The small lookout tower and the city quarter from above offer two scales of the same landscape.
