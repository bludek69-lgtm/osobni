Semily Na Kruhách: Jizera, tennis and neighborhood memory

Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Semiles from above are not just a set of roofs. In the Na Kruhách locality, the river, gardens, trees and a tennis court meet. The video offers a view of a part of the city whose story can be read precisely in the relationships between these ordinary places.

Jizera as a landmark

An aerial view helps to differentiate what we perceive gradually when walking down the street. The river creates a continuous line, the trees separate the individual spaces and the roofs show the layout of the building. When watching, it is therefore worthwhile to first orient yourself according to Jizera and only then look for smaller details.

Such an image of the city does not require that every house be a famous monument. The distance between the courts and the green, the garden behind the house or the way the open space transitions into the building can be interesting. For locals, they are common parts of the neighborhood; the visitor can discover a different view of Semily in them.

Tennis club and a long memory of the sports field

The history of one specific place is brought closer by the Semily Tennis Club. On its website, it states that it was founded on March 8, 1928 under the name Lawn-Tennis Department of S. K. Semily and reminds of the former sand courts. The current image of the sports ground is the result of the long-term work of several generations.

This is a useful context for understanding the video: courts are not just colored rectangles in an aerial composition. They are a place for regular meetings, maintenance and club life. However, the current operation or the possibility of playing needs to be verified directly with the club; the travel record itself does not provide any access.

Sports, work and neighborhood

The video also reminds of Josef Vaňko's factory. In addition to sports history, it opens up another layer of local narrative: urban work and production. The point is not to convert each roof to a precise historical plan, but to notice that different kinds of memory overlap in one location.

After an aerial view, a simple walk on public roads can be interesting. Changing the height of the view will remind you how differently the same place is composed when walking slowly. The map above the article is for orientation, it is not a sign of free entry to gardens, houses or sports facilities.

If you want to compare the city with the wider landscape, continue with the story Kozákova or U Borovice lookout tower. From the river to the hills, the scale changes, but the local landscape remains connected.
