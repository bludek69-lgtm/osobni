Venice: the story begins on the lagoon

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 min read
Video · 12:26 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

The journey to Venice begins in this video even before the first narrow street. From Punta Sabbioni it goes across the lagoon, past San Giorgio Maggiore to the canals, the Doge's Palace and St. Mark's Square. Arriving by water gives familiar sights a framework that is missing from looking at a single photograph.

The lagoon as the beginning of the story

UNESCO protects Venice together with its lagoon. It is an essential connection: the historic city cannot be completely separated from the water, which influenced its transport, construction and relations with its surroundings. Therefore, the boat introduction of the video is not just waiting for the main sights, but part of getting to know the place itself.

Try to keep track of the distances between individual points during the cruise. The silhouette of a church or a tower first serves as an orientation and only later turns into a detail. Upon arrival, the scale changes again: open water is replaced by a narrow channel and a view between houses.

Gondolas and daily passages

Gondolas are among the most famous Venetian paintings. But they can also show the dimensions of waterways in relation to the surrounding houses and bridges. The gondola ride alone is not a necessary condition for getting to know the city; many connections can be perceived from the waterfront and during a walking tour.

The alleys between the big stops are worth as much attention as the square. Film editing can shorten the journey, but the visitor must pass through specific passageways and bridges. The actual plan therefore states on the map and actual distances, not just the minutes between chapters.

Palace, Square and Basilica

The Doge's Palace and St. Mark's Basilica form a striking pair at the end of the film. The entire square then allows us to compare their relationship to the open space. To visit the interiors, it is necessary to separately verify reservations and operations; an outdoor walk and a paid tour are not the same program.

  • 1:34 – lagoon cruise
  • 5:04 - San Giorgio Maggiore
  • 7:50 a.m. – Doge's Palace
  • 11:12 - St. Mark's Basilica

Before you travel, confirm the current boat connection, the city's visiting conditions and any obligations for day visitors. Plan your return with a reserve. The entry shows one route, not today's timetable or entry rules.

After the main sights, leave room for a smaller canal as well. Water will then cease to be just a backdrop and once again become what connects the individual parts of the city.

History and transport

Build the day around your return boat

The film follows Punta Sabbioni, the lagoon, the San Marco waterfront, the Doge’s Palace and the Basilica. For your own visit, I suggest choosing the return service before deciding how many interiors to see. Walk the waterfront and square first, then select one longer interior visit. Allow time to walk back and find the correct departure pier.

Separate boat transport, monument admission and any city Access Fee. A single ticket does not automatically cover all three. Compare transport options on Venezia Unica against your planned journeys and the ticket’s coverage.

Checked 18 November 2026: the city says its 2026 Access Fee trial ended on 26 July, with 2027 arrangements to be announced separately. Check the city’s official information before booking a future trip rather than relying on a 2023 film.
