Croatia: stone towns, rivers and the road to Pelješac

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 6:15 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Croatia can also be recognized by stone and water: by the villages on the Istrian hills, the river branches by the old mills or the walls above the coast. This part of the Hidden Gems connects Kotli, Hum, Grožnjan, Motovun, Rastoke, Lubenice and Ston with Mali Ston.

It is a selection of places for slower exploration of the country. The stops lie in several regions, so take the order of the video as inspiration for a longer journey. You will get the most of them if you choose a smaller area and also leave time for the streets between individual monuments.

Istria close up: Kotli and Hum

The beginning goes to Kotli. With the stone village and the river landscape, it is worth observing how close nature and traces of human work can be. The water here is not just an accessory to the view; it helps to understand why life centered around her. Kotli in the video from 0:29 ↗

Hum offers a different way of viewing. Instead of a long list of attractions, it is useful to focus on the scale of a small settlement: the passage to the streets, the relationship of the houses to the road and minor changes to the stone facades. The travel experience here doesn't have to depend on how many stops you make. It can also be a short walk where you don't skip anything.

Grožnjan and Motovun: towns in the countryside

Another pair is Grožnjan and Motovun. In the video, they come one after the other, so it is easy to compare two views of the Istrian hinterland: the mansion in the distant view and the same world from the level of a narrow street. Only with such an alternation does the panorama become a concrete place.

For your own trip, it is useful to choose one village as the main destination and add another one depending on the time. Transfers, finding parking and the journey to the historic center are part of the program as well as the tour itself. It offers an overview of cities and visitor information the official tourist website of Istria. Grožnjan and Motovun from 1:41 ↗

From Rastoke via Cres to Ston

Rastoke u Slunje once again returns attention to the river. After the stone towns, it's a change of environment and the rhythm of the narrative. The comparison with Kotli can be more interesting than trying to determine one "prettiest" place: water, terrain and buildings meet differently every time.

Watermelons on Cres move the story to the island. When planning, it is therefore also necessary to consider island transport and travel time. The conclusion belongs to Ston and Mali Ston on Pelješac. The video thus ends with another form of stone landscape, this time associated with fortifications. Lubenice and the final stop at Ston from 4:10 ↗

How to create your own path from the selection

Practically, three separate parts are offered: the Istrian hinterland, Rastoke as its own stop, and the island or southern part of the journey. It is not necessary to drive through all of them for the first acquaintance. Choose the one that best suits your time and the way you travel.

V the map to the video you will open individual locations including both towns in Ston. Check current access, parking and possible boat connections before your trip. A photogenic residence can also be a place of everyday life; it is the space for its regular rhythm that makes a slower visit interesting.
