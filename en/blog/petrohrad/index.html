St. Petersburg: between the great monuments and the ordinary street

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 minutes of reading
Video · 5:11 · YouTube loads on startupOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Sights in photos, a wide river and then driving through city streets. The St. Petersburg record alternates between two scales: known landmarks and common movement between them. The article accompanies it as a return to the captured city and its architectural context.

A city that is measured by the Neva

UNESCO describes St. Petersburg as a large-scale urban planning project started under Peter the Great at the beginning of the 18th century. It emphasizes the connection between architecture, water bodies and the waterfront. Therefore, the river is not just the background of the photo: it helps to explain distances, open views and how individual landmarks show each other.

At the part with the Neva and the Winter Palace, you can notice how much space separates the observer from the building. For the same reason, the city as a whole is read differently from the embankment and differently while driving down the street. The video allows these views to be compared without mistaking them for a single continuous walking route.

From the big names to the common street

The introduction recalls the Smolny Temple, the Kazan Cathedral, the Peter and Paul Fortress and other well-known buildings. Next comes the Neva, the museum ship Aurora, the night city and street traffic. The transition between photographs and driving is substantial: next to representative monuments, the city appears as an environment of everyday movement.

It is not necessary to identify all the houses on each pass. The regularity of the facades, the opening of the view by the water or the change in lighting are also interesting. Such a view complements the list of famous buildings with an experience of distances and urban rhythm that cannot be explained by a single photograph of a landmark.
