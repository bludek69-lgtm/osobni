Siena: from the narrow streets to Piazza del Campo

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 minutes of reading
Video · 8:32 · YouTube loads on startupOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Siena is entered through the streets in the video. Only after them comes the cathedral and the wide area of Piazza del Campo. This sequence is also characteristic of the city when viewed: a narrow passage focuses attention on a detail, an open square suddenly widens it.

Campo is no ordinary rectangle

Visit Tuscany describes the Piazza del Campo as a shell-shaped space, divided into nine sections reminiscent of the Rule of the Nine. The shape of the square is therefore worth observing as a whole: the sloping surface, the direction of the individual fields and the relationship to the Palazzo Pubblico. In an edge shot, these links can stand out better than when looking at just one building.

The Palazzo Pubblico and its tower give the place a distinctive aspect and orientation. In the streets before arriving at the Campo, the space is composed gradually; here it suddenly opens. It's a good time to stop the video and try to find on the map where the walk to the square came from.

A city with its own rhythm

The Palio, a race associated with the local districts, belongs to the Camp. The tourist portal lists its two traditional events during the year. However, the normal shape of the square in the video cannot be deduced from the shape during the celebration: the space, the movement of people and the possibilities of passage can change. For your own trip, it is reasonable to find out whether it falls on an ordinary day or an event.

In addition to the large square, smaller transitions between houses are also important. Siena is not just a few separate monuments. Streets allow us to compare the scale of people with the scale of public buildings and provide the main stops in their surroundings. When you return to the video, you can notice just these sections between the goals.

The cathedral as the second focal point of the walk

The cathedral and the Campo form two different peaks in the video. At the church, attention turns to the facade and architectural details, in the square to the entire space. For your own visit, think in advance whether you want to stay at the outdoor walk or enter the monument complex. Check open sections and tickets with the operator.

  • Leave time for the journey between the cathedral and the square. Getting around is part of getting to know the city.
  • When visiting during events, check the restrictions in the center and on the Camp.
  • For another Tuscan city try Montepulciano; it serves for an overview of wider paths Tuscany in all senses.
