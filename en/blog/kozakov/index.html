Kozákov: lookout tower, hut and landscape below the peak

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20262 minutes of reading
Video · 8:43 · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

A lookout tower with technical equipment, a tourist hut and a landscape that diverges from the top in several directions. Kozákov is not just a point for a distant view. The video combines its buildings, the surrounding fields and forests, as well as a reminder of the hill's volcanic past.

A tower for technology and tourists

KČT Semily explains the origin of today's lookout tower by connecting a military tower with a publicly accessible walkway. The tower was erected in 1994 and ceremonially opened to the public in April 1995. It measures 48 meters in total, the visitor walkway is 24 meters high and 120 steps lead to it.

These numbers help to read the picture: the upper technical part and the tourist viewpoint are not the same. In addition, the view from the drone can come from a different height than the one experienced by a person on the sidewalk. Therefore, take the aerial footage as a supplement to the idea of ​​the place, not as an exact demonstration of the view available from each step.

The lodge gives the peak another dimension

Next to the tower, Riegro's tourist hut appears in the video. After broad views of the landscape, attention returns to the specific background of the peak: the road, the building and the space between the buildings. It is in such transitions that the vantage point turns into a place where you can spend some time.

The reminder of the volcanic past adds another layer of time to the landscape. But for this trip, it is not necessary to determine each stone from an aerial view. It may be more useful to observe the shape of the hill, the continuity of forests and open areas, and the relationship between the peak and the surrounding settlements.

The outlook depends on the day

A clear picture of the surroundings often encourages you to look for familiar places on the horizon. Conditions change, however, and a specific view from a video is not a promise of the same visibility during your own visit. KČT announces the opening of the observation tower in good weather. Therefore, plan the approach to the top and the entrance to the walkway as two related but different parts of the journey.

  • Check the current operation of the lookout tower and the cottage; the record does not replace today's visitor information.
  • Expect a staircase to the walkway and an open summit environment.
  • The map will help with orientation in the surroundings, choose a specific approach according to the marked paths and your own possibilities.

Continue to compare lookout U Borovice or return below to Jizera in Semile. The difference between the peak and the city helps to understand the scale of the whole region.
