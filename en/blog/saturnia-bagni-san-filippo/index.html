Saturnia and Bagni San Filippo: a landscape built by water

Water can be an architect in Tuscany. Near Saturnia it forms shallow steps and pools, in the forest near Bagni San Filippo it creates white formations resembling a statue. The video connects two well-known thermal places, which are worth perceiving each separately.

Saturnia and cascades at the mill

The Cascate del Mulino are linked to the Maremma landscape. The official Visit Tuscany describes the sulphurous water that creates warm pools and small waterfalls here. It is therefore worth watching the people in the water and the way the water transitions from one stage to another in the video. The whole works as an interconnected system, not as several separate pools.

Photos of these places often give the impression that the appearance of the cascades is still the same. During your own visit, however, the weather, the number of people and the specific place from which you are looking enter into the experience. Take the older shot as an introduction to the landscape, not as a promise of a quiet lake just for yourself.

White whale in the forest

Bagni San Filippo offers a different scene: water, forest and light limestone deposits. A unit called Balena Bianca, the white whale, is formed by the deposition of minerals from thermal water. The contrast between the light surface and the green surroundings is striking. The name suggests how people interpreted this unusual shape.

Visit Tuscany expressly points out the fragility of the formation: it must not be walked on, touched or bathed in its immediate vicinity. Choosing a place to observe therefore also means respecting that the formation is not a staircase or a photo prop.

Two stops, two different trips

A shared video does not mean that the two locations are next to each other. When preparing your own trip, use the map and decide how much time you want to spend moving and how much staying in place. Sometimes it's better to pick one stop and get to know its surroundings than to just go through both quickly.

  • Verify current access and local rules. A hot spring is not the same as a swimming pool with 24/7 supervision and facilities.
  • Respect the defined sections and fragile deposits, especially for the White Whale.
  • A story can also serve as a guide for the journey around Bagni San Filippo Val d'Orcia; wider context offers Tuscany in all senses.

Both places are connected by the slow work of water. That is why it is worth slowing down as a visitor as well and paying attention to what the flow gradually creates.
