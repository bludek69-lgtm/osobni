Bohemian paradise: among rocks, castles and stories

Two towers on the horizon, bright rocks above the forest and a mill known from a fairy tale. The Bohemian Paradise is not just one view: its story consists of rock towns, castles, ponds and village buildings. The video connects sixteen places and shows how nature, history and film meet in one landscape.

This article complements the photographic journey Bohemian Paradise | Wandering among rocks, castles and ponds. It will help you choose a stop for your own trip. The sequence in the video is a narrative across the region, not a ready walking route for one day.

Prachovské skály and Jičín: two different beginnings

In Prachovské skály, attention alternates between the view and the space right in front of you. From the vantage point you can see a cluster of sandstone towers, below the path is lost in the passages between them. It is the alternation of open views and narrow corridors that gives the rock town its character.

Some towers are up to 40 meters high. For the first visit, it is useful to choose a marked circuit according to time and difficulty; visitor routes have their own regime and entrance fee. You can find the current offer in the official overview of the Prachovské skals. Prachovské rocks in the video from 0:21 ↗

In Jičín, the natural towers will be replaced by Valdická brána. The next stop, the Valdštejnská loggia, reminds us that Albrecht of Valdštejn also thought about arranging the landscape outside the city. In the video, the connection of the city, the alley and the loggia is a counterbalance to the wild impression of the rocks.

Kozákov, Vranov, Frýdštejn and Rotštejn

Kozákov offers a view of the region from a different height. The peak is 744 meters above sea level and there is a tower with a tourist observation platform next to Riegro's cottage. The lodge was inaugurated in 1928. History of the site and information on visiting is published by KČT Semily. The extent of the view depends on the weather; height alone does not guarantee far visibility.

The subsequent part of the video shows three forms of rock castles. Vranov nad Mala Skálou, also known as the Pantheon, rises above the Jizera valley. You can recognize Frýdštejn by its massive cylindrical tower. At Rotštejn, on the other hand, the masonry, openings and sandstone blocks connect into a fragmented whole. While watching, try to compare where the builders used the rock itself and where they supplemented it with a wall. Cossacks and rock castles from 1:36 ↗

Hruboskalsko: from rock towers to water level

Hruboskalsko returns the narrative between the light sandstone towers. It is not only the individual rock formation that is important, but also the forest, ravines and buildings on their edge. Hrubá Skála Castle stands on the site of a medieval castle, and its current form was created by later reconstructions. Today it also serves as a hotel, so the entire building is not a regular castle tour. Check the possibilities of the visit in overview of Hrubá Skála castle.

Nearby Valdštejn dates back to around 1260. A place of pilgrimage and romantic touches were added to its medieval story. When you look at the bridge, courtyard and buildings, you are watching several different layers of history, not a castle that has been preserved unchanged. Details are provided the official tourist overview of Valdštejn.

At the Věžické pond, the rhythm of wandering calms down. The rocks rise right at the surface and their reflection adds another dimension to the landscape. Věžák belongs to the Podtrosecké údolí, where ponds, watercourses and sandstone walls meet. It offers more about the place regional tourism website. Hruboskalsko, Valdštejn and Věžák from 3:14 ↗

Wreck and Bone: Where History Ends and Rumor Begins

The silhouette of Trosek is formed by the Baba and Panna towers. In the video, a view of the castle is followed by one of the legends that connects the names of the towers to two feuding women. It is a story told about a place, not a documented biography of its inhabitants. Information about the castle itself and links to the administrator can be found in Debris overview.

Similarly, you need to read the story about Kosti and Jan Žižek. The video explicitly separates the known rumor from the older documented name of the castle. Rumors belong to the Bohemian Paradise: they can revive the view of a tower or a wall, but they do not replace historical documents. Debris, the rumor about Baba and the Virgin and Kost from 4:55 ↗

Humprecht, Vesec and the real address of the fairytale mill

The conclusion moves to Sobotka. After the castles comes Humprecht and then a smaller scale of half-timbered houses in Vesc. Its round semi-trailer and preserved buildings remind us that the charm of the region is not only created by large monuments. You can use it to connect the surrounding places on foot the official subject of the trip from Sobotka Plakánek to Kost; plan the turn to Vesce and the total length according to your own route.

The Strěhom mill is in the film There are no jokes with devils the home of Petr Máchal. But the real wooden building with the water wheel also belongs to the history of everyday work at the Klenice stream. According to CzechTourism – Where to go out of boredom today it serves as a private house. During the trip, therefore, respect the privacy of the residents and do not automatically count on a tour of the interior. Strěhomský mill from 7:13 ↗

How to choose your own trip from the video

Don't try to connect all the stops at all costs. Choose a part of the landscape and leave time for a walk and a tour. The following division is for orientation, it is not a calculated circuit:

  • Rocks and the City: Prachovské skály, Jičín and Valdštejnská loggia.
  • Castles and views: Kozákov, Malá Skála, Frýdštejn and Rotštejn as separate destinations or as a base for a longer stay.
  • Rocky landscape and ponds: Hruboskalsko, Hrubá Skála, Valdštejn and Podtrosecká valley.
  • Saturday: Kost, Humprecht, Vesec and Střehom; select a specific pedestrian connection according to the map.

V map overview of all sixteen places you unlock individual goals. Before departure, check the visiting hours of the monuments, the accessibility of the roads and the weather. In the rocks, expect steps and uneven surfaces; the suitability of a specific circuit for a stroller or limited mobility, check with the administrator.
