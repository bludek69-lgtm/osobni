Tuscany in all senses: from cypresses to the sea

Luděk · Cestovatel6916. 11. 20263 minutes of reading
Video · 32 min · YouTube loads after launchOpen a video on YouTube ↗
Places on the map →Photo sources and materials →

Cypress trees, town squares, thermal water, marble and the coast. The first part of the Italy series presents Tuscany in every sense as a landscape of many different environments. You can watch the more than half-hour long video in its entirety or choose a chapter that fits your own journey.

The journey starts in Val d'Orcia and ends at the sea and Elba. In between, it passes through Montepulciano, Siena, Florence, Pisa, Lucca and smaller settlements. It is not a program for one day: the purpose of the selection is to show the connections between the landscape, the cities and what people created in them.

Val d'Orcia: the landscape between the individual destinations

The opening part belongs to hills and cypress roads. Then come San Quirico d'Orcia, Castiglione d'Orcia, Montalcino and Pienza. It is this group of places that shows that the Tuscan experience is not just about arriving in the city. The landscape between them is also important.

While watching, try to compare the open view of the valley with the area of the square. In both cases, it depends on the arrangement of the space, but each time on a different scale. It offers an overview of the area and its cultural context Visit Tuscany. The start of the journey in Val d'Orcia from 0:14 ↗

Water, wine and smaller towns

You can recognize Bagno Vignoni by the unusual center of the village: instead of the usual square, it is made up of a large reservoir of thermal water. But its presence in itself does not mean the possibility of bathing. He explains the difference between the historic reservoir and today's spa services official website of Bagno Vignoni.

In the video, Bagni San Filippo and Saturnia follow, then Pitigliano, Sorano and Montepulciano. Individual chapters offer different entrances to the region: some will be drawn to water and landscape, others to urban architecture or wine. It is practical to choose one such motif and limit the number of moves accordingly. Montepulciano from 9:47 ↗

Siena, Florence and the way to the west

In the middle part, the scale changes again. After Cortona, Arezzo and La Verna comes Crete Senesi and Siena. Monteriggioni, San Gimignano, Certaldo, Volterra and Chianti follow later. You can thus compare a large city center with smaller settlements and their surroundings.

Florence has its own longer chapter, as did Siena before. It pays to take it as a separate program when planning, rather than another quick item between two stops. The video then continues through San Miniato, Pisa and Lucca towards the mountainous Garfagnana and Carrara. Siena from 14:49 ↗ · Florence from 21:53 ↗

The coast and your own pace

The final chapters include Livorno, Spiagge Bianche, Populonia, Baratti and Elba. After inland panoramas and city streets, the journey takes another ending: the sea and an island environment. It is advisable to plan Elba including a boat connection, not as an obvious detour when crossing the mainland.

Map to video distinguishes between main stops and specific places. For your own stay, choose a smaller group from it: for example Val d'Orcia, a couple of big cities or the coast. Check the opening hours of monuments, entry rules and transport before your trip. Tuscany fits easily into one list; you have to leave the space for the actual stop in the program yourself.
