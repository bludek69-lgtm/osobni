Mountain huts of the Krkonoše: homes, work and human stories

A mountain hut used to be much more than a lunch stop. It was a home, a workplace and a refuge for people living through long winters far from the valleys. This episode of Journeys with a Story looks at that everyday side of the Krkonoše.

From mountain meadows to meeting places

The film begins with woodcutters and mountain farming. It moves through schooling in deep snow and the beginnings of skiing to Luční and Labská bouda, Dvoračky and Štumpovka. These stops form a story about changing mountain life, rather than a single walking itinerary.

Food, supplies and winter journeys have their own chapters. Behind the familiar photographs lies the work needed to provide warmth, meals and somewhere to sleep.

What survived and what disappeared

Another part follows the destruction and rebuilding of Petrova bouda and the fate of Rennerova, Obří and Jestřábí boudy. Contemporary photographs alternate with historical material. Individual photographers and licences are listed in the linked credits.

Hanč, Vrbata and Emerich Rath

From 9:32, the film follows the human story of Hanč, Vrbata and Emerich Rath. The next chapter distinguishes two memorials and their different meanings. The short preview below the film introduces this part.

Before your own trip

This photographic documentary does not replace current route planning. Check trail conditions, access and visitor rules with the Krkonoše National Park administration ↗. You can also browse my photographs from Czechia.
