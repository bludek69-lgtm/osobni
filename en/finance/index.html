Finance & investing

Finance with my own apps

I use two apps I built myself: Portfolio Tracker (PT) for my investment portfolio and Účetní kniha (Bookkeeping) for the broader financial picture. I develop them around my everyday work with data. Here I explain their roles and share my approach to finance.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This page is a personal view and a technical demo. It is not investment advice. All numbers in examples are illustrative and serve only to explain the principle.

My finance apps

Portfolio Tracker (PT)

Investments over time

I use PT to keep track of investments. It works with transactions, holdings, dividends, fees and performance, with statement imports and analytical views for reviewing a portfolio.

Účetní kniha

The broader financial picture

I use Bookkeeping for wider financial records. It connects documents and transactions with income, expenses, assets and liabilities. Being able to trace a number back to its source matters to me.

How I use them together

Bookkeeping gives me the overall picture. PT explores the investment portfolio in more detail, from individual transactions to performance over time.
