Finance with my own apps

I use my own applications for an overview of finances: Portfolio Tracker (PT) for investment portfolio a Accounting book for overall financial records. I build them according to what I need in my daily work with data. Here I show how they complement each other and share my approach to finance.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This page is a personal view and technical demo. This is not an investment recommendation. All numbers in the examples are models and serve only to explain the principle.

My financial app

Portfolio Tracker (PT)

Investment portfolio over time

PT serves me as an overview of investments. It works with transactions, positions, dividends, fees and performance. It offers import of statements, analytical views and background for portfolio review.

Accounting ledger

Overall overview of finances

I use the accounting book for broader records. It combines documents and transactions with an overview of income, expenses, assets and liabilities. Traceability is important to me: what a specific number was created from.

How I use them together

The ledger answers the question of what the finances look like as a whole. PT goes more into the depth of the investment portfolio: from individual transactions to its development over time.
