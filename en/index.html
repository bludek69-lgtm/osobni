About me

I’ve been behind the wheel since 1997. I spent some time in Lerici in Liguria, near Tuscany. I also ran a computer business and an online shop. Today I drive buses for a regional carrier.

In my free time I enjoy Italian cooking, fishing, tennis and playing the drums. I’m also a member of the SDH Semily volunteer fire brigade. I enjoy making practical tools for everyday life.

This website and the apps are personal, non-commercial projects created in my spare time. Detailed project and travel pages are in Czech.
