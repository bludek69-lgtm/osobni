Actors and their stories

Faces from my Italian film collection. Expand a name for a short biography and a link to its source.

Edwige Fenech

Born in 1948 in what is now Annaba, Algeria. She became a familiar face of 1970s Italian comedy, often playing police officers, teachers and doctors, before also working in television.

Biography and source ↗
Lino Banfi

Born Pasquale Zagaria in Andria in 1936. He moved from variety theatre into film, developing a distinctive comic style. His best-known roles include the coach in L’allenatore nel pallone and grandfather Libero in Un medico in famiglia.

Biography and source ↗
Paolo Villaggio

An actor and writer from Genoa (1932–2017), he created the hapless accountant Ugo Fantozzi. The character moved from his books to the cinema in 1975. He also appeared in films by Federico Fellini and Ermanno Olmi.

Biography and source ↗
Bud Spencer

Carlo Pedersoli was a successful swimmer before his film career. He adopted the name Bud Spencer for Dio perdona… io no! in 1967, alongside Terence Hill. His solo roles include Inspector Rizzo in the Piedone films.

Biography and source ↗
Gloria Guida

An Italian actress, former model and television presenter. L’affittacamere (1976) represents her work in my film selection. Rai’s BellaMa’ profile traces her career from modelling and cinema to television.

Biography and source ↗
Alvaro Vitali

A Roman comedian discovered by Federico Fellini, who cast him in Satyricon (1969). He later became a familiar face of Italian comedy alongside Lino Banfi and Renzo Montagnani. His signature comic character is Pierino.

Biography and source ↗
Ezio Greggio

An actor, comedian and presenter born in Cossato in 1954. He became known through Drive In and presented Striscia la notizia from its first season. Here, he is featured above all as Maresciallo Arturo Colombo.

Biography and source ↗
Renato Pozzetto

Born in Milan in 1940, Renato Pozzetto began in cabaret and became famous as half of Cochi e Renato. He made his film debut in Per amare Ofelia (1974). His best-known roles include Artemio in Il ragazzo di campagna and the comedy Le comiche.
