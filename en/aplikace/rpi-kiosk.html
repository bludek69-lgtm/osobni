Why was it created and what can it do?

Why it exists: controlling a smart home via phone is fast only for how slow it is. Before a person unlocks the phone, finds the app, clicks on the right icon, scrolls — that can be turned on manually. Wall panel solves exactly this. It's always on, always ready, with everything important on the first screen.

Hardware: Raspberry Pi 4 (small computer the size of a chewing gum box) + 7" touch screen, screwed to the wall in the hallway. They cost a total of about 2500 CZK. It lives in the Chromium browser running in "kiosk mode" — no system, no menu, just a full application window.

What it can do: control all the lights, start/stop the radio, switch the scene (relax / cinema / cooking / dinner / arrival / departure), adjust the heating, see what is in the fridge (not really — but see what the house consumes in real life, who is at home, who is sleeping, where the motion sensor is currently moving).

Loxone style scenes: large tiles, the thumb presses "Cinema" and the whole apartment is reset — the lights are turned to 30%, the music is turned down, the shutter blinds the street, the heating is turned up a notch. One touch instead of ten.

Detailed list of features
  • Touch screen in the hallway — Raspberry Pi 4 + 7" touch display on the wall. Always on, always ready.
  • Scenes with one thumb — Relaxation, Cinema, Cooking, Dinner, Romance, Work, Arrival, Departure — each scene sets everything at once.
  • Light control — All rooms, dim, color, scenes. No searching on your phone.
  • Audio from the radio — Start/stop internet radios, speaker selection, volume.
  • Heating and energy — Large Heating page: room selection, quick dials Comfort / Night / Exit / Schedule and temperature dial. In addition, boiler runtime, TRV zones and current consumption.
  • Camera and car surveillance — Live image from the camera in front of the house directly on the panel. The system itself recognizes that someone is standing by the car and announces it out loud and with a message to the mobile phone — at the same time ignoring passers-by in the yard.
  • No phone, no login — Nemusí se nikdo přihlašovat, nemusí se nic hledat. Open the door — the panel will show you.

Availability and current status

It runs 24/7 on a Raspberry Pi 4 in the hallway. Auto-restart in case of power failure. About a year without problems. From October 2026, it also controls the heating in the rooms, dims the display in the evening and turns off at night, and after a security check, only the panel itself can control the smart home via the panel.

Development journal
  • More secure home connection (October 2026) — Panel mluví s chytrou domácností přes vlastní prostředníka, který drží přístupový klíč. After a security check, only the panel itself may use it — other devices in the network cannot control the house through it or read its settings.
  • No Secrets Online (October 2026) — When reading the code, it turned out that the panel's simple web server was also issuing files from another computer on the home network that didn't belong there — the smart home key and camera login. Now it only serves the panel's own screens and rejects everything else; the key is stored outside the public files. Verified from another computer: screens load, sensitive files don't.
  • Display night mode (October 2026) — Panel v chodbě v noci svítil do místnosti. Večer se teď ztlumí, v noci (nebo když dům spí) displej zhasne a dotykem se na dvě minuty slabě rozsvítí. Stav domu bere ze stejného zdroje jako dashboard; když ho nemá, řídí se hodinami, a při nejistotě svítí naplno — černý panel ve dne je horší než svítící v noci.
  • Panel heating (October 2026) — Separate large Heating page: you select a room, tap Comfort, Night, Exit or Schedule, or set the exact temperature with the rotary control. The manual change is valid until the next transition in the schedule, then the head will return to the schedule by itself. The panel and the Config Center PC app share the same control module.
  • Touch panel on the wall — Raspberry Pi 4 + 7-inch touchscreen in the hallway, always on and ready. Scenes with one thumb (Relax, Cinema, Cooking, Dinner, Romance, Work, Arrival, Departure).
  • Control of the entire apartment — lights (all rooms, dim, color, scenes), audio from Internet radios (speaker selection, volume), heating and energy (runtime boilers, TRV zones, current consumption).
  • No phone, no login — no one has to log in or search for anything; you open the door and the panel shows you.
  • Camera in front of the house (August 2026) — the panel shows a live image from the camera and recent events with previews. You don't have to go anywhere or look for an app on your phone.
  • Car Watch (August 2026) — the system automatically recognizes that someone is standing by the car and announces it loudly in the kitchen and by sending a message to the mobile phone. The difficulty was not to recognize a person, but to distinguish "someone is at my car" from "someone is walking in the yard" — the yard is passable and more than a hundred people pass through it every day. The first attempt even made it worse: there were more false alarms. It turned out why — in a flat image, a person can be fifteen meters away right next to a car. Now it is judged how big it is in the frame: who is standing next to the car is seen as big, who is far away is small. False alarms dropped from twenty-six to zero.
  • Two announcement volumes — loud when someone is right next to the car. And a quieter message on the mobile phone when someone is just lingering by the car. So that you don't start turning off notifications after a week.
  • Reliable 24/7 — runs all the time, auto-restart in the event of a power failure, about a year without problems.

Note: the application only runs locally, real data and personal data do not go to the website.
