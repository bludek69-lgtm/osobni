Italia Travel Planner

🇮🇹Italia Travel Planner

Italy trip planner for people who travel there often or long. Builds an itinerary, tracks tickets, draws a map, manages a budget, warns about ZTL zones (where a car drive-in costs 80–200 €). Works offline, no accounts, no payments through the app.

React TypeScript Vite Tauri (Windows) PWA

← Back to My apps

What it does

Why it exists: Italy travel has its own rules. Historic centers have ZTL zones (Zona Traffico Limitato) — drive in by car and you get an 80–200 € fine. Tickets for the Vatican, Uffizi, and Colosseum sell out a month ahead for specific time slots. Motorhomes need different infrastructure than cars — Stellplatzy, area sosta, dump stations. Mainstream travel apps don't handle this — either they force an account and try to sell everything, or they're Italy-blind.

One user, everything local: the app is mine, runs on my computer, on my phone as PWA, or as a Windows desktop app. No backend, no cloud, no API keys. Plans the trip — itinerary, tickets, budget, route. Actual purchases (flights, hotels, tickets) I make myself on official sites — the app just opens them in the browser via deeplink. No contract, no commission, no vendor lock-in.

Planning from scratch: New trip (name, region, dates, party size, budget, style — city / roadtrip / coast / mountains / wine & food / camper). The app offers four transport modes with deeplinks (Kiwi for flights, Booking for car, Trenitalia for trains). For roadtrips there's a dedicated planner with 10 country picker, ~100 city autocomplete, and route style (Fast / Balanced / Experience).

Itinerary and tickets: Each day three sections (Morning / Afternoon / Evening) with specific activities and times, plus food tips, transport notes, rain backup plan. Tickets tracked with date, time, confirmation code. Alternatives from GetYourGuide and Big Bus are manual deeplinks with status „unknown“ or „pending user confirmation“ — the app never assumes they're paid.

Camper mode: Unique feature most travel apps lack. Create a profile for my motorhome (dimensions, weight, fuel, equipment). The app then filters stops by type (Stellplatz, Area sosta, Camping, Agriturismo, Service point, Free wild, …) and by facility (water, electricity, toilet, shower, black water, …). Warns when a spot doesn't have enough height/length for my camper, or is inside a ZTL zone. Plus camper-specific budget (fuel + tolls + camping + service).

Export to real tools: Itinerary → Google Calendar (ICS), POI → Google My Maps (CSV), budget → Excel, full trip → JSON for backup. No proprietary format.

Fully offline: After first load the app is in browser cache or installed as a desktop app (Windows installer, ~18 MB). When I'm in Italy and roaming costs €1/day, I pay zero for internet — app shows everything locally from memory.

Key features

  • Planning from scratch with deeplinks — New trip with budget and style, 4 transport tabs with deeplinks to Kiwi / Booking / Trenitalia. App doesn't broker purchases — opens the official site in the browser.
  • Roadtrip planner for 10 countries — Pick origin/destination country, ~100 city autocomplete, route style, stop count, multi-select interests.
  • Route planner on the map — Click the map to add points, drag them, reorder them. The route is saved to the trip and survives closing. Import from GPX / KML / GeoJSON files or a Google Maps link; reverse "Open in Google Maps". All from OpenStreetMap, keyless.
  • Along-route stops — Each stop shows which leg it sits on, how far along the route, distance from the route and an estimated detour — and appears as a pin on the map. Keyless online search for fuel / WC / food / pharmacies near the route (OpenStreetMap).
  • Accommodation from the map — Finds hotels, guesthouses and campsites near the route from OpenStreetMap and attaches them to a specific night. No made-up prices, no automatic booking — you confirm the actual booking yourself on the official site.
  • Day-by-day itinerary — Morning / Afternoon / Evening with times + food + rain backup. Per trip, fully offline.
  • Tickets with alternatives — Main reservations (Vatican, Uffizi, Colosseum) with specific date + 7 GetYourGuide / Big Bus alternatives as manual deeplinks, status 'unknown'.
  • Budget with cap utilization — 12 categories, visual bar of % paid, mandatory vs optional vs paid.
  • POI map + Google My Maps export — POIs visualized with color icons, one-click CSV importable to Google My Maps.
  • Camper mode with ZTL warnings — Motorhome profile, 10 stop types, 11 facility chips, warnings when spot is in ZTL or too small for your camper.
  • Works offline, multi-platform — Web PWA + Windows desktop (installer, ~18 MB) + portable web launcher from one codebase. No roaming needed in Italy.

Status

Version 1.6.5 — beta (August 2026). A run of distribution fixes that kept uncovering each other. The app actually stores your data now — up to 1.6.1 the embedded browser ran in private mode and its profile landed in a temp folder, so nothing survived closing the window; from the outside it looked like an app with sample data, not like data loss. And the app now tells you a new version exists — the update banner was being dropped by the page's own security policy, so the server was never asked and the update could not be started at all. That is the one proven reason automatic updates never happened, and also why the storage fix never showed up. The way the app closes before installing changed too. On the feature side: backups now carry photos and checklist state, and a backup can be restored from inside the app.

Version 1.5.0 brought the big map update: plan a route directly on the map (click to add points, drag them, reorder — the route is saved to the trip and survives closing), along-route stops now show which leg they sit on, how far along the route, and the detour, and appear as pins on the map. New: pick accommodation from the map (finds hotels/campsites near the route from OpenStreetMap and attaches them to a specific night) and route import from GPX / KML / GeoJSON files or a Google Maps link. Everything keyless (OpenStreetMap / Overpass / Nominatim), no made-up prices, no automatic bookings.

The earlier base remains: Pre-departure hub (documents, budget dashboard, packing list), Car trip to Italy planner, camper mode with ZTL warnings, itinerary with conflict checking, 11 exports (PDF, Word, KML/GPX, checklist…), keyless online search for stops (fuel, WC, food, pharmacies) from OpenStreetMap, checksummed backups, offline mode, AI assistant. Windows installer + PWA + web launcher. Private, for my own trips — no public hosting, no accounts, no payments.
