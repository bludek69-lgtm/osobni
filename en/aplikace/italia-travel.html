Why was it created and what can it do?

Why it was created: traveling in Italy has its own rules. Historic centers have ZTL zone (Zona Traffico Limitato) — if you drive into them, you will be fined €80-200. Tickets to the Vatican, the Uffizi and the Colosseum are bought a month in advance for a specific time and day, otherwise you won't get in. A motorhome has different needs than a passenger car — you need Stellplatzs, area sosta, dump stations. And classic travel apps don't solve it — either they force you to have an account and buy everything through them, or they are completely blind to Italy.

One user, all local: the application belongs to me, it runs on my computer, on my phone as a PWA, or as a Windows desktop app. I save trip data locally. Location search uses online map services; the optional accommodation price feed requires its own API key. He plans the trip — itinerary, tickets, budget, route. I then buy the actual purchases (air tickets, hotels, tickets) myself on the official website — the application just opens them for me in the browser via deeplink. No contract, no commission, no vendor lock-in.

AI Autopilot (from 1.7.0): I answer a few questions — to, from where, for how many days, a hotel or sleeping in the car — and Autopilot suggests the entire trip: route, division into days, specific stops along the route and specific accommodation for each night. Will offer three variants that are really different from each other (how many hours a day I spend behind the wheel, hotel vs. boarding house, how big a detour I can take). All locations are real from OpenStreetMap — with coordinates, address and visible origin of each number ("from map data" vs. "computed"); what could not be found is honestly marked as a wish, not a place. Prices and availability see application he doesn't invent — the map data does not have them, so it does not assert them. The proposal can be modified with the words ("Add a day in Rome") and a verified backup will be created before writing to the trip — if it fails to create, nothing will be overwritten. For sleeping in the car, he looks for campsites (not parking lots where he would quietly pass off sleeping as permitted) and suggests a backup sleeping place in case the first one is occupied.

Planning from scratch: Enter a new trip (name, region, date, number of people, budget, style — city stay / road trip / coast / mountains / wine & food / camper). The application then offers me four modes of transport with deep links (Kiwi for flights, Booking for car, Trenitalia for trains). For the road trip, it has a separate planner with a selection of 10 countries, autocomplete of cities (approx. 100 European destinations) and route style (Fast / Balanced / Experiential).

Itinerary and tickets: For each day, 3 sections (Morning / Afternoon / Evening) with specific activities and times, plus food tips, transport notes, backup plan for rain. Tickets are registered with a specific date, time and confirmation code. Alternative offers from GetYourGuide and Big Bus are only deeplinks with the status "unknown" or "pending user confirmation" — the application is never does not automatically assume as paid.

Camper mode: A unique feature that most travel apps don't have. I will create a profile of my motorhome (dimensions, weight, consumption, equipment). The application then filters tracks by type (Stellplatz, Area sosta, Camping, Agriturismo, Service point, Free wild, ...) and by equipment (water, electricity, toilet, shower, black water, ...). It warns when the site is not high enough/long enough for my camper, or when it is in the ZTL zone. Plus camper-specific budget (fuel + tolls + camping + service).

Export to real tools: Itinerary → Google Calendar (ICS), POI → Google My Maps (CSV), budget → Excel, complete trip → JSON for backup. No proprietary format, all standard.

Offline Saved Plans: I can open the itinerary and saved documents even without a connection. Searching for new places, route recalculation and current accommodation offers require the Internet.

Detailed list of features
  • AI Autopilot — A few questions → the whole journey: route, days, actual stops and accommodation from OpenStreetMap. Three variants with a different goal (hours behind the wheel, type of accommodation, tolerance of stops), edits with words, verified deposit before each entry. No fancy prices — what the provider doesn't have, the application doesn't claim; without the network, he admits what could not be found.
  • Planning from scratch with deeplinks — Nová cesta s rozpočtem a stylem, 4 záložky dopravy s deeplinky na Kiwi / Booking / Trenitalia. Aplikace nákupy nezprostředkovává — otevře oficiální stránku v prohlížeči.
  • Roadtrip planner for 10 countries — Selection of country of departure and destination (CZ/IT/AT/SI/DE/SK/HR/PL/FR/CH), autocomplete of cities (~100 destinations), route style, number of stops, multi-select interests.
  • Route planner on the map — Click to add points to the map, drag them, rearrange them. The route is saved to the trip and remains even after closing. Import from a GPX / KML / GeoJSON file or from a Google Maps link; reverse "Open in Google Maps". All from OpenStreetMap, no key.
  • Stops along the route — At each stop, you can see at which section it is located, how far along the route, the distance from the route and the estimated detour — and they are like points on a map. Online search for fuel / toilet / food / pharmacies near the route (OpenStreetMap, keyless).
  • Accommodation from the map — Finds hotels, guesthouses and campsites near the route from OpenStreetMap and adds them for a specific night. Bez vymyšlených cen, bez automatické rezervace — skutečnou rezervaci si potvrdíš sám na oficiální stránce.
  • Itinerary day by day — Morning / Afternoon / Evening with times + food + rain backup plan. For each trip separately, fully offline.
  • Tickets with alternatives — Main reservation (Vatican, Uffizi, Colosseum) with specific date + 7 alternatives from GetYourGuide and Big Bus as manual deeplinks with 'unknown' status.
  • Budget with cap utilization — 12 categories (transport, accommodation, food, tickets, parking, tolls, camping, …), visual bar how much % is paid, mandatory vs optional.
  • POI map + Google My Maps export — Points of interest visualized with colored icons, one-click CSV export importable to Google My Maps.
  • Camper mode with ZTL warnings — Motorhome profile (dimensions, weight), 10 track types, 11 facility chips, warning badges when the space is in the ZTL zone or small for your camper.
  • Works offline, multi-platform — Web PWA + Windows desktop (installer ~18 MB) + portable web launcher — all from one codebase. It does not need roaming in Italy.

Availability and current status

🔒 The application is not publicly available for download. You will not find the installer here on purpose — I send the link personally to the person I give the application to. The version number on this page remains: it is replenished by itself from the same place where the already installed application gets automatic updates.

Version 1.11.0 — beta (October 2026). Corrections from security and technical audit. Autopilot no longer gets stuck because of photos — before, about eleven photos of your own mobile phone were enough, and the saving of plans was permanently blocked because the insurance deposit did not fit into the limit. Links from imported or shared paths will just open as a regular web address; a link that would run a file or system function in Windows will not let the application out. Place names on the map are displayed as text only. Queries to public map services go at most once per second and have a time limit, so a service outage no longer bogs down the scheduler. The diagnostics of the local server is now also started in the installed application — until 1.10.0 it ran only in the development version.

Version 1.10.0 brought application technical log: the Diagnostics page will show crashes, performance and save status, each problem has an incident number and everything stays on the computer — no cloud and no telemetry.

Version 1.9.2 fixed the disappearing field in Autopilot (after the first letter the field disappeared and only "Ř" got into the plan instead of "Rome") and added a whisperer of real places for "To" and "From".

Version 1.9.1 fixed it price for accommodation: the wholesale partner rate was showing instead of the amount you actually pay when booking on the official site — €1,296 instead of €3,697 for two nights for one verified hotel. The recommended selling price marked as indicative from the provider is now displayed.

Version 1.9.0 brought prices and photos of accommodation from the actual provider (LiteAPI) — optional and with its own key held by the desktop application's local server. Without the key, the application behaves as before (OpenStreetMap without the key) and never makes reservations through the provider.

Version 1.8.0 (August 2026) brought route on a real road ("By road (OSRM)" — routed kilometers and times, honest provenance of costs), dividing the route into days (daily kilometers and driving limits, "Day N" at stops), "Measure along the road" for stops and repairs from a complete visual audit.

Version 1.7.0 (August 2026) brought AI Autopilot — you answer a few questions and the app plans the entire journey: route, days, actual stops along the route and specific accommodation for each night, all from OpenStreetMap with the visible origin of each number. Three variants that are really different; editing the draft with words; for sleeping in a car, campsites and back-up accommodation; a verified backup before writing to the path — without it, nothing will be overwritten. Without a network, the application will admit what it could not find, instead of making it up. The release passed a full gate (over 1400 tests) and a complete visual audit of the entire application.

The 1.6.x versions were a series of distribution fixes that overlapped each other. Application it's really saving data now — until version 1.6.1, the built-in browser ran in private mode and the profile ended up in a temporary directory, so nothing survived closing the window. The application itself will announce that a new version has been released — the update menu bar until then was throwing away the page's security settings, so no one ever asked the server; that was the only proven reason why the auto-update never happened. The first real silent update was verified from 1.6.4 to 1.6.5 — banner, click, install, restart, new version. It has increased from functions backup including photos and checklist status and restore from a backup directly from the application.

A major map update was added in version 1.5.0: route planner directly on the map (you click to add points, drag them, rearrange them — the route is saved in the path and remains even after closing it), stops along the route now they show which section they are at, how far along the route and which detour, and are visible as points on the map. He added selection of accommodation from the map (finds hotels/campsites near the route from OpenStreetMap and adds them for a specific night) a import route from a GPX / KML / GeoJSON file as well as from a Google Maps link. All without an API key (OpenStreetMap / Overpass / Nominatim), without invented prices and without automatic reservations.

The previous basis remains: Preparing for the trip (documents, budget dashboard, packing list), planner Car trip to Italy, camper mode with warning of ZTL zones, itinerary with conflict control, 11 exports (PDF, Word, KML/GPX, checklist...), online search for stops (fuel, WC, food, pharmacies) from OpenStreetMap, backups with checksum, offline mode, AI assistant. Windows installer + PWA + web launcher. Private for your own journeys — no public hosting, no accounts, no in-app payments.

Development journal
  • v1.11.0 — fixes from audit (October 2026) — the architecture and security audit found five things that the tests didn't catch because they only show up on real data or in an installed application. Autopilot makes an insurance backup before each recording — previously even with full-resolution photos, and it has a ceiling of 32 MiB: about eleven photos from the mobile dropped it, and then Autopilot never saved anything again. The backup before writing now does not carry photos because Autopilot does not change them; during the repair, the second trap appeared — the restoration made its own insurance deposit, which also took photos, so the checkpoint would be created, but it could not be restored. The diagnostics of the local server did not start at all in the installed application: the installed version starts in a different way than the development version, and the tests monitored only the development version. Links from imported and shared paths went straight to Windows with an arbitrary scheme in the desktop app — now only http(s) goes through, twice independently (web and desktop); the names of the places on the map are just text. The planner did not create a new path with the same name in the same month, but wrote "Path established" and opened the old one. And the route on the road was called without a time limit, the search for places had three different clients and only one monitored the rules of the public service — now everything goes through one queue, at most once per second. Upgrade from 1.10.0 verified in isolation, data survived.
  • v1.10.0 — App Tech Log (September 2026) — the application now keeps a record of itself: what fell, when it happened for the first and last time, how fast it starts, how much space the log takes up. Each issue is given a number (eg INC-20260902-A7F921), by which it can be traced. But the most important is the fix that is not visible: the error reporting was turned on only when opening the Health Center, so the crash on any other page did not stay anywhere — now it is turned on right at startup. The error when rendering the screen used to end with a white window, now a message with the incident number will appear and the trip data remains saved. The diary is deliberately poor in content and rich in technique: passwords, keys, e-mails and username paths are already blacked out when written, path names and addresses are not written there at all, and the package to be sent also omits free text.
