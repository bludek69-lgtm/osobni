Detailed list of features
  • Monthly overview of shifts — registered shifts are compiled into an overview with attendance totals.
  • Calculation of salary documents — according to the rules (hours, surcharges); at the end of the month compared to the payslip.
  • Closing of the month and top-up from the tape — the main page will show an open month; at the closing time, with one button, it adds things outside the sample tape (premiums for PHM and regular, laundry, CNG) directly from the uploaded pay tape.
  • Tournament editor in the application — new schedule in the table or modification of the existing one, bulk import (HTML/XML/ZIP), stop whisperer and field for the vehicle (V-code, e.g. "bus with 3 axles").
  • Assessment by law — for an individual shift and for the whole month: does the planned shift fit with AETR and NV 589/2006? Violation overview day by day, detailed and abbreviated version, print to PDF.
  • Plan vs reality from driver card — next to the tour schedule, it will be shown what is really on the card (driving, work, rest) and where it differs; rests are assessed continuously through midnight, times from the card are recalculated from UTC to local time.
  • Overall rating of the month — pay slip, shifts taken and driver's card in one overview: how much was actually driven, how it compares with what was paid and a summary of all violations.
  • Driving Time Monitoring (AETR) — shifts with a possible violation of the Road Traffic Act are flagged for inspection; salary remains unaffected.
  • Inspection tape including bonuses and benefits — premium from recorded tape (for PHM, for sales, regular), pension insurance, telephone and laundry; The controlling gross salary thus sits on the crown.
  • Recalculation of closed months after correction of the sample — the application itself finds the closed months affected by the change and offers a recalculation. The turn is not rewritten retroactively: each day is counted by the version valid on that date.
  • Month selection and work indicator — in the wage overview, the month is switched with the roller or arrows; when the app is calculating in the background, you can see it on the bottom right.
  • Overview of holidays for the year — which you took and how much was extra for them, and which fell on rotation leave, where the claim does not arise.
  • Loading the driver card — data from a smart card via a reader (from v1.2.13).
  • MPV Live / Operation — real operation of Semila trains: currently running connections, details of the connection (delays, occupancy), map of car positions and immediate delays (from v1.2.14, read-only).
  • Enrollment checks — validation of times and values, so that the error does not creep into the payment.
  • Silent automatic update — new versions will install themselves; configuration and data remain.
  • No cloud — no account, no server, data only on computer.
Calculations, checks and work with data

A bus driver receives a pay slip every month with one number on it. Verifying it means going through shifts, counting the hours of driving, waiting, night and weekend supplements and meal allowances — and that's according to the collective agreement, not by eye. BudLine Panel does exactly this: it keeps track of what has gone, calculates how much would was to come, and will place it next to what actually came.

1. Record of the day

The day is entered from two sources. Tournament (pattern) says what should have been driven — connections, times, kilometers, breaks. TWO (daily record of vehicle operation) is a paper from the dispatcher: document number, bus, tachograph and tachometer statuses. The application combines the two and calculates the rest, so that only what differs from the plan is entered manually.

  • Bus change in the middle of the day — just say from which line you changed. The application calculates the times and kilometers for both cars from the sample sheet, and when the counter statuses are filled, it takes the fact instead of the plan.
  • Two shifts in one day — when the dispatcher issues a new DZVA for the afternoon, the day is divided into two shifts. The remuneration is calculated according to the driving time; the meal remains in full because the whole day was spent at work.
  • Extra or unused connections — the difference in driving time, work time and kilometers is calculated; duration and km are taken from the timetable, nothing is disputed.

2. Calculation

The wage calculation according to the collective agreement runs above the records: tariff, performance component, waiting, difficult environment, night, weekend and holiday supplements, meal allowance according to the length of the shift. The result can be seen after days or for a whole month.

  • Salary overview of the month — breakdown by days: how many hours, how much of the driving, what surcharges, how many crowns.
  • Reports — own payroll a sheet of paper in the same form as the paper from the employer so that it can be compared line by line.
  • Payroll check — item by item against recorded tape; what doesn't fit can be seen immediately.
  • Check the rotations — a rough estimate of what each turn will bring, even before you go.

3. Control and honesty of numbers

The app is not supposed to make up numbers. When she can't be sure, she will say so and not show the calculated value rather than show the wrong one.

  • Nonsensical data will not be used — when the meter reading goes to 50,000 km per shift or significantly over the plan, the application marks it as a typo and stays with the plan. It doesn't give a typo for a detour.
  • A blank field will say why — if the information is missing on the DZVA, or only one status is filled out. An inverted counter (end lower than start) is marked in red.
  • The salary truth remains on tape from the employer — the application is for checking, not replacing the ledger, and writes it directly on its outputs.
  • Deadline of the month — after closing, the numbers are locked in the snapshot so that it cannot be changed retrospectively.

4. Data stays at home

  • Runs locally on Windows (and Mac), no cloud, no account, no telemetry. The data is on the computer.
  • Advances with one button, optionally encrypted with a password. You can try the recovery first dirty to an isolated copy — you will see what the backup contains, and the raw data will not be affected.
  • Update are downloaded and installed by themselves; data and settings remain.
  • The pages regenerate themselves every time the data changes — there is nothing to refresh manually.

What the app doesn't do

It is not a payroll system and does not replace an accounting office. It doesn't count strangers, it doesn't send anything to the employer, and it doesn't have a web version. It's one driver's tool for checking your own pay — and that's how it's written.

Installation, specifications and status

Python · SQLite · Windows · macOS

🔒 The application is not publicly available for download. You will not find the installer here on purpose — I send the link personally to the person I give the application to. The version number on this page remains: it is replenished by itself from the same place where the already installed application gets automatic updates.

🍏 macOS: Click on the app when you first launch it right click → Open (it is unsigned, it is our own). If it says "corrupted", run it in Terminal xattr -cr "/Applications/BudLine Panel.app".

🛡️ Windows may display a SmartScreen warning (unsigned beta installer). If you know the application and expect it from me, click "More information" → "Start anyway".

  • Core — registration and control of payment — entry of shifts with control (times HH:MM, no negative numbers), composition in a monthly overview, calculation of salary documents according to the rules (hours, night/weekend/holiday supplements) and comparison with the pay slip. Everything locally (SQLite), no cloud or accounts.

Note: the application is an orientation control aid, not accounting or tax advice. The employer's pay stub is binding.
