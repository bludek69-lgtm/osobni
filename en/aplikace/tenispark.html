Why was it created and what can it do?

Why it was created: whoever takes care of a couple of tennis courts with a kiosk knows it: reservations in one notebook, "to record" (who drank what and didn't pay) in another, in the evening counting the cash register on the calculator and own money in the third. TenisPark combines it into one application that absolutely everyone can use — there is a clickable "how to" help on every page.

Tennis courts: reservations are made in a few clicks — court, from–to, name. The application does not allow two people on the same court at the same time, the price is pre-filled from the price list (different for weekdays and weekends), after payment, click "Paid". In addition, maintenance records (lining, sprinkling) and occupancy and income reports for the day and month.

Kiosk: selling goods with self-monitored stock — you cannot sell more than is in stock, and the application notifies you when something is running out. The special function is debtors notebook: if you don't pay immediately, your consumption "to be recorded" will be entered with your name, and if you don't pay within 3 days, the light will be red. In the evening, the daily close is calculated — the application compares how much should be in the cash register with what is actually there. When prices are changed, the old price can be seen crossed out next to the new one in the price list.

Household budget: personal income and expenses like receipts, categories (drawers), monthly "how much do I want to spend on food" limits, plus a year-round planning part for seasonal work — how much to put aside in the summer for the winter.

All locally and securely: the data is in the SQLite database only on the computer, it is backed up with one click to a file with a checksum, and the restoration is always shown first in the preview. The app has a light and dark look in clay colors.

Detailed list of features
  • Reservation of courts without collisions — the application simply does not allow double booking of the same court; price list weekday / weekend, statuses Reserved / Paid / Canceled / Did not arrive.
  • Kiosk with stock — the sale itself reduces the stock, monitors the minimum stocks, it is not possible to sell more than is in stock. The daily closing compares the cash register with the sales.
  • Borrowers notebook — consumption "on record" with name, name whisperer, after 3 days without payment red "overdue", payment with one click.
  • Price list with old and new price — when the price increases, the old price remains crossed out next to the new one, with a history of all changes.
  • Household budget — transactions in pennies (no rounding errors), monthly limits by category with "Exceeded" message, season plan.
  • Backups with checksum — one click, recovery always via preview and confirmation; current data is automatically set aside before recovery.
  • Help for the layman everywhere — clickable "💡 Help — how to" on every page in normal Czech, no technical jargon.
  • Mobile phone and narrow window — on a narrow screen, the side menu is hidden and opened with the ☰ button; on the computer unchanged.
  • What's new and News — after the update, an overview of the changes is shown once and the entire version history is in the menu.
  • Windows application — the desktop window starts and stops its local server by itself; or it can be launched with one double-click in the browser.

Installation (Private Beta)

🔒 The application is not publicly available for download. You will not find the installer here on purpose — I send the link personally to the person I give the application to. The version number on this page remains: it is replenished by itself from the same place where the already installed application gets automatic updates.

🔒 The installer is password protected — I give the password personally, it is not on the website and will not be. Without a password, no one will open the installation.

🛡️ Do you have Avast or AVG? It can block the installer silently (Windows then reports "can't access the file"). Open Antivirus → Quarantine → restore file and add an exception, then run the installer again.

Availability and current status

BETA — private beta for friends (version 0.2.8, July 2026). Windows application with installer.

  • The installer is password protected — only people to whom I personally give the password will receive the application. There is and will be no password on the site.
