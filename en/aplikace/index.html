AUTO VÝBĚRjust for me

Used car finder — merges listings from classifieds, computes a 0–100 score, finds risks and contradictions, and for a car with a published VIN it starts a history check by itself. From a VIN it pulls the vehicle record from the state registry. It also values my own car, can read a VIN from a photo and puts together questions for the seller before a viewing. Afterwards I record what I found, and it computes the contradictions against the ad. It backs its data up and verifies every backup. You can also search by number plate. For every value from an ad it shows where it came from, and it links the same car across sites only with proof — keeping the differences visible. No cloud, no account, no paid databases.
