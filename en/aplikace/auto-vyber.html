Why was it created and what can it do?

Buying a used car depends on two questions: the price is reasonable a I don't know anything about that car. The advertising servers only respond to the first one, and that's even worse — everyone has different data, the same car is on three servers with different drives, and the missing data looks as innocent as the filled one. This application unites both and most importantly honestly separates the documented from the undocumented.

  • From the search profile (make, model, year, mileage, price, equipment, location) it finds offers from sources that allow in robots.txt — for others, it will open a browser search, accept an import or embedded ad text. Robots.txt is not bypassed, CAPTCHA is not cracked — and robots.txt is not a license, so for each resource its conditions are also read.
  • Every offer normalizes (units, fuel, transmission, equipment), deduplicates by VIN and similarity and compares with median of comparable cars including correction for different run-in.
  • It calculates score 0-100 in six areas where at each point you can see where it came from. Missing score data will never increase — it's a loophole, not an advantage.
  • He will find risks: suspiciously low price against market, import with no history, deposit required before inspection, inconsistencies between ads and generally known weaknesses of model and engine (27 entries, each with source and date).
  • For a car with a published VIN he will check the history himself and projects the findings into risks, scores and a watchlist.
Detailed list of features
  • Data with provenance, not just values. From the details of the ad and from embedded text the app will tell the difference year of manufacture, first registration, first registration in the Czech Republic and model year — they are commonly confused in advertisements and can differ by years for an imported car. For vans, it recognizes the length and height (L2H1), design and number of seats, but only from explicit data, does not think anything from the name of the model. For the price, he can tell if it is with VAT, without VAT, subject to credit, or if it is only monthly payment — it is never sold at a price, and only a price that is really comparable can be compared with the market. For each data you can see how it was obtained and from what extract; when the ad contradicts itself, the app shows it as a contradiction and does not choose for me. Optional AI is allowed missing data only suggest with a verbatim excerpt, which the server validates in the text, and I have to save them.
  • Same car on multiple portals. The same car is often on three servers with different mileage and price. The app connects him with proof of identity only — the same VIN, registration number of the same dealer, or the same photo according to the entire address or the same entrance and another sign. At the same time, he keeps every ad custom price, link and verification time and the differences are not discarded: they are contradictions to consider. The same claim by one seller repeated on multiple portals there is no independent verification and the app calculates it that way. My manual decision "different cars" cannot be bypassed through the third ad, and the old automatic merges can be reconsidered — preview first, then confirmation.
  • A search that will admit what did not pass. I set the number of pages and details in my profile, and the details are loaded for the cars that best fit the profile, not according to the order of the server. The search is on on the background, shows progress and can be canceled — what was done remains. When the limit does not allow everything to go through, the app writes "5 of 8 pages loaded". Network outage, server congestion, blocking or site change never appear as "0 offers" or "sold" and are visible in the resource status. And when the raid changes for a well-known ad, the app reads a new detail or saves the new value as a contradiction with the date of both data — nothing disappears quietly.
  • Automatic VIN check. The control is established alone every time the offer gets into the app — live connector, AI agent result, embedded URL, CSV/JSON import and manual VIN completion. The queue is stored in the database so it survives a reboot; the same VIN in five ads means one check, not five.
  • Honest statuses instead of comforting zeroes. A timeout, deadlock, or parser error is never saved as "no record". And for "no finding in verified sources" it is directly written that it does not mean a flawless car. The page load date is never mistaken for the event date.
  • Resources according to reality, not according to wishes. Self-registration of advertisements runs automatically, NHTSA vPIC (it is honestly written that it decodes the VIN, but does not give the history) a Register of road vehicles of the Ministry of Transport — it has a public documented API and you just need to insert your own key into the application, which is free. Speedometer check, Czech Police search and manufacturer recall are behind CAPTCHA or login, so they remain manual step with a link and instructions — protections are not bypassed. None of this is paid.
  • Contradictions worth noting. Decreasing odometer reading between ads, two simultaneous ads of the same car with different mileage, year or make from VIN not matching the ad. On each is written what else it can be — by itself it is not proof of curl.
  • Blinds instead of writing. Make, model, generation, color and place of origin they choose (39 brands, 303 models, 79 cities). The model depends on the brand chosen and there is an escape option everywhere "other (write by hand)", so the dial is never blocked by an unusual car.
  • Data from the vehicle register by VIN. The Register of Road Vehicles (MD CR) has public documented API. The application itself reads from it the date of first registration, the number of owners and operators, the validity of the technical inspection, retained documents and the condition of the vehicle — and most importantly, it makes contradictions against the ad. For your own car, just enter the VIN and the form he fills in himself. Only empty fields are overwritten: what you wrote will remain and the difference will show as a difference. He doesn't think anything — the drive, transmission or drive is not registered in the register and it is written in the application; the year is marked as derived from first registration, not production.
  • AI search in one go. The application does not start the AI itself — it produces it entry, which you feed to Cloud or ChatGPT, and JSON with the result is returned into the box in the same window. The assignment has i output schema, so the answer fits right in and doesn't need to be corrected. After inserting, the app validates the result and switches directly to the results.
  • Search by license plate number. The brand is the only thing that can be seen on the car from the street and what people remember. According to her, the application finds what does he already have in his records about that car — offers and own cars — and when it knows their VIN, it will offer a history check. And above all, he does not hide the essentials: the vehicle register cannot query by license plate number. I verified this by actually querying their API: by VIN it returns data, by brand it returns empty. If the app kept it silent, the empty result would look like "verified, clean" — and that's the worst possible response. The mark is stored so that "1AB 2345" and "1ab-2345" are the same mark, and when inspected compares to what is on the car; another make is a finding of the highest severity, which sends me to check the VIN because it does not change.
  • I choose the AI ​​— and it's still just an add-on. In addition to Anthropic, applications can also Google Gemini, including the selection of a specific model. Each provider has its own key, so I won't lose the other one by switching. List of models se loads from the provider, not from a hard-written list — it would offer models that no longer exist in a few months. Without the key, nothing is offered and nothing is assumed. The key is always in the header of the request, never in the address (it is logged). And the main thing still applies: AI just explains the evidence, does not count scores or risks.
  • A backup that checks itself. The application holds a single copy of the data, so it backs them up once a day — but only if it has changed since the last backup. And most importantly: will verify each backup immediately. It opens it as a separate database, checks readability and compares record counts with live data; if it fails, the file deletes — a broken backup is worse than none because you rely on it. Status can be seen in the Health Center and failure is never passed over in silence. It is not an off-computer backup: it's on the same drive, so it protects against database corruption and deleted data, not disk failure — and the app says it outright.
  • Record of the visit. The foundation is one half, this is the other: what I actually found out about the car. The information in the ad is the seller's statement, the information from the registry is a record of the office — but I saw the state of the speedometer on the dashboard. Write down what I saw, and Contradictions against the ad are counted by themselves: different VIN on the car (then the entire history check was run on a different car), lower drive than in the ad (mileage cannot decrease), higher price on site, missing service book. Everything translates into risks with the car. There are points to be filled the same ones, which are printed in the background — and the default state is "not detected" not "ok". Application from it does not count discount or price: there is no basis for this and they would be made-up numbers.
  • Basis for inspection. For each car, the application will honestly write down what is missing — and he will make of those gaps questions to the seller a points to check at the car. It is written on everyone why: a point for no reason is just another bullet point. It just consists of what the app already knows and doesn't know about the car — missing data, risk findings, manual VIN verification steps, general model weaknesses. The points are ticked off and can be downloaded for printing, because you don't go on the tour with a laptop. It is not a tour or a review and it does not replace a physical inspection at the service station; the general weakness of the model is always marked for what it is — a place where it pays to look more closely in the given model, not an identified defect of a particular car.
  • VIN from photo. You take a picture of a tag or embossing and the app will offer what it read — you have to save it yourself after comparing with the photo. The recognition runs on your computer (OCR built into Windows), the photo is not sent anywhere and nothing is paid. The task is made easier by the fact that the VIN has a fixed shape: it does not have the letters I, O or Q (the standard omitted them precisely because of confusion with 1 and 0) and there is usually a check digit in the ninth position. But she there is no proof — European manufacturers often don't count it, so a mismatch doesn't mean a misread VIN. Most of the work was in making the module he didn't invent a VIN where there isn't one: the account number, invoice line and "STK 06/2027 KM 182350" also have seventeen characters, and the check digit is randomly hit approximately every eleventh attempt.
  • Selling a car: valuation of your own car. Your own car is registered and the application calculates for it from the offers it knows recommended advertising price, achievable spread and fast / patient selling variants. For each compared offer, you can see if it has been counted, and if not, so on why — wrecked, for parts, price subject to financing, price without VAT, duplicate or your own ad. The buyout estimate is not issued at all, because the application has nowhere to substantiate the purchase prices, and they are the basis tender prices, not proven sales — the missing ad does not prove a sale. Both the negotiation discount and the quick sale discount are granted, adjustable assumptions, not the result of measurement. The proposal of the ad will be created only from the registered data including known defects and the app won't send it anywhere.
  • Watchlist and Alerts. Price and availability tracking, history of changes, local alerts for new risk or price drop. Application she never contacts the seller herself, does not reserve anything and does not pay deposits.
  • Distance estimation without third party service. The distance to the seller is calculated from an offline table of cities and zip codes; verified against ten known routes with a deviation of up to 12%. It is always marked as an estimate, not an exact route.

Availability and current status

Beta version v0.13.8 (October 2026), 737 automated tests plus a visual inspection of screens, an interactive walkthrough of applications and a real test of upgrading from an older version. Fully standalone application — it only runs on my PC in its own window (pywebview), the data remains in local SQLite, it downloads updates silently from the home NAS. Private, no public hosting and no installer to download. It is not an expert opinion or a car inspection — it is a tool that sorts the offers and mainly shows what is documented and what is not.

What is only partially verified: retrieving the VIN from a photo is tested on one real mint photo and on rendered labels. But one real picture is not a statistic — different light, angle or glare may turn out differently. Two external resources are live: VIN decoding from NHTSA vPIC (not service history) a Register of road vehicles, which gives registration and technical data — not service history and not speedometer status. The registry response mapping is verified at once a real car; other cars may have other fields filled in. Checking the speedometer, searching for the Police of the Czech Republic and recall actions remain a manual step. Valuing your own car is worth it tender prices, not on documented sales, and therefore does not issue a purchase estimate at all. The new reading of the data from the advertisements (years, variant, type of price) is verified at fabricated sample advertisements, which I wrote myself — accuracy on actual advertisements is not measured. The new version of search has not yet run against the live web and the official mobile.de API is live unverified, until it has access data. Repeated failed start on one laptop has no confirmed cause — start is more durable since v0.13.1 and since v0.13.5 the app has its own diagnostic package, but the data from that computer has not yet arrived.
