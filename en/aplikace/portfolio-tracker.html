Why was it created and what can it do?

Accounting book I use Portfolio Tracker for overall financial records to track investments over time. The original Finance Analytik is now part of PT — so I have an overview of the portfolio and analysis of individual stocks in one application.

Transactions are the source of truth. Instead of manually typing "I have 10 stocks", the application keeps track of individual purchases, sales and dividends — and derives positions, average price and realized profit from them (FIFO). Thanks to this, taxes and history fit.

It loads statements from brokers itself. Just drag and drop CSV / XLSX / PDF from XTB, eToro, Trading 212 or Interactive Brokers — the application recognizes the broker, normalizes the format and does not duplicate repeated imports (print of each transaction). XTB accounts can be divided by currency (USD/EUR/CZK separately).

Taxes according to Czech rules. FIFO sales matching, 3-year time trial (exemption for long tenure) i value test § 4 (income from sales ≤ 100,000 CZK/year). The summary is always calculated in crowns, even if I have a portfolio in dollars (recalculated at the historical exchange rate on the trade date).

Performance and fair value. XIRR, CAGR, TWR, Volatility, Sharpe, Value Over Time Chart and monthly returns (year × month). For individual titles, 6 valuation models (DCF, Graham, Lynch, DDM, EV/EBITDA, Multiples), a screener with its own composite score, a sector heatmap and a calendar of results (earnings).

Data from public sources, keys only with me. Live prices from Yahoo Finance, fundamentals from SEC EDGAR and Finnhub, rates from CNB, insider trades and 13F reports from SEC, optional AI analyst via Gemini. Any API keys sit locally in an encrypted Windows vault — they never go in or out of the code. The entire app runs locally, no server can see my position.

He doesn't guess. This is the quality I value most in money: the missing data is marked as missing, it is not calculated by estimation. When the rate does not arrive for the position, the app does not value it with the purchase price and does not issue it as the market value — it writes that the price is missing. When the source does not report withholding tax for the dividend, it does not deduct it from the rate. A grading axis without data does not get a "middle mark", but it drops out and has a reason written next to it. Zero is an assertion, not padding.

It checks against the broker. For eToro, which doesn't issue trades through the API, the app will at least download the total account value and compare it to what it calculated itself — if the numbers diverge, it will say that a more recent statement is probably missing. The coverage semaphore, in turn, monitors how old the last imported statement is for each account. There is a preview for each import that lists in advance exactly what will be written, overwritten or lost.

Own analytical cuts. The "Analytical Canvas" is an array of blocks over my transactions — value breakdown by sector, fees, dividends, valuations. It consists of questions of the type Where is my money anyway? or How much does it cost me in fees? and each block acknowledges the data source, period and coverage, so you can see what the number came from.

He looks for quality companies and reviews what I hold. The "Stock Pick" page goes through a curated list of companies, my portfolio or watchlist and scores each company on six axes (financial health, growth, quality, competitive advantage, valuation, momentum) from annual SEC EDGAR reports and Yahoo awards. He always stands up to the score certainty — how many axis were really counted and how old the data is — and for each axis the proof "how we got there". Held positions will pass the same score; A "sell review candidate" only arises from deteriorating fundamentals, red flags and valuation, never from price movement alone. Appka does not trade anything and does not change anything in the portfolio — the only thing it can write is to add a candidate to the watchlist with a reason.

Detailed list of features
  • Management purchases and thesis revisions — Optional automatic alerts for documented CEO/CFO purchases and purchases by at least three different insiders within 30 days. The investment journal reminds you to revise theses for the portfolio and watchlist: own deadline, repeat, complete and postpone for seven days. No manual import, with the application open. The first full insider check creates a silent default; incomplete data will not produce a certain signal.
  • Position, P/L and Allocation — overview of portfolio value, unrealized and realized profit, breakdown by sector, type, position and currency, concentration monitoring.
  • Import from 4 brokers + live API — XTB, eToro, Trading 212, Interactive Brokers (CSV/XLSX/PDF); in addition live connection to IBKR via Flex Web Service, Trading 212 API and eToro Public API (read only — balances) with optional by automatically downloading transactions at startup. Idempotent dedup, broker → account → transaction model, XTB split by currency. The statement period is read from content of the file, not from its name — the order of day and month must not be disputed.
  • Czech taxes (FIFO) — 3-year time test and value test of CZK 100,000, an overview of how many days are left until exemption, tax loss harvesting (tax-loss harvesting with a distinction between eligible and exempted parts), export of trades for declaration. Indicative, not tax advice.
  • Performance — XIRR, CAGR, TWR, volatility, max drawdown, Sharpe, graph of value over time se by comparison against the S&P 500 / NASDAQ and monthly returns (capital gain by month).
  • Dividends — annual income and return by position, actual income after months (TTM + year-on-year comparison) and dividend calendar with projection 12 months ahead.
  • Fair value & screener — 6 Valuation Models, Model Switcher, Custom Composite Score, Beta and Analyst Consensus (Finnhub), P/E and Fwd P/E.
  • Stock selection (from 1.14, extended in 1.15 and 1.16) — score 0-100 on six axes with visible evidence, risk as penalty, 12 red flags with explanation, catalysts only from statements, fair value bear/base/bull with admitted assumptions and deterministic Czech thesis. Same engine review of held positions (hold / review / sell review candidate), 2-5 firm comparison, 11-section detail including sources, data age, and SEC filing links. Universe is an honestly named curated list of 66 companies, not an index. Read-only to the portfolio, no trade orders. From 1.15 in addition: statements of the predecessor for the successor registrant, FFO/AFFO for real estate companies only from documented items (price / FFO instead of P/E), peer percentiles within the run, P/E history for 5 fiscal years and a CIK map that will survive the restart. From 1.16: universe of composition of the fund (CSV holdings from iShares or Invesco that I download myself — the app doesn't scrape and doesn't call it an index) and an estimate in advance of how many titles and megabytes the run will download.
  • Heatmap, calendar, AI analyst — portfolio sector heatmap, results calendar and AI analyst (Gemini) over portfolio data — all with the disclaimer that this is not an investment recommendation.
  • Market & news directly in the app — inline reports (portfolio and the entire market), Market Pulse (VIX, Fear & Greed, Sectors, Biggest Moves) a macro calendar of economic events — all from public data for free, without a paid API.
  • Notice board and analytical canvas — a custom deck of cards from other sites and a canvas of analysis blocks that can be switched by question ("Where exactly is my money?", "How much is it costing me in fees?"). The layout is saved only in the browser, it does not move the portfolio data.
  • Portfolio health and signals — a composite score of seven axes (diversification, valuation, risk, quality, income, sentiment, factors). An axis without data is not counted — falls out and it has a written reason why the score does not look better than the basis for it.
  • Check against the broker — with eToro, the total value of the account is downloaded via its API and compared with its own calculation; a difference of more than 5% indicates that a more recent statement is probably missing. Traffic light coverage monitors the age of the last statement for each account.
  • Course for tax return according to § 38 ZDP — the CNB daily exchange rate or the GFŘ uniform exchange rate, to be chosen and documented; appka downloads the annual exchange rates from the CNB itself and reports when the uniform exchange rate for the given year has not yet been announced.
  • Corporate event — splits, spin-offs and transfers between accounts like events about paper, ne obchody. The lots will retain their original date and purchase price, so the three-year time test will not be interrupted at all. Without an exact time, the event is marked as "uncertain order" — the app doesn't guess what happened first.
  • Cash to be admitted — separately documented balance from broker to date and separately recalculation to date from movements after that date; the derived number is never saved as a document. Therefore, the monthly statement does not overwrite the balance (the sum of the window is not the account status), but the cash is added from it — and if there is a period missing between the balance and the statement, the app will tell you.
  • Withholding tax on dividends with reference to the regulation — credit ceiling according to country of the payer and agreements on avoidance of double taxation, collection number and article for each rate. A country with which there is no treaty (eg the Cayman Islands) will remain unsubstantiated — to substitute the "ordinary 15%" would be to invent a claim. In addition, W-8BEN records including validity.
  • Backups, snapshots and recovery — kompletní JSON záloha (volitelně i s nastavením a klíči, nebo bez tajných údajů do archivu), obnova přepsáním i sloučením, bezpečnostní snímky před každou automatickou změnou dat a stránka Zdraví systému s diagnostikou.
  • Investment diary and goals — notes on decisions for individual titles and investment goals with projection; at the target, the app calculates the necessary monthly amount, but only if I enter the expected return myself — substituting "usual 8%" would make a made-up number a plan.
  • Portfolio right after launch — the app remembers the last known prices and rates, so values and totals are visible immediately, and fresh prices are only updated in the background. But the stored price she doesn't look alive: has its own tag with the date, and for a price older than a day, the daily change is not counted at all. To that tax notices — the upcoming three-year time trial can be seen on the main screen, not until taxes — a projection both dividend income and financial independence, both honestly as a model above given assumptions, not as a prediction.
  • Stocks and ETFs separate from crypto — each instrument has a product type by broker (equity, ETF, ETC/ETN, directly held cryptocurrency, CFD), underlying exposure and section; crypto ETFs remain among securities, leveraged CFDs are not taken as stocks, and the type, which the source does not specify, turns out to be unverified — not like stocks. You can see the source and reason for each inclusion.
  • Tax profile and document import — the profile (residence, employee, private investments) is always valid for one year and only with explicit confirmation; there is an exemption as well as an indicative assessment of whether to file a return. The app reads tax returns (XML from the Financial Administration portal) and W-8BEN forms from brokers (PDF) by itself, shows what is entered, and saves it until confirmation. A scan that cannot be read will be honestly rejected.
  • Risk only from new market valuations — volatility, Sharpe and the risk axis of the portfolio evaluation are calculated only from the days when the valuation actually recovered. A day when the last known price was just repeated or the position was valued at the buy price is not a new observation and merges with the next interval. As long as there are at least 21 such observations, appka the risk does not issue and writes what is missing — instead of a score built on a stationary line.
  • Crypto and options as separate modules — custom records with import by file content from Tastytrade, IBKR, Anycoin, CoinBank, Binance, Trezor, Exodus, OKX and Etherscan; matching of transfers between own wallets as proposal for confirmation, expiration of options, checking of data and annual economic realization with historical exchange rate. A position without a price is "unpriced", not zero. The app does not yet calculate the tax rules of crypto and options — and it says so.
  • Crypto market overview — 250 coins from CoinGecko without key (Demo key optional in Windows vault), heatmap, filters, coin detail, charts up to the whole history with real candles, my own trades directly on the chart and risk (volatility, Sharpe, correlation with BTC or S&P 500).
  • Portfolio reduction — I enter the target number of positions and those that I definitely want to hold; The AI ​​(Gemini) will propose successive full closes from the technical background and fundamentals, and the app will check the proposal against the instruments actually held. Nothing trades and the plan becomes invalid when positions are changed. In addition, a performance comparison with SPY in the same period and currency.
  • Data directly from the SEC (from 1.39 and 1.40) — My companies they monitor changes in US companies' filings, quarterly results and management outlooks. For US ETFs, the app from the fund's quarterly report (N-PORT) breaks down which companies I actually hold in them and shows the overlap with direct stocks as well as the actual concentration. The Fund's Expenditure (TER) takes it from its annual report, compares the dividend per share with the company's annual report (10-K) and, for the stocks held, highlights a new large shareholder, announced insider sales or delisting. European funds do not report to the SEC — the app will tell them, not imagine.
  • Crypto and options in taxes, targets by country, warnings — indicative partial base according to § 10 ZDP for crypto and options: each type separately, exempt income even with its expenses outside the base, loss is not counted with another type. I also enter the target allocation according to countries and sectors (the unspecified part is not included in the targets) and price alerts can be monitored in the background and announced by a Windows notification.
  • No account, no cloud, 6,921 tests — all local (IndexedDB, with an insurance backup before each risk step), data does not leave the device. Under the hood, 6,921 automatic tests and a control valuation layer (self-verification of calculations against independent implementation). I am verifying the corrections mutations — if I temporarily revert the fix, the test must fail; otherwise it proves nothing.
