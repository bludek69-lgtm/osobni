Why was it created and what can it do?

I built it because I got tired of switching between the internet bank, brokerage websites and Excel. The application can process 25 different resources — bank and payment accounts, brokers including options, crypto exchanges and wallets, P2P platforms, precious metals and pension savings. Instead of logging into each platform separately, everything is on one page: assets, cash, investments, debts and net worth — and for each number, the app can show which lines it was created from.

She will find a job herself. Documents that are not yet in the book can be found by itself in monitored folders and in the e-mail box (a small monitor on the Raspberry Pi downloads attachments from Gmail and the List and is set directly in the application; documents are downloaded from it to every computer with the application). What is unequivocal, he accounts for; what not, she will present to me with a Czech sentence why she left it up to me. Twice a day, she recalculates the reports herself, compares the names and currencies of the accounts according to the statements, projects the extraordinary loan repayment into the principal amount and handles the queue of lines for decision. When I start it after a week, it catches up on the missed runs right after the start.

Nothing is calculated by estimation. The account balance is a document from the statement, not the sum of movements. The exchange rate is the CNB exchange rate as of the date. A tax rule has a paragraph, an official source and a date of verification — a historical transaction is assessed according to the law of its year. Where a document is missing or the law does not provide an answer, the app prefers not to issue a number and write why. Every important calculation has an independent "shadow" calculation next to it that watches over it.

No cloud, no data out. The database is a single SQLite file on my Windows. A complete backup carries the entire system (database, documents, settings) and is only encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with API keys. The book remains on the local computer; the home NAS is only used for silent updates (verified by a checksum) and for a one-way backup, which is always stored encrypted.

Detailed list of features

The application has 14 modules in the main menu and around 150 screens. This is a selection based on what I do most often in it.

  • Overview — assets, cash, investments and net worth on one page; graphs of portfolio and net worth development for 1 year, 2 years or the whole period, portfolio distribution with a breakdown of "where and how much" after clicking, tiles of financial reserve and limits, semaphore of up-to-date statements (which account is missing a statement and since when), loan coverage by crypto value, cash-flow outlook and financial independence.
  • Imports — 25 resources with no code intervention — bank statements (PDF, CSV and API), payment accounts, XTB, eToro, Trading 212, Interactive Brokers, options broker, crypto exchanges as well as hardware and software wallets, P2P platforms, precious metals, pension, pay stubs. The wizard tells you exactly which export to download. The bank statement is self-verified before posting (opening balance + movements = closing balance) and the mismatched statement is rejected in its entirety. The same file won't write anything a second time.
  • Matching Center — transfers between own accounts, bank ↔ broker, bank ↔ crypto exchange, payment ↔ invoice, salary ↔ pay slip, installment ↔ loan. Documented pairs (variable symbol, counter account, contract number) match themselves; matching a mere amount is not enough. Each gear leg has a status, or a written reason why the pair does not.
  • Invoices and regular payments — received invoices with supplier and account, standing orders confirmed from the history with one click, expected payments in the calendar and in the cash-flow outlook. The supplier can change the account and the variable symbol, and the app will recognize that it is the same company. QR payment of the invoice by phone (the app never pays by itself).
  • Institutions and Accounts — one directory of institutions, companies, my accounts and counterparty accounts. The book distinguishes between my account, counterparty account, broker client number and position held; compares the name and currency of the account according to the headers of the statements. The detail of the institution shows accounts, cards, savings account envelopes, loans and connection status.
  • Stocks and ETFs — positions and cash at all brokers, FIFO with corporate actions (split, merger, symbol change), dividends and withholding tax by country, performance, allocation, dividend calendar, targets, portfolio health. A ticker is not an identity — the paper is identified by its ISIN.
  • Crypto — holdings by coins and wallets, lots and FIFO, own transfers between the exchange and the wallet connected to the chain (the transfer is not a new purchase), on-chain synchronization of the hardware wallet from the public key (addresses are derived locally, the balance fits with the official application of the wallet on the last digit), reconciliation "the difference is zero or has a named cause", tax footprint from sales through lots to paragraph, average acquisition price, how much would be left to repay the loan after the actual sale of the crypto.
  • Loans — evidence from the bank's payment calendars (it sits with the bank for a penny), extraordinary installments and early top-up from the statement, "what to pay off first" strategy, refinancing, turning point "pay off or invest" instead of a verdict. Drawing up a loan is not income, refinancing is neutral on both sides.
  • Taxes — a separate module with 15 sub-pages above the register of 75 tax rules (each with a section, official source and date of verification), tax audit with the resulting tax and the return conclusion. Value test CZK 100,000 and a ceiling of 40 million from total income, three-year time test, dividends, options, crypto, P2P, wages. Document for accountants as ZIP (XLSX + CSV + PDF) with checksum — it's a document, not a return; an independent shadow engine recalculates each year.
  • Retirement and financial independence — pension from a certified annual statement plus contributions from pay slips, records of periods from the CSSS (spaces are added), pre-retirement outlook. Financial independence separates FIRE capital from net worth (reserve, pension and illiquid P2P are not counted), the goal is based on real necessary expenses, stochastic simulation with seed, stress scenarios and optimizer levers with real projection.
  • Analytics and Outlook — projections of cash, cash flow, assets, debts and net worth for 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20 years; scenarios, simulations, probability of targets, sensitivity to inputs and required investment. Saved scenarios will show that they are out of date after changing the book. It's a model, not a prediction; if the input is missing, the application will say what instead of outputting zero.
  • Bank cards and counter account tracing — cards, card payments and merchants with rules that I confirm; the list of public accounts of institutions with an official source works without the Internet, and an unknown counter-account can be found on the web with just a click, with a preview of what will leave.
  • Cash-flow outlook, reserve, goals, risks — 12 months ahead from the contractual calendar, regular payments and documented balances (each month's account identity is measured); rezerva z pravidelných výdajů posledních uzavřených měsíců; financial goals by priority; rizika DSTI, DTI, likvidita, koncentrace — chybějící vstup je „nelze“, nikdy nula.
  • Gold and crowdfunding — investment coins with spot price and documented price range from invoices, Zlaté konto calculated from bank payments and invoices, P2P platforms with value from statement and breakdown after investments.
  • Cash and audit — one ledger from which all views go; check between each pair of documents "balance + movements = next balance"; the difference is called the amount, not the color.
  • Storage without surprises — every form shows "Unsaved changes → Saving → Saved / Error", exit with unsaved change will ask, saving will start a visible recalculation of subsequent data with phases, and the screens will redraw without F5. Fixing an old line propagates itself to all affected domains (impact graph, 13 domains in fixed order), never recomputing the entire book unnecessarily.
  • System: health, engines, operations — "What's missing" with one of six verbs (Complete, Synchronize, Import, Pair, Check, Fix); 20 application engines with human condition and solution site reference (an unmeasured engine never pretends to be fine); reconciliation that the modules sit on a dime with each other; history of operations and recovery of incomplete conversion; incident diagnostics with a printout of the problem instead of a white screen.
  • Backup, transfer and uninstall — kompletní záloha celého systému po částech s ověřením, automatická šifrovaná záloha na domácí NAS (jednosměrná, obnova ručně), přenos API klíčů jen v šifrované obálce, obnova nahráním po částech s náhledem, úplná odinstalace i s daty (externí zálohy se nikdy nemažou). Silent update from home NAS with SHA-256 checksum.
  • Ctrl+K Quick Search — finds over 130 pages without diacritics and by path; destructive actions cannot even be written into the palette.
