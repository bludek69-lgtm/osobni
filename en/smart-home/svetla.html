🏠 Experience · Lights and lighting

Lights which they know who is in the room

The lights adapt to movement in the apartment and to how much light is outside. The system also takes into account sleep and shading, so that the light does not disturb the moment when there should be peace.

Technical details

Two sensors for open space — mmWave radar for a quiet person, classic PIR for movement. Either one or the other. Never both false. A 4-layer pipeline runs above the sensors, which ultimately switches the capacity of the real light bulb — with privacy guard, sleep guard and triple-sensor anti-flicker logic.

🧍 What it does in practice

The lights adjust according to the household mode, the stage of the day and the presence. The atmosphere is different in the morning than in the evening.

What does it do?

Lights that respect context

There are two sensors in the open space (dining room + kitchen): Aqara FP2 (mmWave radar — sees even a sitting person, micro-movements, breathing) and classic Z-wave PIR Fibaro (just movement). The rule is simple — the light stays on as long as any of the sensors report presence. It only turns off when both say "there's nothing here".

But there is no setCapability above the sensors. It is in between 4-layer pipeline: request → priority engine (TTS has priority 100, lights 60 — TTS overrides) → light router → physical write. When the priority engine says "no", the lights wait until the speech is finished.

And two more rules above the pipeline: privacy guard (windows are visible from the street when the blind is over 20% = see in from outside → open space lights prohibited) and sleep guard (when I sleep, no light can be turned on — not even on motion).

Daily operation

How the lights react during the day

The lux sensor decides, the presence sensor confirms, privacy and sleep have the right of veto.

  1. In the morning

    I will get up, I will not break my sleep

    The motion sensor in the kitchen detects movement. Sleep state is already unset (sleep brain detected awakening). Lux below the threshold → kitchen lights on in dim mode, open space waiting for FP2 confirm.

  2. Day

    Lux drives, not motion

    At lux above 400 (daylight sufficient), the lights stay off even if someone walks by. They turn on when lux is below 120 and presence is true. Hysteresis prevents flickering around the threshold.

  3. Evening

    Privacy guard activates

    When it's dark outside and the blind is over 20% — The sector, the dining room table and the LED strip MUST NOT be turned on. The safety interlock will auto-close the blind at 0.15 and release the lights.

  4. Night

    Sleep guard locks everything

    Sleep state = active → priority engine returns false on light request. No motion, no FP2 vibration will turn on the light. Movement in the bathroom has its own gentle path (bathroom router).

Scenes

Five situations, five behaviors

No explicit button — lights are decided by sensor + context.

Entrance to the apartment

FP2 detects presence → light_request='on' → priority engine allow → router → setCap. At lux below 120 dim to 40%, otherwise full.

Privacy guard

Blind above 20% and is night → safety interlock blocks light request. If the user explicitly wants to → auto-close the shutter to 0.15 and release the light.

Luxury car

Lux > 400 → lights remain off even in presence. Lux < 120 → they turn on. Meanwhile, hysteresis (config: sh_cfg_lux_open_space_on/off).

Sleep silent

sh_spim='yes' → priority engine denied for light request. The movement is logged, but the physical action does not occur. Bathroom has its own rules.

Stuck recovery

When sh_priority_active='tts' over a minute → brain guardian detects and resets to 'none'. The lights will start responding again.

Hardware

What really shines and what makes the decision

Two sensors + three groups of lights + one blind that plays a role in the privacy logic.

Aqara FP2 (SNZB-06P)

mmWave radar · presence · Zigbee

It also sees a sitting person, micro-movements and breathing. "Open space" zone (dining room + kitchen). Capabilities alarm_presence. False can flash to False for a minute — hence the triple-sensor guard at presence_off.

Fibaro Motion Sensor

PIR · motion · Z-wave

Classic PIR. It detects motion (change in IR), not a still person. Capabilities alarm_motion. Acts as a guard for presence_off — when motion is still true, presence_off is ignored.

Sector 1

Open space ceiling · WiFi (Tuya)

The main open space ceiling light. Controlled via setCapabilityValue('onoff', ...). Disabled in privacy mode (roller on top) — directly visible from the outside into the apartment.

Dining table 1 + LED strip

Desktop + ambient · WiFi

Table lamp above the dining table + LED strip behind the TV. The same privacy guard as Sektorka. When the lux is below the threshold, they turn on with presence, otherwise they are off.

Fyrtur 1 blind

IKEA · Zigbee · windowcoverings_set

It plays a dual role — physical shielding and a binary privacy signal for the light router. Position above 20% = "open" for privacy purposes. With safety interlock, it auto-closes at 0.15.

For the tech-savvy

What's going on under the hood

Pipeline, dual-sensor logic, triple-sensor guard, privacy thresholds.

4-layer pipeline (request → priority → router → setCap)
  1. Request: the flow sensor sets sh_light_request='on'/'off'. This is where the sensor path ends — no setCap yet.
  2. Priority engine (sh_priority_engine_v1): will decide if light can win. When TTS runs, it returns false and the flow pipeline ends.
  3. Router (sh_light_router_v1): applies privacy guard, sleep guard, lux check. Returns a specific device + capability + value.
  4. setCap: physical write via Homey API ( setCapabilityValue('onoff', ...)) to a specific device from the device map.

Before 25.4. there was a bug — the pipeline ended at priority_engine, the router was not called. Fix: existing flow "SH – Priority – Light" extended by 2. action chained to outputSuccess. No new flow (REUSE FIRST).

Dual-sensor logic (FP2 + Z-wave)

The rule: lights ON if FP2.presence === true OR motion.motion === true. OFF only when FP2.presence === false AND motion.motion === false.

  • FP2 ON → flow "SH – OpenSpace – Presence ON" → router
  • FP2 OFF → flow "SH – OpenSpace – Presence OFF" with 15 min delay → router
  • Z-wave motion → flow "SH – Kitchen AI – Motion ON/OFF" (only kitchen lights, NOT open space)

Z-wave does NOT send events directly to the open space router. It is read as a guard from the script when presence_off.

Triple-sensor guard for presence_off (v1.18)

FP2 sometimes "flashes" to False (cooldown / sensitivity). 15 min delay → presence_off. At that moment, the user was already back. The lights weren't supposed to go out. Fix: 

if (normalized === 'off') {
  if (motion.alarm_motion === true) → keep on (off_ignored_motion_active)
  if (presence.alarm_presence === true) → keep on (off_ignored_presence_active)
  if (max(open_presence_ts, open_motion_ts) > now - 5min) → keep on (off_ignored_recent_5min)
}
Privacy guard (blind > 20% = open)

When the blind is over sh_cfg_blind_safety_threshold (default 0.2 / 20%), direct view from the street into the living room. Open lights (Sector, Table, LED strip) are prohibited in that situation.

Security interlock with light request with privacy fail:

  1. Auto-closes the blind on sh_cfg_blind_privacy_position (0,15)
  2. The light is blocked during the closing time
  3. After reaching the position, it will release light and setCap normally

v1.13 had an inverted comparison (< place >) — a user-closed blind was considered "open" and blocked the lights. Fixed v1.14.

Stuck priority bug (TTS gate)

When the TTS pipeline does not complete (network glitch, speaker disconnect, ...), sh_priority_active='tts' will remain stuck. Light request priority 60 < TTS 100 → priority_engine returns false → light_router is never called → darkness even in presence.

  • Detection: active='tts' AND result='allow' AND age > 1 min = stuck
  • Recovery: brain guardian resets to none/idle
  • Long-term fix: TTS script must call priority reset on completion
