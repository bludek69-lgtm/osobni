05 — Equipment

What does the system control?

Overview of lights, sensors, heating, speakers and other parts of my apartment. Devices are divided according to what they are used for; you can find details in the individual categories.

Technical details

Devices divided into functional categories. Each category has its own router and its own rules.

Lights

Open space + bedroom + office. Multi-signal: FP2 presence + Z-Wave motion. Privacy mode (roller top = open space LED off). Sunrise simulation when waking up in the morning.

Heating

FGS-214 + 3× TRV. Three modes (auto / manual / off), state-aware hysteresis, anti-cycling, runtime-based kWh tracking. Planned: AI layers (preWakeBoost, weatherAdjust, occupancyBonus).

Audio

Cast speakers + TTS pipeline with 200-char split (Google Cast hard limit). Sleep-respect (no TTS at night), priority engine, radio router, briefing every morning via Gemini.

Sensors

FP2 mmWave presence, Z-Wave motion, door/window contacts, lux, temperature, humidity, PM2.5. All data enters the decision layer (request-first pipeline).

Cameras

Indoor + outdoor. Stream via Homey, automatic switching during departure / upon alarm trigger. Live preview on the dashboard at the entrance.

Blinds

Lux-driven + home mode + shutter_router v3. Hysteresis for stable control, privacy guard for open space, morning/evening time windows.

AI / Diagnostics

Brain Guardian, state validator, expected state engine, release guard, self-healing engine, daily Gemini analysis. A meta-layer above the entire system.
