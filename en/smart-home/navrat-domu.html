When I come home

Arrival at home is recognized by the system based on several signals. It then adjusts the home mode and related functions, such as heating or sound. Below I describe the sequence of steps and the check that prevents false arrival.

Technical details

Multi-signal arrival pipeline — from the moment the car enters a radius of 100 m around the house, until the moment the house is fully switched to home mode. 30 seconds, 4 signals, 5 subsystems, no explicit command. The other side of the coin compared to the morning routine — not a planned ritual, but a reaction to user appearance.

30 sTotal sync time
4Signals in multi-confirm
100 mGeofence radius
5 minutesMotion confirmation window

Context

Monday 14:30, return from work

Status before: sh_rezim_doma='pryc' from 04:10 in the morning (after the morning routine). Heating preset away — all zones 16 °C target. Lights blocked via priority engine. Audio idle. The robot vacuum cleaner started the planned cleaning at 14:00, it is currently finishing (~70% progress).

User: journey from Liberec, 14:25 arrives on the street, parks, goes to the apartment, opens the door. No phone in hand, no "home" button, no NFC tag. He simply came. The system itself decides when and what to switch.

Pipeline target: know that it is indeed a user return home, not a fake geofence trigger (neighbors, delivery man, driving by). Therefore, multi-signal — geofence + door + motion in a 5 min window. Single-signal too often false positive.

Timeline

30 seconds from entry to sync

Each milestone recorded in the EventLog with a timestamp.

  1. 14:24:00

    Geofence outside

    Car on the street, GPS location 200 m from the house. Mobile Homey app reports geofence='outside'. sh_rezim_doma='pryc' still active, all quiet. The robot vacuum cleaner continues cleaning.

  2. 14:24:30

    Geofence approaching

    GPS shift within a 100 m radius around the house (radius config: sh_cfg_geofence_radius_m). Mobile app fire geofence_enter event Homey flow trigger → sh_arrival_pending='yes', waiting for further signals. Multi-signal counter = 1/3.

  3. 14:24:30

    Robot vacuum cleaner graceful stop

    Geofence enter event starts the flow "SH – Robot – Pause on arrival" — the robot receives a pause command, returns to the dock. The user does not come to the door with a starting vacuum cleaner. Cleaning will auto-resume in the evening (after sleep block expiration).

  4. 14:24:55

    Door contact open

    Door/window contact on the front door → alarm_contact='open'. Flow "SH – Door – Front" → sh_arrival_pending still active, counter = 2/3. Way-finding light in the hall auto-on (motion + door open + lux below the threshold) for 60 s.

  5. 14:25:02

    Motion in the hall

    PIR motion in the hall → alarm_motion=true. Multi-signal counter = 3/3 (geofence + door + motion in 5 min window). Confirm threshold met — this is a real user arrival, not a false positive.

  6. 14:25:02

    Home preset cascade

    sh_rezim_doma='doma' a hundred Cascade into subsystems: sh_heating_preset='home', sh_audio_request='idle' (no auto-radio, but unblock TTS), sh_priority_active='none' (light router released).

  7. 14:25:05

    Heating reactive

    Scheduler cron next tick (within 5 min) will recalculate targets according to the current schedule slot (afternoon comfort): dining room 21 °C, bedroom 19 °C, bathroom 22 °C, toilet 18 °C. Demand cron calculates the delta from current (16+ °C because away) → boiler_request='on'. The boiler starts for ~20 min comeback.

  8. 14:25:08

    FP2 presence confirmed

    After moving from the hall to the kitchen, FP2 is activated in the open space. Lux check (cold, but daylight OK ~250 lux) — above "off" threshold (400) no, but above "on" threshold (120) yes. Border zone, hysteresis keeps lights off.

  9. 14:25:25

    Optional: Comfort suggested

    The AI coordinator evaluates the situation = arrival_idle (came home, no immediate task). Option level 4 suggest: "Hello, today was a cold day, do you want to start a relaxation scene?". TTS waits 30 seconds for cube tap accept.

  10. 14:25:30

    Sync done

    All subsystems in home mode. Brain Guardian next cron 14:30 will check consistency. Energy tracking will re-start (boiler runtime today += 0 calculated, the boiler is now running again — the runtime is incremented from the next demand cron).

  11. 14:55:00

    Heating reached comfort

    After 30 min of heating (dining room 16 → 21 °C, delta 5 °C, 9 kW boiler) zone within hysteresis. The boiler turns off. Runtime today += ~0.5 h × 9 kW = 4.5 kWh for the afternoon comeback (the winter outside worsens the accuracy estimate).

What if

Variants of the scenario

What happens in edge cases — and what actually happened.

Delivery man

Geofence enter = 1/3, motion in hall = 2/3 (knocking), DOOR_OPEN no (only opens after 30 seconds of silence). Multi-signal fails → arrival_pending expires in 5 min → away remains. It works.

Driving around

Geofence enter = 1/3, but GPS leaves the circuit in 1-2 min. Geofence_exit event before motion confirm → counter reset, arrival_pending cleared. No false positives.

Returning from a walk

No car, just walking. Geofence works the same (mobile GPS), door + motion the same. Pipeline neutral to the means of transport — only the final arrival home matters.

Car phone (dead)

No geofence signal. Door + motion = 2/3, geofence = 0. Multi-signal fails — but arrival_pending counter has a tolerant rule: 2/3 + door open = arrival high confidence (alternate path).

Return through storms

GPS noise can cause geofence_enter / exit to blink. Pipeline has a debounce of 5s on geofence transitions. Multiple re-entries within 1 min are counted as 1 event.

For the tech-savvy

What's going on under the hood

Multi-signal counter (why 3 signals, not 1)

Single-signal arrival is unusable because:

  • Geofence GPS has a spread of 30-50 m → passers-by, neighbors, driving by = false trigger
  • Door open only = delivery person, inspection of the gas company, visit
  • Motion only = robot vacuum cleaner, cat next door (vibration through the wall)

By combining 3 out of 3 signals within a 5 min window, we get high confidence. Tolerance rule:

  • 3/3 → high confidence, immediate cascade
  • 2/3 + door open → medium confidence, cascade after 30 s additional confirm
  • 2/3 without door → low confidence, pending continues
  • 1/3 → ignored, cleared after 5 min expiration
Cascade order (why heating first, robot second)

After home preset confirm, subsystems switch in deterministic order:

  1. Heating (priority highest) — has the greatest inertia, ~30 min for a comeback from away
  2. Audio gate — release of TTS for arrival comfort suggest
  3. Light gate — release lighting (closed-by-sleep = false)
  4. Robot — pause + return to dock
  5. Brain Guardian — re-evaluate state coherence

Heating first because the slowest. When the user arrives, the house is cold (away 16 °C), comfort 21 °C lasts ~30 min. Robot second because less time sensitive.

Robot vacuum cleaner graceful stop

Without this, the robot would continue cleaning during user arrival → collision, noisy, unpleasant. Implementation: 

// flow "SH – Robot – Pause on arrival"
trigger: geofence_enter event
action 1: setCap robot.vacuum_cleaner_state = 'docked'
action 2: log event 'robot_paused_on_arrival'
action 3: schedule resume @ next_evening_after_sleep_off

Pause does not mean cancel — cleaning resumes in the evening after sleep gate release. State remembers progress, resumes from the interrupted point.

Heating comeback without overshoot

30 min cold house → 21 °C target = 9 kW non-stop ~30 min. Without safety:

  • Naive heating: boiler 100% until reaching target → over-shoot → swing 21 → 23 → 21 → 19 cycle 2 h
  • With hysteresis: target 21 °C, hyst_warm 0.1, hyst_cold 0.5. The boiler switches off at 21.1 °C, switches on again up to 20.5 °C. After the first stabilization cycle.
  • Plus safety cap: max runtime 4 h at a time → forced off (if runtime estimate bug → defense in depth).
