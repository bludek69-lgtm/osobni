Protocols used

ProtocolNumber of devicesFor what / what runs on it
cloud (web)1Yr.no weather forecast feed (text).
Google Cast (Wi-Fi)3Google Nest Mini / Hub Max — TTS, briefing, audio scenes.
Matter9New standard via Homey Matter bridge. Meross plug, MOES plug + virtual RGBTW bulbs.
Wi-Fi6Appliances (cleaner, vacuum cleaner, HP printer, Foscam cameras, AndroidTV).
Wi-Fi (Cloud)8Shelly Plug S via cloud relay (Pro series). Metered sockets + Shelly Pro 3EM energy monitor.
Wi-Fi (HA)2Home Assistant bridge — Sun + Person device for presence.
Z-Wave11Stable mesh for critical equipment — TRV head, Fibaro motion, boiler relay.
Zigbee39Mesh network, battery + USB hub. Mainly sensors, buttons, some lights, Sonoff Zigbee.
Zigbee (Hue Bridge)5Philips Hue bulbs via Hue Bridge.
