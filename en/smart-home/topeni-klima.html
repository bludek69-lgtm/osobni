🏠 Experience · Heating and climate

Heating which anticipates and saves

Heating has its own schedules for individual parts of the apartment. It takes into account presence at home, sleep and outside temperature; he can prepare a warmer bathroom before getting up. Below are the rules that govern them.

Technical details

Electric boiler 9 kW, three zone TRV with its own weekly schedule, three modes and three AI layers. Instead of a thermostat on the wall, a script makes a decision every two minutes — depending on whether I'm currently sleeping, how many degrees it is outside, and whether the alarm clock rings in half an hour.

🧍 What it does in practice

The system monitors household mode, presence and temperature zones. Thanks to this, it does not heat up unnecessarily and at the same time maintains comfort.

⚙️ Under the hood

The logic runs via HomeyScript, zone TRV, state variables, hysteresis and local logging to SQLite.

What does it do?

Heating that is not just a thermostat

Each room has its own radiator head — dining room, bedroom, bathroom, toilet — and each has its own weekly schedule. Different temperatures in the morning, different during the day, different at night. But the boiler does not turn on according to the timer: it turns on when one of the zones drops below the desired temperature by more than half a degree, and turns off when it exceeds the target by a tenth.

They work over schedule three AI layers. Pre-wake boost warms up the bathroom five to thirty minutes before the alarm clock. Weather adjust adds an extra notch when it drops below -5°C outside. The Occupancy bonus adds half a level to where someone is. All with a global ceiling of ±3°C to keep the AI ​​from going crazy and drowning in belief.

And three modes: CAR (schedule + AI), MANUAL (just a schedule, no tricks) a OFF (anti-freeze 7 °C). Switching with one button on the dashboard.

Daily operation

What it looks like on a normal day

Four phases, four behaviors — all autonomous, no thermostat on the wall.

  1. 02:45

    Morning pre-boost

    Thirty minutes before the alarm clock (3:15 a.m. during the week, 5:15 a.m. at the weekend) the bathroom rises by +2 °C. Toilet by +1 °C in the window 5–15 min before The goal: to enter a warm room in the morning, not a cold one.

  2. 05:00

    The day goes on

    Schedule slot "comfort" — dining room 21 °C, bedroom 19 °C, bathroom 22 °C, toilet 18 °C. The boiler switches on every now and then for 5-15 minutes depending on the heat loss in the room. Hysteresis keeps the swing in the 0.6°C band.

  3. 22:00

    Night damping

    Schedule switches to night slot — dining room 18 °C, bedroom 17 °C (cooler for sleep), bathroom 19 °C, toilet 16 °C. In the meantime, the sleep manager will cancel any boost preset that might have been running before.

  4. Permanently

    Winter compensation

    When it drops below -5°C outside, the weather layer adds +1°C to all zones. When it is above 15 °C, on the contrary, it takes -0.5 °C. It reacts to the forecast from yr.no (Homey Weather Forecast app, location of the apartment), not until ex post according to the internal sensor.

Scenes

Five actions, five buttons

Some start presence detection or an alarm clock, others go manually from the dashboard.

I'm leaving

Geofence detects the exit → preset = away → all zones drop to 16 °C. Returning home will automatically return the home preset from the current schedule slot.

Boost 30 min

"I'm cold" button — immediately +2 °C for 30 min in the current zone. After expiration, it will automatically return to the schedule slot, no manual shutdown.

I'm going to sleep

Sleep brain sends a signal → preset boost is canceled (if it was running), schedule night slot takes over. No explicit "turn off the heating".

Pre-wake

It depends on the alarm time from the mobile phone. The bathroom is preheated in the window 5–30 min before, the toilet 5–15 min. At the weekend shift to 5:15 according to dayOfWeek.

I turn everything off

Mode = off, anti-freeze 7 °C. The boiler will only turn on if there is a real danger of pipes freezing. For vacation or a long trip in winter.

Hardware

What really heats

One boiler, one Z-Wave relay, three thermo heads, one 3-phase meter.

Protherm RAY 9 KE

Electric boiler · 9 kW · 3-phase

The main source of heat. Modulated power 3 / 6 / 9 kW according to request. In winter, it runs in 4-hour windows per day for approx. 30-60 min/h, in transition only 5-15 min/h. Old school but reliable.

Fibaro FGS-214

Z-Wave Single Switch · boiler switching element

The only control that Homey directly controls. It switches on the boiler via the control input — it does not release the power phases, it only gives the "heat/not heat" signal. The boiler solves the 3 / 6 / 9 kW switching itself by means of internal modulation.

3x Fibaro FGT-001

Radiator heads · Z-Wave · Dining room / Bathroom / Toilet

Per-zone flow regulation to the radiator. Own temperature sensor, own schedule. When the "toilet 18 °C" is met, the head closes the valve, even if the boiler is still running. Battery powered, replace ~annually. Bedroom + open space (Kitchen/Dining room) go without TRV — heated by diffusion through open spaces.

Shelly Pro 3EM Triphase LIVE

3-phase meter · CT clamps · Wi-Fi · from 12/05/2026

Real reading of kWh from the three phases of the boiler via HTTP polling on 192.168.1.xxx. Complements the runtime-based estimate (the boiler internally modulates 3 / 6 / 9 kW according to the water temperature, the runtime itself achieves ~95% accuracy). 3EM gives the exact actual consumption without an estimate.

For the tech-savvy

What's going on under the hood

Scripts, hysteresis, AI layers and safety guards — for those who want detail.

Three scripts that control the heating
  • sh_heating_scheduler_v1 (cron 5 min) — reads JSON schedule per zone, finds current slot according to weekday + hour:min, applies preset (home / away / boost / override) and three AI layers. He writes sh_heating_zone_<z>_target_temp.
  • sh_heating_demand_v1 (cron 2 min) — compares current vs. target of each zone, decides on/off with anti-cycling guards. He writes down sh_heating_boiler_request and increments runtime_today_s for kWh estimate.
  • sh_heating_boiler_executor_v1 (trigger: boiler_request changed) — setCapability on FGS-214. If the device is unavailable, it goes into simulation mode (only logging, no physical switching).
State-aware hysteresis (why the boiler does not beat every 2 min)

Naive thermostat: turn on when curr < target, turn off when curr > target. Consequence: an on/off cycle every two minutes. Instead:

  • When the boiler is OFF: turns on only when curr < target − hyst_cold (default 0.5 °C)
  • When the boiler is ON: turns off only when curr ≥ target + hyst_warm (default 0.1 °C)
  • The resulting swing: for a target of 22 °C realistically 21.5–22.1 °C (0.6 °C band)
  • Anti-cycling guards: min runtime 5 min, min off-time 3 min
  • Safety cap: max runtime 4 h at a time → forced off + alert
  • Anti-freeze emergency: any zone < 7 °C → forced on, override all other rules
AI layers (preWakeBoost, weatherAdjust, occupancyBonus)

Three opt-in layers above the baseline schedule. Each returns a delta in °C, adding up to a global cap of ±3 °C. Input for layers is cached ctx (weather, presence, alarm time, sleep state).

  • Layer 1 for WakeBoost: bathroom +2 °C in the window 5–30 min before the alarm clock, toilet +1 °C in the window 5–15 min. Alarm time from cfg (sh_cfg_heating_wake_weekday_min = 195 = 03:15, _weekend_min = 315 = 05:15).
  • Layer 2 weatherAdjust: outdoor < -5 °C → +1 °C, outdoor > 15 °C → -0.5 °C. Reads from global ctx (Open Weather Map, refresh every 15 min).
  • Layer 3 occupancyBonus: active zone (motion age < 600 s) → +0.5 °C. Map: dining room ↔ open_space, bedroom ↔ bedroom, bathroom ↔ bathroom, toilet ↔ toilet.

Master switch sh_cfg_heating_ai_enabled = 'yes' (default no, backward compatible).

kWh estimate (runtime × power consumption)

Demand cron increments every 2 minutes sh_heating_runtime_today_s += 120 s when boiler_state = on . Midnight reset copies the runtime to runtime_yesterday_s and resets the today counter.

kwh_today = (runtime_today_s / 3600) × kotel_kw. Accuracy ~95%, because the boiler internally modulates stage 3 / 6 / 9 kW according to the water temperature. The real measurement runs parallel through Shelly Pro 3EM Triphase (HTTP polling on 192.168.1.xxx, CT clamps on 3 phases in the switchboard, live from 12/05/2026).

41 variables in four groups
  • Masters (7): mode, preset, boiler_state, boiler_request, boiler_since_ts, demand_count, last_scheduler_ts
  • Runtime (3): runtime_today_s, runtime_total_s, runtime_reset_date
  • Configuration (12): hysteresis (cold / warm), anti-cycling (min runtime / off-time), safety cap (max runtime), AI toggles, defaults for away preset, …
  • Per zone × 4 (24): enabled, current_temp, target_temp, manual_override_temp, manual_override_until_ts, schedule (JSON)
