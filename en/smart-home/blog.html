2026-10-29 PERF

Roller blind 7x in 100 minutes → 2x in the model

On a sunny morning, the blind alternated between shading and opening seven times in about 100 minutes, usually right after the 15-minute protection expires. Data from 1,337 router rows showed why: lux varies between adjacent samples by up to 26,000 lx per minute and the median of the short swing is about 3,600 lx. Thus, the hysteresis of 4000 lx corresponded to normal noise, not state separation. Playing the same day with different parameters: 20,000 lx and 45 minutes will give 2 changes, as well as an hour, but with a shorter limitation of common reactions. The threshold for shielding (30,000 lx) remained. The change has taken place standard Config Center form including validation and history, no code intervention. The live router used the new interval immediately (roleta_anticyclo_suppressed with 2,700 s). The long-term reduction in the number of movements is so far WATCH, replay is a guess, not a proof.

2026-06-15 FEATURE

Blind as a privacy shield — privacy-light latch + anti-cycling (3 layers)

When it's light outside, but it's bright at home and it's dark inside, you can see beautifully into the apartment from the street. The new logic solves it by itself — pulls down the blind and turns on the light. Roller shutter router (sh_jidelna_roleta_router_v2, sha 675a427f) has now three layers. (1) pure-lux: lux<200 close, >300 open, in between deadband 200-300 hold. (2) privacy-light latch: it only starts when the lights are off (to measure pure daylight, not the bulbs themselves — anti-feedback), when the blind would otherwise be up, it's dark inside (<18 lux) and I'm home and awake → blind all the way down + the scene lights up. End of latch: lights out (90s grace) / dark outside / exit / sleep / manual intervention / fuse 4 h. (3) anti-cycling: after each movement, a 15-minute lock on light movements, so that passing clouds do not sway the blind — privacy, open window, arrival and change of presence will pass immediately. The first attempt (v1) was rolled back: I put auto-closing in the light router, but then two regulators were fighting over the blind and oscillation occurred. Returned to 81c1d1c3 and overwritten as the only controller in the roller shutter router. All sandbox-first (new test scenarios for latch and anti-cycling PASS before live), debuggable in Config Center without code (sh_cfg_lux_room_dark=18, sh_cfg_roleta_min_move_interval_s=900).

2026-05-19 FIX

SIGABRT every 5 minutes — group concurrency, not a faulty script

Four days of memory, library and logging audits. Deployed forensic patch on 80 hot-path scripts (removal Promise.race + wait() from inline fetchWithTimeout). The crash cycle continued. Only when the user visually announced "it falls exactly every 5 minutes", the cause began to converge. It wasn't one bad script — it was group concurrent load: 17 Advanced Flow with trigger cron:every_nth {n:5, type:minute} started HomeyScript synchronously at the 00s 5min boundary, the runtime could not handle the concurrency. Emergency 5-min isolation (turning off the whole /5min HomeyScript group) stopped the crashes — crashedCount +0 in 12 minutes. Then R1 restored 3 critical flows with 30/60/90s delay (Heating, Presence Failsafe, Brain Guardian). R1B spread 8 light flows over delays 5-195s. R1C reduced the cadence of the 3 hardest to /10m (Device Availability, Health Monitor, OpenSpace Autoheal) — mem peak dropped from 235 MB to ~145 MB median (−38%). ENABLE-ALL sequential returned the remaining 6 heavy flows (Dashboard, Health Alert, Context Full, Diagnostic, Meta, AI Guard) to /15m or /30m with offset 215–320s, 1 PUT per flow + real fire verification + 10 min observation window. Three hours, 6 steps, 0 crashes during deployment. No HomeyScript PUT, no business logic affected, no manual restart. Key Lesson: before a deeper crash analysis, first calculate the intervals between crashes — regular interval = trigger-driven cause.
