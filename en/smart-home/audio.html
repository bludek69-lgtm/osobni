🏠 Experience · Audio and TTS

Music and voice messages

I direct music and voice announcements to specific rooms. Morning briefings have their place and time; at night the household is to remain quiet. Below I also describe the rules that prevent accidental replay.

Technical details

Three speakers, four modes, one orchestrator. TTS goes via Google Cast with a hard limit of 200 characters per utterance — that's why the morning briefing is divided into two phases. Sleep guard silence at night. Prev_playing guard to prevent the radio station from the previous routine from playing in the empty bathroom after the TTS.

What does it do?

Audio that knows when to be silent

Three speakers in the apartment — kitchen, bathroom, bedroom. All Google Cast, all controlled via sh_audio_brain_v2 (dispatcher) a sh_tts_orchestrator_v1 (speech). Central audio_request variable — anyone can request radio or TTS, the brain decides which speaker and in which order.

Google Cast has a hard limit of 200 characters per utterance. Longer text is cut off in the middle of a sentence, the user hears "...maybe you already t". Therefore, in sayTTS there is a defensive truncation and the morning briefing is divided into two phases — the first at 3:15 (time, weather, holiday, up to 150 characters), the second 15 min later (BTC, PM2.5, humidity, funny comment, up to 170 characters).

It has audio priority 100 v priority engine — when TTS is running, lights (priority 60) and other automations will not override it. After completing the TTS, the priorities are released and the previous radio can be summarized (only if prev_playing='yes' — guard po bathroom misroute bug).

Daily operation

How the audio reacts during the day

It's quiet most of the day. Events are ritualistic — briefing in the morning, maybe a comfort suggest in the evening.

  1. 03:15

    Phase 1 Briefing

    15 minutes before the alarm clock. Greeting, time, date, weather today, holiday of the day. 150 character limit. Kitchen speaker (where coffee is prepared in the morning). Once completed, a 15 minute countdown to phase 2 will begin.

  2. 03:30

    Phase 2 Briefing

    BTC rate, PM2.5 outside and inside, humidity, funny comment from Gemini. 170 character limit. Same speaker. The goal: finish the morning briefing before I leave for the car at 03:45.

  3. Day

    Idle, waiting

    sh_audio_request='idle'. No automatic audio during the day. Speakers work as TTS recipients for alerts, otherwise silence. The AI ​​coordinator is monitoring the situation, not triggering anything yet.

  4. Evening

    Comfort suggest (maybe)

    AI coordinator can evaluate "evening + idle scene" → comfort_suggest. TTS reads the suggestion: "Do you want to start a relaxation scene?". Dispatch radio only if the user explicitly accepts via Cube or Voice (no timeout fallback).

  5. Night

    Sleep silent

    sh_spim='yes' → priority engine blocks TTS. Critical alerts (flooding, fire) go through their own safety bypass. Comfort notifications are waiting for you to wake up.

Scenes

Five actions, five roles

Most are triggered by the system, not user input.

Morning briefing

Two phases, 15 min apart. Limit 150/170 chars. TV mute before, unmute after. Speaker = kitchen (where coffee is being prepared). Gemini generates, sayTTS truncates to 200 max.

Comfort suggested

AI coordinator will evaluate level 4 situation in the evening → suggest. Without user accept (cube/voice) NOT auto-execute — that was a bathroom misroute bug.

Bathroom radio

Motion in the bathroom + TOD in the morning → starts the radio (Italia Classic Hits, Sanremo feed). Stop at motion_off + 30s timeout. Resume guard checks prev_playing='yes'.

Sleep silent

Sleep state → day priorities for TTS. Critical alerts (gas, water leak) have a safety bypass. Briefing 03:15 goes normally (sleep state already unset).

Resume after TTS

If the radio was playing before the TTS (prev_playing='yes', prev_mode='radio_*'), it will resume after the TTS is finished. Without the prev_playing guard, it would be summarized even if nothing was playing.

For the tech-savvy

What's going on under the hood

Orchestrator, dispatcher, priority engine, prev_playing guard.

200 char Cast limit + briefing split

The Google Cast SDK has a hard limit of 200 characters per utterance. Exceeds → cast stream crashes or truncates mid-sentence. It's NOT a bug, it's a safety net v sh_gemini_brain_v1::sayTTS(): 

if (cleaned.length > 200) {
  cleaned = cleaned.substring(0, 200);
  logMsg('GOOGLE CAST: message truncated to 200 chars');
}

Briefing split into two phases:

  • briefing (3:15) — greeting, time, date, weather, holiday (max 150 chars)
  • briefing2 (+15 min) — BTC, PM2.5, humidity, funny comment (max 170 chars)

Orchestration via flow "SH – Morning – Briefing 2" with 15 min delay. Pending var: sh_morning_briefing2_pending_ts.

Audio brain + TTS orchestrator
  • sh_tts_orchestrator_v1 — issues a TTS request, waits for completion, emits tts_completed event They hold sh_priority_active='tts' for the duration of the speech.
  • sh_audio_brain_v2 — dispatcher for non-TTS audio (radio, music, ambient). He reads sh_audio_request, the speaker decides according to the active zone and TOD. It emits audio_dispatch event
  • sh_tts_resume_exec_v1 — after TTS is finished, it decides whether to summarize the previous audio. Guards on prev_playing='yes' (after bathroom misroute fix v1.8).
Priority engine (TTS=100, light=60)

Central Arbiter. Each request has a priority, the higher one wins. When TTS active (priority 100), light request (60) postponed. Table:

  • 100 — TTS (briefing, alerts, voice response)
  • 80 — Critical alerts (water leak, gas, fire)
  • 60 — Light request
  • 50 — Audio comfort (radio, music)
  • 30 — Background ambient

Stuck recovery: when active='tts' > 1 min without forward progress, brain guardian resets to none/idle.

Prev_playing guard (post bathroom misroute)

Bug 25.4. night: after comfort_suggest TTS, ~46 s later the bathroom radio started by itself. Root causes: sh_tts_resume_exec_v1 fallback if (target === 'idle' && currentMode === 'radio_bathroom') summarized without verifying that something actually played before TTS.

sh_audio_current_mode was stuck on radio_bathroom from the morning routine. The resume condition was met even if the speaker had been silent for a long time.

Fix v1.8: added guard if (sh_audio_prev_playing !== 'yes') return skip. Resume only when the radio actually played before TTS.

TV mute / unmute pipeline

Before each TTS:

  1. Read TV state (Android TV remote, IP 192.168.1.xxx)
  2. If volume > 0 → setCapability volume_mute = true, save original volume
  3. Starts TTS
  4. Mon tts_completed: setCap volume_mute = false, restore volume

Without this, the TTS voice would compete with the TV sound — the morning news + briefing could not be heard. Capabilities volume_mute is toggle, no need to wait for driver ack.
