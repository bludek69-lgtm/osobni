Morning in 3:15

An example of a working morning with getting behind the wheel: preparing the heating in the bathroom, gradual light, radio and coffee. The process is detailed step-by-step, including checks that determine whether I am really getting up.

Technical details

A minute-by-minute timeline of Monday morning as the house plays itself out. It starts with a pre-wake boost of the heating in the bathroom half an hour before the alarm clock, and ends with going to the car. No classic alarm clock, no mobile app touches, no manually triggered scenes. Only subsystems that themselves know what and when. (The times in the sample below are illustrative.)

Context

Monday, January, -3 °C outside

The date: Monday in January 2026. Outside: -3 °C, humidity 85%, moderate easterly wind. In the house: night slot schedule (dining room 18 °C, bedroom 17 °C, bathroom 19 °C), sleep state active from evening, blind down to 0.1.

User target: get up very early in the morning (working day) and leave for the morning shift shortly after. No time to struggle with technology, no tolerance for "it didn't work for me this morning". This was the tester of the whole smart home development — it simply has to work.

The following timeline is real behavior of the system as EventLog records it — not a plan, not an ideal, real data from the last business Monday of the year.

Timeline

85 minutes of autonomous operation

Each roll in the EventLog corresponds to one line here.

  1. 02:45:00

    Heating layer 1 turns on the pre-wake boost

    cron scheduler: preWakeBoost AI layer detects alarm time = 03:15 (MON-FRI), 30 min before it. Zone bathroom target_temp from 19 → 21 °C (+2 °C delta). Toilet 18 → 19 °C (+1 °C, window 5-15 min, not yet started).

  2. 02:47:30

    Demand cron switches the boiler on

    Bathroom current 19.2 °C, target 21 °C, delta = 1.8 °C > hyst_cold (0.5). boiler_request='on', executor setCap FGS-214. The Z-Wave relay clicks, the boiler starts the 6 kW stage. The other zones are still within hysteresis.

  3. 03:00:00

    Toilet layer 1 starts

    Second preWakeBoost slot: toilet from 18 → 19 °C, window 15 min until alarm time. Demand cron evaluates the next tick (03:01:00), there is no demand yet (delta < 0.5).

  4. 03:14:55

    Alarm fires (-5 s buffer)

    Mobile alarm time 03:15, Homey flow "SH – Alarm – Pre-fire" trigger 5 s before. sh_alarm_pending='yes', sh_morning_brain prepares: ensureHeatingMorningMode (preset boost bathroom to 23 °C 60 min), ensureAudioMorningMode (TV mute prep).

  5. 03:15:00

    Briefing phase 1 starts

    Gemini brain generates text: greeting, time, date, weather, holiday. Defensive truncation to 150 characters (Cast limit 200). TV mute (Android TV remote setCap volume_mute=true). The kitchen speaker will start TTS.

  6. 03:15:08

    Briefing 1 ends, sleep_state unset

    TTS completed, tts_completed event TV unmute (volume restore). sh_spim='no' (sleep gate released). Schedule next stage briefing 2 at 03:30 (delay 15 min due to pending var).

  7. 03:16:30

    Got up kitchen motion

    PIR Fibaro detects movement in the kitchen. Motion-on event → flow → light_router with priority engine check. Sleep guard already unset, lux below the threshold (darkness) → kitchen dim 0.4 (morning soft profile). FP2 in open space is waiting for confirmation.

  8. 03:17:00

    FP2 presence confirmed

    FP2 detects a sitting/standing person in open space → alarm_presence=true. Open space router: lux check, privacy guard (roller down, OK), sleep guard unset → Sector dim 0.5, Dining table 0.3 warm.

  9. 03:18:20

    Coffee trigger (maybe)

    Plug measure_power of the coffee machine from 0 → 800 W → flow logger records the appliance on. No action yet, just passive monitoring. (Planned: trigger for sound suggest "Monday music?".)

  10. 03:30:00

    Phase 2 Briefing

    Pending timestamp expiration → flow "SH – Morning – Briefing 2". Gemini generates part 2: BTC exchange rate, PM2.5 (-1 / 8 µg/m³), humidity 45%, funny commentary. Truncation 170 chars. Kitchen speaker, TV mute again → TTS → unmute.

  11. 03:35:00

    Bathroom motion start

    Motion in the bathroom → bathroom router (own pipeline). Lux below the threshold + TOD in the morning → bathroom ceiling dim 0.8 (bright morning). Sanremo radio starts on the bathroom speaker (Italian Classic Hits).

  12. 03:35:05

    Heating boost bathroom 23 °C

    ensureHeatingMorningMode applied boost preset → bathroom target = 23 °C (config: sh_cfg_heating_morning_bath_boost_temp). Demand cron next tick 03:36 boiler_request='on' (the boiler has been running since 02:47, only the target is different).

  13. 03:50:00

    Shower ends, motion off

    Motion sensor in the bathroom 30 s without movement → motion-off event. Bathroom router waits another 30 s grace period, then Sanremo radio stop. Ceiling dim ramp from 0.8 → 0 in 5 s. Heating boost still active (60 min countdown from 03:14).

  14. 03:55:00

    Breakfast, full kitchen

    Motion in the kitchen continuous, lux still below the threshold → kitchen dim ramp 0.4 → 0.8 (brighter profile for breakfast). FP2 still has a presence → open space lights hold. Heating: dining room current 18.5, target 21 (schedule comfort from 5:00 is already running).

  15. 04:08:00

    Entering the hall

    Motion in the hall → way-finding light auto-on (60 s timer). Front door opening → door_contact event. sh_departure_pending='yes' flag, waiting for geofence exit confirm.

  16. 04:09:30

    Exit, geofence exit

    Phone GPS has left the home zone (radius 100 m). Multi-signal confirm — geofence_exit + door_contact open + 0 motion in 5 min window. Probability of leaving = high.

  17. 04:10:00

    Away preset cascade

    Multi-signal confirm fulfilled → sh_rezim_doma='pryc'. Cascade: all heating zones → preset away (16 °C target). Audio brain → idle silent. Light router → blocked all non-essential. Robot vacuum cleaner → unblocked (can start daily cleaning).

  18. 04:10:15

    Hall timeout off

    Motion in the hall last at 04:09, 60 s timer expired → way-finding light off. The house is quiet, dark, the boiler is running on low (16 °C target, a large delta from the current 19 °C → off to hysteresis).

Post mortem

What worked, what could have gone wrong

Which worked without hesitation
  • Pre-wake boost timing — 30 minutes in advance was enough, bathroom at 03:15 = 21 °C
  • Briefing split — 200 char limit držel, ani jedna fáze nebyla truncated mid-věty
  • TV mute pipeline — synchronization with TTS without race condition
  • Light routing — sleep guard unset on time, dim profile morning gentle
  • Multi-signal departure — without false positives (neighbors behind the wall at 4:00 a.m. = did not report)
What could go wrong (and has gone wrong in the past)
  • Stuck priority='tts' — if the TTS pipeline did not throw the tts_completed event, the light router would remain blocked. Brain Guardian would detect after 1 min. It actually happened on 22.4. — fix in guardian v1.1.
  • SNZB-04 deep-sleep — if the door/window contact fell to unreachable, the departure pipeline would lose the door_contact signal. Multi-signal would not confirm, away preset would not come. It happened on 23.4. — Aqara P2 replacement planned.
  • Cold-start bug — if the anti-cycling guard did not allow the heating to switch on (sinceTs=0 → forever cooldown), the pre-wake boost would not have taken place. It actually happened on 21.4. — fix demand v1.2.
  • UUID re-pair orphan — if, after replacing the battery, the sensor received a new UUID and the flow trigger cards remained on the old, silent fail. It actually happened on 4/26. (Door/Window Sensor Dining Room1) — bulk swap fix.
  • Ghost var sh_audio_current_mode='radio_bathroom' — after an incomplete stop, audio_brain could start bathroom radio even without user. It actually happened on 4/25. — prev_playing guard fix v1.8.
What's Not Here (Phase E Plan)
  • Voice intent — option to say "give me briefing 2 now" instead of cube
  • Ambient morning music — add light background music instead of TTS-only briefing
  • Proactive smart suggestion — "it's -10°C outside today, take a jacket"
  • Coffee level monitoring — the plug power curve detects when the coffee is ready
