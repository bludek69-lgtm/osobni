Watermills - authors and licenses

The main volume uses 72 photographs and one painting. The photos included in the Walsch Mill Short and both backgrounds for the miniatures are marked with individual shots. In the main part, the documents were edited for the 16:9 format, with movement, blending and captions. CC BY-SA photo edits are shared under the same license version as the relevant entry.
