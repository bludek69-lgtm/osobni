Krkonoše huts

As long as there is someone to open — photo authors, music and sources

Short's new vertical thumbnail

The miniature from November 17, 2026 uses the real Voská bouda over the entire area of 9:16, corresponding to the name Krkonošská bouda. It replaces the older miniature with a mound and empty lanes. Photos Vosecka bouda 2015.jpg, the author SkywalkerPL, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0. Actual edits: vertical cutout, technical reduction, dark transparent gradient, title and Czech color theme. No retouching, generation or finishing of photographic details. The original photo remains unchanged; thumbnail graphics are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Short himself continues to narrate the two monuments, and his footage is not altered by this miniature swap.

Image version and optional subtitles

Fixed November 17, 2026 V6 does not have voiceover transcription hard-coded into the image. Czech speech subtitles are a separate track that can be switched off. Brief names, factual designations of the place and resolution of the archival or contextual image remain. The older V5 version also included in-picture speech captions; earlier mentions of this mod below refer to V5. Motion, blending, photo selection, timing, and commentary with music have all been retained in V6.

Full sources of the main document, the related Short, and both thumbnails. This page does not contain a player or link to private videos.
