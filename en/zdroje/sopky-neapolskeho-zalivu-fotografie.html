Information sources for shots

  • Vesuvius National Park: The shape of a volcano is the result of repeated eruptions; lavas and pyroclastic deposits including ash and rock fragments. The eruption of 79 destroyed Pompeii.
  • Vesuvius National Park: The Gran Cono trail follows young volcanic deposits: ash, lapilli and small lava flows; views into the crater.
  • Soprintendenza Pompei, guide 2015: The burial of a city by ash and lapilli during an eruption; quadriportico and small theater; large containers embedded in the brick counters of the thermopolies.
